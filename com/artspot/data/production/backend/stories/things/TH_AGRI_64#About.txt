
Sikkim Large Cardamom truly owes its name to its extraordinary flavour, derived from *Elettaria cardamomum*, albeit the significantly larger berries native to the beautiful Himalayan state of Sikkim in India.

This iconic Indian spice stands out due to its bigger, teardrop-shaped green or dark brown pods, which can house anywhere between 15-20 seeds compared to common small cardamom's typical three. It forms the cornerstone for much contemporary Indian single-origin cards, celebrated for its sharp, woody aroma and vibrant citrus notes.

Predominantly cultivated in Sikkim, especially around regions like Kalpa, Pelling, North Sikkim's Askot valley, by both small and large farming families, this variety thrives best at elevations above 1500 meters. Its distinctive smoky, woody undertones coupled with sweet notes differentiate it from similar spices found elsewhere.

Beyond culinary uses – adding unbroken pods to sambar or using powder for masala chai while broken seeds enhance biryani and garam masala recipes – it's integral to Ayurveda and renowned globally. Key characteristics include the naturally dark colour of large pods (often appearing scorched) and their potent flavour upon crushing.

Sikkim Large Cardamom offers a unique, often spicier, aromatic alternative beloved for its robust taste and suitability in various global cuisines that appreciate bold spices.
