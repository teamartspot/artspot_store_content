
Known today primarily for its abundant sunflowers, the regions of Dahanu Ranges and Gholvad in Maharashtra were historically renowned as major orchards dedicated to the cultivation of Chikoo mangoes. These areas, straddling the Nanded district towards the west and parts extending into Latur district, formed a significant cluster crucial for this specific variety.

Originating from cultivars likely brought by laborers, farmers, or traders from regions like Sindhudurg where the famous Girnar 'chikoos' were grown, their widespread adoption is believed to date back several decades. While not definitively documented in ancient texts, these chikoo groves became intrinsically linked to local agricultural identity around Maharashtra's Western Ghats foothills.
Unlike many high-value speciality cultivars, Dahanu-Gholvad chikoo does not have an extremely long documented pedigree compared to globally famous types like the Sharbat Aam or Kalpataru. However, its regional prominence grew gradually starting from the late 20th century following commercial expansion of home-based cultivation in these particular taluka borderlands. Some community elders suggest roots stretching back further but concur this is likely tied to development post-1985 onwards.

What distinguishes Dahan (Ghotel) chikoo? It typically ripens earlier than its more celebrated Sindhudurg cousin, perhaps July-August versus August-September. Its fruit shape varies locally but often resembles the long-culured South Indian type though likely smaller and broader for easier transport to local markets which were the historical basis for commercial expansion via home-based cultivation.

Over time, Dahanu-Gholvad chikoo became one of Maharashtra's distinct mango types, valued regionally despite needing more space between trees for optimal growth compared to high-density plantations. Its lineage points towards Girnar 'Dussehri', but through local adaptation and cross-pollination resulted in this unique type.

Locally, its name evolved simply from referring to the fruit when discussing production in these two regions: one could say "I bought chikoo near Dahanu / Gholvad" meaning a mango grown there. The origin lies within Maharashtra's Western borderlands between Nanded district and Latur but historically focused around specific villages known for home-grown early variety 'Dussehri' style chikoo fruit.
