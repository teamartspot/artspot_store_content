
Originating from the northeastern state of Tripura, India, the "Queen" pineapple gained significant regional recognition perhaps partly ironically for its perceived dominance over other Assamese varieties like Ananta. This specific cultivar stands out due to unique cultivation practices in areas like Sehore under Udaipur SIDC and parts of North Tripura's Gaya district.

The lineage is debated, but the key lies not just in genetics alone, but perhaps also regional adaptation and superior care during propagation. Unlike most tropical pineapples thriving best in distinct regions globally for commercial success – often associated with brands from Costa Rica or the Philippines alongside traditional Indian cultivars known mainly to domestic markets – Tripura Queen represents a notable deviation.

Its fame surged because these pineapples matured exceptionally well under North-eastern climate conditions, yielding fruits comparable in quality and size elsewhere. Marketed as 'Tripura Queen', they became synonymous with premium value amidst established rivals. The variety's success underscores Tripura’s capability to adapt tropical crops effectively even outside the state, highlighting a unique agricultural achievement despite niche conditions usually requiring precise matches for ideal growth.

Today, Tripura Queen pineapple is exported widely across India and internationally from its home base in Melghat, showcasing consistent excellence developed through specific local techniques achieved under these challenging Northeastern conditions. It offers consumers worldwide high-grade pineapple flavor with the added appreciation of its unique Terai origin story.
