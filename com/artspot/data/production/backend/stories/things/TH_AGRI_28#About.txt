
Often hailed as India's premier spice region, Coorg is particularly renowned for its exceptional green cardamom. This isn't just another type, but represents *pure* cardamom in its most fundamental form – the unprocessed seed pods directly harvested from plants grown on Coorg's fertile lands.

Coorg green cardamom distinguishes itself through a unique blend of three shades: vibrant black, deep mahogany red, and bright emerald green. This colour spectrum ensures a complex aroma profile when ground, offering notes unavailable in dried-only forms. The defining characteristic is the abundant presence of the distinctively large lime-green pods.

These whole, fresh green pods aren't just aesthetic; they contain significantly more essential oils than their smaller counterparts. When gently crushed or lightly toasted directly before grinding (a critical step!), these vibrant pods release a truly potent and characteristic fragrance – that signature 'sparkle' so prized in aromatic coffee blends like Earl Grey, masala chai, or the classic Viennese.

Brewers cherish Coorg green cardamom for providing robust flavor without the need to source expensive miniature pods directly from farms. Its availability as bulk whole green leaves ensures freshness and maximum aroma yield when freshly processed just prior to use.
