
Wayanad Jeerakasala rice, proudly cultivated in India's ecological haven of Wayanad, Uttara Kannada stands apart as a unique variety with exceptional aroma and flavour profile. Like all landrace Basmatis produced here since generations, it is also naturally resistant to Black Chaff disease (Bacterial blight) and high yielding even without resorting to biotechnology.

Its golden hue, amplified by processing alongside turmeric residue ("Sura Kalika"), gives the rice grains a characteristic amber colour. This distinctive visual quality, combined with its rich, nutty fragrance evoking ginger and cardamom notes and slightly sweet taste, makes Wayanad Jeerakasala rice instantly identifiable.

This aromatic rice offers more than just culinary pleasure; it is believed to have certain specific health benefits due to the turmeric link during processing. Its pleasant flavour softens upon cooking without losing its signature long, slender, fluffier grains typical of Basmatis when well-cooked. Known for its ease in cooking perfectly fluffy and crisp florets, Wayanad Jeerakasala rice is prized in both home kitchens and hotels.

It connects to the traditional "Sura Kalika" varieties cultivated locally using organic farming methods near turmeric fields since generations ago. This heritage aspect adds to its appeal, making Wayanad's Amber Rice not just a staple food grain but a special product embodying local biodiversity and culinary distinction.
