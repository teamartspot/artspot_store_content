
Known locally as **Borlade**, the Virupakshi Hill Banana quickly became famous in and around Tiruvannamalai. Revered by some and debated over its origins by others, it's a variety grown specifically in the hilly region surrounding the ancient Virupaksha temple complex here.

Its fame rests not just on cultivation but deeply intertwined with local culture and beliefs. Market vendors sell small bundles near temple gates, often claiming unique qualities tied to their sacred origin – perhaps enhanced taste or perceived medicinal value (though such claims should be verified).

The banana itself is medium-sized when picked green for transport; the creamy-yellow 'Dhapal' at the core possesses a rich texture famously absent in many imported bananas reaching Indian shores. Locals believe taking Virupakshi Hill Bananas contributes to good health and prosperity.

While some insist it's entirely local cultivation tied to divine grace, others note its resemblance to Cavendish types but with distinct flavour notes – sweeter perhaps, or more buttery.

Its status transcends mere produce; there are even pilgrims visiting from afar specifically for this banana. Cultivated in fields often near temples and nestled within scenic hills, it offers more than just sustenance, providing a sensory pleasure enjoyed locally across Tamil Nadu for generations.
