
Allahabad Surkhya Guava (often called Sikkim Purple or Pink-skin Guava outside India), hails from the pristine highlands of the Himalayan state, Sikkim. Known as **Bhutani Guava** locally, its fame underscores the exceptional quality nurtured at high altitudes.

This variety is readily distinguished by its mesmerizingly deep purple skin, almost black in some instances. Beneath this striking exterior lies exceptionally creamy, soft flesh that offers very little fibrous material.

The flavor profile of Allahabad Surkhya Guava is truly remarkable – a rich, complex sweetness with pronounced floral undertones and subtle hints reminiscent of muskmelon (hence the occasional alternative name reference). Its taste is often described as sweeter and more aromatic than common green guavas.

Botanically known as *Psidium cattleyanum*, this specific cultivar thrives best in cool, elevated regions. Sikkim provides these ideal conditions, yielding fruit that possesses a balanced level of tartness or acidity relative to its intense sweetness. The flesh is dense and holds up well post-harvest compared to many other purple varieties.

Its striking appearance presents an aesthetic appeal beyond the taste sensation. This unique color makes it highly attractive for decorative purposes – often adorning fruit displays, enhancing festive meals visually, and adding a vibrant touch to jams or preserves even before eating.

Naturally grown in such ecologically sensitive zones gives consumers added confidence regarding pesticide residues levels. Its rich taste profile encourages people seeking healthy fruits without artificial enhancements. Its popularity continues growing among health-conscious individuals globally due its potent goodness packed into an exquisite form.
