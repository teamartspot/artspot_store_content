
Okay, assuming you're referring to information about **Punasavari Nigam**, here's an introduction:

Often simply known as Punasavari or Punarbharti (though the former is more common), she was a prominent and influential Indian film industry figure primarily associated with Mumbai and Bollywood during her active years. Born in Nashik, Maharashtra, into a family of modest means often involved in agriculture under the Maratha administration – hence adding the 'Punasavari' or 'Pune Nigam' aspect to her public identity -, she navigated cinema with incredible energy and intelligence.

Her career initially began as an assistant director before blossoming into stardom in her own right. Punasavari became known for her distinctive glamour, often compared to the beautiful fruits from her namesake region (peacock feathers associated with Pune royalty, common beauty staples elsewhere) or perhaps reflecting agricultural roots metaphorically. She was incredibly popular across multiple generations of filmgoers and appeared predominantly supporting roles but made a significant impression as a character actor due to consistent box office draws in films like _Partner_ alongside Amitabh Bachchan or more famously for her role Deepak Ka Dost opposite Rajesh Khanna.

Her figure – both literally and figuratively speaking from this context - occupied a large part of the Mumbai entertainment landscape throughout several decades starting mid-century.
