MJ Grain Products Pvt. Ltd., established in 2008, has grown into North Bengal’s largest manufacturer of parboiled rice and a prominent supplier of premium Basmati rice in India and international markets. The company built its reputation through trusted in-house brands such as RANJEET, RANGEET, SWABHUMI, and JEEVANMOTI. Their journey focuses on delivering quality, consistency, and authenticity in every grain while expanding globally. Alongside business growth, they emphasize sustainable agricultural practices and community empowerment. With multiple production locations and strong leadership, the company positions itself as a trusted name in the rice industry.
