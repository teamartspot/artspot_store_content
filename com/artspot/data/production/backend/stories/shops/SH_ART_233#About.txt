Sree Payyannur Pavithram Sree Payyannur Pavithram is the registered maker of the Payyannur Pavithra Mothiram, a traditional sacred ring originally crafted from darbha grass and later rendered in gold and silver for permanent use. The organisation manufactures Pavithra Mothiram rings and related jewellery items, combining ritual knowledge with metalwork for devotees and collectors. They provide certification of authenticity, product documentation and maintain an online presence for orders and enquiries.
