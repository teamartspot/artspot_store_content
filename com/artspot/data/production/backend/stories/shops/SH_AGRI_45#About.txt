From the renowned chilli fields of Byadgi, Karnataka, Angadi Enterprises brings the vibrant character of India’s finest red chillies to kitchens and industries around the world. Established in 2018 under the guidance of Mr. Jishan Ali Angadi, the company was built on a simple yet powerful promise: authentic produce, sourced directly from farmers and delivered with uncompromising quality.
Each chilli is carefully selected, hygienically dried, and rigorously tested to preserve its deep color, rich aroma, and distinctive flavor. Combining trusted agricultural relationships with modern quality assurance processes such as sterilization and precision packing, Angadi Enterprises ensures consistency and purity in every shipment.
Rooted in respect for farmers and driven by a commitment to excellence, Angadi Enterprises stands as a trusted partner in the global spice trade, bringing the bold spirit of Byadgi chillies to the world with reliability and pride.
