Ms Silk Saree is an online and physical retailer focused on Banarasi and designer silk sarees.
The brand stocks a variety of Banarasi fabrics including Katan, Kora Organza and designer weaves.
They present collections suited for bridal, festive and formal wear and also supply to wholesalers.
Ms Silk Saree maintains an online product catalogue and accepts orders for retail and trade.
