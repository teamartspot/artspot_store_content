The legacy of Sarvaangi is deeply rooted in the cultural heritage of Yeola, the birthplace of the iconic Paithani saree. The craft was first nurtured by the Bhandge family, whose dedication to excellence spans over 150 years and three generations. What began as a humble weaving practice in the historic town of Yeola has today evolved into one of the most respected names in the world of Paithani.

Embodying grace, tradition, and royalty, the Paithani was once a treasured possession of Maharashtrian nobility and remains a symbol of pride for brides even today. At Sarvaangi, each saree is a tribute to this legacy—handcrafted with meticulous attention to detail, using the finest silks and intricate motifs that have been passed down through generations.
