Palekar Food Products began its journey in 1939, when Dadasaheb Palekar founded a small bakery in Satara with a vision of delivering fresh, high-quality baked goods. Over the decades, the family business grew under the leadership of Govindrao Palekar and later Neelkanth Palekar, expanding its range from breads and khari to cakes, cookies, pastries, and other bakery specialties. As pioneers of bakery products in the Satara region, the company combined homemade recipes with self-developed techniques and modern manufacturing technology. Today, after more than eight decades, Palekar Food continues to blend tradition, innovation, and quality, carrying forward a trusted family legacy while serving customers across Maharashtra and beyond.
