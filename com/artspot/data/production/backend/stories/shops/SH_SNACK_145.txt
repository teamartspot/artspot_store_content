Prashant Corner was founded in 1989 by Mr. Prashant Sakpal in Thane with a simple vision of serving authentic, pure, and delicious Indian sweets and savouries. What began as a small passion-driven venture gradually grew into one of Maharashtra's most trusted sweet brands through an unwavering commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. The company combines time-honored recipes with modern, hygienic, partly automated production facilities to ensure freshness and consistency in every product. Over the years, Prashant Corner has become a preferred destination for festive sweets, namkeen, and snacks, celebrating occasions such as Diwali, Ganesh Chaturthi, and Gudi Padwa. Today, with multiple outlets across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, the brand continues to preserve its legacy of authenticity, hygiene, and exceptional taste while evolving with changing consumer preferences.
