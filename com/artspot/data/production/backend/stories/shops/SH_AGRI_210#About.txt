In the heart of Kolhapur, where jaggery has long been a symbol of tradition, PureMe Organics began with a bold thought to reimagine sweetness through science and sustainability. Founded in 2020 by a mother-daughter duo, the venture was born from a deep understanding of farmers’ challenges and a desire to bridge tradition with modern technology. Each batch of jaggery is not just produced but carefully researched, refined, and innovated to make it healthier, longer-lasting, and easier to use in everyday life. What makes this journey unique is its purpose-driven heart empowering rural farmers through technology while creating products that remain pure and chemical-free. From lab insights to farm fields, every step reflects a blend of knowledge, care, and collaboration. It is not just about offering a natural sweetener, but about transforming an age-old industry into something future-ready. In every grain of jaggery, PureMe carries a story of innovation rooted deeply in tradition, proving that even the simplest sweetness can evolve with time.
