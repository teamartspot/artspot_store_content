In the serene hills of Sikkim, where cooperation shapes progress, SIMFED began its journey in 1983 as a bridge between farmers and markets. Established as an apex marketing federation, it works to carry the efforts of countless growers from fields to larger trade networks. What makes its story unique is its collective spirit uniting cooperative societies to ensure fair supply, better pricing, and wider reach for agricultural produce. From supplying essential goods to managing surplus harvests, it quietly supports both rural livelihoods and everyday needs. Each activity reflects a system built not on individuals, but on shared growth and trust. In every transaction, SIMFED carries the strength of the community, turning cooperation into a force that sustains both farmers and the state’s organic vision.
