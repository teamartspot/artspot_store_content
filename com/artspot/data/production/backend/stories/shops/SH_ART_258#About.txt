Karaikkudi Antiques Market The Karaikkudi Antiques Market in Tamil Nadu is a famous destination for antique lovers and collectors. The market is known for its wide variety of traditional furniture, artefacts, brassware, and Chettinad architectural elements sourced from old mansions. Dealers and shops in the market supply antiques to interior designers, collectors, and visitors. The market is regarded as one of the premier antique hubs in South India.
