Girish Rajsoni Girish Rajsoni is an artisan from Pratapgarh associated with Thewa and traditional jewellery making. His practice includes producing thewa pendants, earrings and small framed pieces that reflect Pratapgarh’s distinctive glass-and-gold enamel work. Rajsoni’s work is sought after by collectors of Rajasthani traditional jewellery and heritage crafts. Girish Rajsoni is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
