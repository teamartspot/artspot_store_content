Mirzapur Art Palace Mirzapur’s carpet trade grew in the 20th century as weaving communities organised into production and export networks; local showrooms such as Mirzapur Art Palace emerged to display finished rugs and to manage wholesale orders. Trade press and cluster reports document Mirzapur’s importance for Indian carpet production and the role of local dealers in connecting artisans to larger markets. Mirzapur Art Palace is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
