Desai Brothers Limited (DBL) was established in 1901 in Gujarat by Shri H. V. Desai, laying the foundation for a business that has grown through 123+ years of heritage and innovation. While rooted in its traditional businesses, DBL expanded into the snacks segment to celebrate India's rich culinary heritage through brands such as Pitaara, Chipseez, Masteez, and Pitaara Mithaas. The company combines authentic Indian flavors with modern food innovation, producing its snacks in FSSC 22000-certified manufacturing facilities to ensure the highest standards of quality and hygiene. With a strong distribution network spanning 14 states, over 300 distributors, and 2.5 lakh retailers, DBL has established itself as a trusted name in the Indian snack industry. Today, the company continues to build on its legacy by blending tradition, quality, and innovation to deliver delicious snacking experiences across the country.
