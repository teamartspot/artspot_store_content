Gurpreet Phulkari Store Gurpreet Phulkari Store (Patiala) is a family‑run emporium specialising in traditional Punjabi Phulkari textiles, including dupattas, shawls and bridal ensembles. The store curates hand‑embroidered pieces worked by local Phulkari artisans, preserving stitch styles such as Bagh, Phulkari and Chhama. Gurpreet sources from regional women’s collectives and individual embroiderers, offering both ready‑made and bespoke pieces for ceremonial wear. The shop is a local landmark in Patiala’s textile market and often receives customers from across Punjab seeking authentic phulkari trousseau items.
