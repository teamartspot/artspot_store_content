Haldiram's was founded in 1937 by Shri Ganga Bhishen Agarwal (Haldiram Ji) in Bikaner, Rajasthan, starting as a small shop selling traditional namkeen and sweets. Driven by a passion for quality and innovation, the brand became famous for its unique Bikaneri Bhujia, which earned widespread popularity across India. Over the decades, Haldiram's expanded from a family-run business into one of India's largest food brands, offering a wide range of snacks, sweets, frozen foods, beverages, and restaurant services. By combining authentic recipes, modern manufacturing, and strict quality standards, the company has built a strong presence in India and international markets. Today, Haldiram's is a globally recognized symbol of Indian taste and tradition, delighting millions of customers across more than 80 countries.
