Rajhans Milk, officially known as the Sangamner Taluka Co-operative Milk Producers and Processing Union, was established on 12 November 1977 by freedom fighter and cooperative leader Late Bhausaheb Santuji Thorat to improve the livelihoods of dairy farmers in the Sangamner region. Built on the principles of cooperation, the union created a strong milk procurement network that ensured fair returns for farmers while promoting scientific dairy practices. Over the years, it expanded from milk collection into the production of value-added dairy products, including cheese, ghee, paneer, shrikhand, milk powder, and packaged drinking water. Today, Rajhans has grown into one of Maharashtra's leading cooperative dairy brands, combining modern processing technology with a continued commitment to farmer welfare, quality, and sustainable rural development.
