In a country where rice is more than just food, Oryzo India began its journey to rediscover grains that time was slowly forgetting. Built on a vision to preserve India’s native rice varieties, it brings together rare, traditional grains that carry the stories of regions, cultures, and generations. Each variety is carefully sourced from local farmers, ensuring that age-old cultivation practices continue to thrive alongside modern innovation. What makes this journey unique is its purpose—not just to sell rice, but to revive what was once on the verge of extinction. Through research, sustainable farming, and small-batch processing, every grain retains its authenticity, nutrition, and natural character. It is a story where heritage meets health, connecting today’s kitchens with the wisdom of the past. With every meal, Oryzo doesn’t just serve rice—it serves a legacy, quietly bringing forgotten traditions back to life.
