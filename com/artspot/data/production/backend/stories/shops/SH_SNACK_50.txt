Parsi Dairy Farm was founded in 1916 by Nariman Ardeshir with a simple vision of delivering pure, fresh, and high-quality dairy products to Mumbai households. What began as a small family-run dairy has grown into one of the city's most iconic and trusted food brands, serving generations of customers. For over a century, the company has remained committed to traditional recipes, premium ingredients, and uncompromising quality in its milk, sweets, kulfis, ghee, and other dairy products. Despite changing times, Parsi Dairy Farm has preserved its heritage while continuously adapting to modern consumer needs. Today, the brand stands as a symbol of trust, purity, and timeless taste, carrying forward a legacy that has delighted Mumbai families for more than 100 years.
