Kutch Bandhani began its journey in 1995 as a humble family-run outlet tucked inside a local mall. Born from a deep appreciation for traditional Indian craftsmanship and a desire to make fashion approachable, the brand steadily gained recognition for its quality and customer-first approach.

By 1998, growing customer trust and demand led to the opening of our first independent store. Since then, we’ve remained rooted in our original values — craftsmanship, inclusivity, and community spirit — while continuing to evolve with the latest trends and needs of our customers.
