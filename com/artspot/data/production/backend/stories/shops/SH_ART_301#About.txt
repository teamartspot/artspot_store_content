Golkonda Handicrafts Emporium is the retail arm of Telangana Handicrafts Development Corporation (TGHDC), showcasing handicrafts, handlooms and artisanal products from across Telangana. The emporium curates Kondapalli toys, Pochampally ikat, Paag and Pochampally textiles, Bidriware and other regional craft specialities through a network of Golkonda outlets and an online storefront. It serves as a platform for small artisans to access urban customers and supports sales, promotion and craft documentation initiatives in the state.
