Halder Venture Limited traces its origins back to 1924 with the establishment of B.C. Halder Rice and Oil Mills by Brindaban Chandra Halder in West Bengal, laying the foundation for a century-old agro business. Over generations, the company expanded from traditional rice milling into edible oils, modern rice processing, and global rice exports. With continuous modernization, public listing, and expansion into multiple international markets, the business evolved into a major agro-processing enterprise. Their journey reflects a blend of family legacy, innovation, and a commitment to delivering quality food products while promoting sustainable growth in the agri-food sector.
