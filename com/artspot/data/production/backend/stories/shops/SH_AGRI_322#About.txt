Nilkamal Traders, founded in 2024 in Kolkata, is a fast-growing manufacturer, exporter, and supplier of agro-based and handcrafted products. The company was established with a focus on delivering authentic Indian agricultural produce and artisanal goods to global markets. Their portfolio includes premium aromatic rice, fresh vegetables, spices, lotus seeds, jute bags, and eco-friendly bamboo products. With strong quality control, sustainable sourcing, and efficient logistics, they aim to preserve freshness, nutritional value, and cultural authenticity. Led by founder Mr. Shyam Prasad Das, the business positions itself as a reliable global partner for traditional Indian products.
