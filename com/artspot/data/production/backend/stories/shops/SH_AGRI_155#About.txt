Long before it became a brand, Satva began as a belief that took root over six decades ago, when farming was seen not as a trade, but as a responsibility to nourish both people and the earth. Guided by the values of Shri Thammanna and Shri Shankarappa, this legacy continues to live quietly in every field and harvest. What followed was not expansion, but evolution when a search for truly honest food led to the creation of a farm-to-fork journey built on trust and intention. Here, every ingredient is grown or sourced with care, often harvested, prepared, and delivered within the same day, preserving its natural vitality.
There are no preservatives, no shortcuts, only certified organic practices shaped by integrity and transparency. From the soil to your table, each offering reflects a balance between tradition and thoughtful innovation. Supported by like-minded organic farmers, the journey extends beyond one farm into a shared ecosystem of purity.
What reaches you is not just food, but a promise clean, traceable, and deeply rooted in purpose. Because true nourishment is not created overnight, it is cultivated over generations.
