Rathi Pedewala began as a traditional sweet and snack business with the vision of serving authentic vegetarian delicacies made with quality ingredients and time-tested recipes. Over the years, the brand has grown into a trusted name in Akola, expanding from its original offerings to a complete range of sweets, namkeens, snacks, and pure vegetarian dining. Built on a foundation of authentic taste, hygiene, and customer trust, the business continues to blend traditional flavors with modern service. Their journey reflects a commitment to preserving culinary heritage while evolving into a well-recognized regional food brand with multiple outlets and a loyal customer base.
