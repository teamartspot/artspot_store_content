Sonal Foods was established in 1995 with a vision of manufacturing and supplying high-quality farsan, namkeen, and ready-to-eat snacks. The company built its reputation by using pure ingredients, hygienic processing, and modern manufacturing facilities to deliver products with exceptional taste, freshness, and long shelf life. Over the years, Sonal Foods has expanded its portfolio to include a wide variety of traditional Indian snacks, serving customers across India as well as international markets. Backed by skilled professionals, advanced infrastructure, and stringent quality control, the company has consistently focused on customer satisfaction and product excellence. Today, Sonal Foods continues to be a trusted name in the snack industry, offering authentic flavors with reliable quality.
