Das Khaman's story began in 1922, when Shri Pitambardas Thakkar founded a small farsan shop in Ahmedabad with a vision of serving soft, fresh, and flavorful khaman prepared using a unique traditional recipe. His unwavering focus on quality and authentic taste quickly made the shop a favorite among local families, and Das Khaman became an integral part of festivals and everyday celebrations. The next generation expanded the business by introducing home delivery and broadening its product range while preserving the original recipes and standards. Over the decades, the brand has grown from a single neighborhood shop into one of Gujarat's most iconic snack destinations, known for its wide variety of khaman and farsan. Today, Das Khaman continues to carry forward its century-old legacy by blending tradition, innovation, and exceptional taste for customers across India.
