ZIVA Clothing began its journey in 2016 with just a small collection of 50–60 custom-designed sarees and a deep-rooted love for traditional Indian textiles. What started as a modest dream quickly evolved into a growing label known for its quality, creativity, and commitment to heritage.

The year 2017 marked a turning point when ZIVA embraced linen sarees—captivating hearts with their comfort and effortless sophistication. This shift deepened our connection with handloom clusters and artisan-led printing houses across India, inspiring us to expand our offerings while staying grounded in heritage.

Since then, ZIVA has released over 1,000 unique saree designs, each celebrating the hands and stories behind them. With every collaboration, our goal has remained the same: to uplift weavers, preserve fading techniques, and bring India’s diverse weaving legacy to the forefront.

From our humble beginnings to becoming a beloved name among saree connoisseurs, ZIVA continues to weave a story that’s rooted in tradition and tailored for the future.
