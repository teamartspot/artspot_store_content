Sohum Sutras was established in 2017 with a vision to bridge the gap between heritage weaves and mindful fashion. Born out of a deep respect for India’s textile traditions, the brand set out to work closely with artisans and weaving communities to preserve their art and bring it to the forefront of modern wardrobes.

What began as a small initiative has evolved into a curated platform where each saree tells a story — of hands that wove it, regions that nurtured it, and traditions that shaped it. From Banaras to Bengal, Kanchipuram to Chanderi, Sohum Sutras continues its journey of discovering and sharing the soul of Indian weaves, one saree at a time.
