Gaatha was founded with a clear vision — to honour and uplift traditional Indian artisans, giving them a platform where their work is valued not only as beautiful creations, but as narratives of culture and heritage. The idea was born from years of observing how handcrafted goods were often divorced from their creators — sold as commodities, rather than cultural expressions.

To change this, Gaatha was envisioned as a storytelling bridge — a way to connect the voices of artisans directly with a global audience. By working closely with craft clusters, documenting their techniques, and helping them reach new markets, we aim to ensure both economic sustainability and cultural continuity.

Through our curated collections and growing research archives, we continue to preserve India's rich craft traditions while supporting the livelihood and dignity of its creators.
