The name ‘Utkalamrita’ combines ‘Utkal’ (ancient name for Odisha) with ‘amrita’ (nectar). 
It symbolizes the essence of Odisha’s traditions captured in products. 
The initiative grew out of a movement to revive Odia millet foods and handloom crafts. 
Its founders partnered with farmer groups, weavers and women self-help groups. 
By building bridges between rural artisans and urban consumers, Utkalamrita preserves Odisha’s intangible heritage.
