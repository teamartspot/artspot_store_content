Kokan Swad was founded with the vision of bringing the authentic flavors of the Konkan region to consumers through fresh, natural, and traditionally prepared food products. Based in Devgad, Maharashtra, the brand sources premium ingredients directly from Konkan's farms, with a special focus on Alphonso mangoes, kokum, amla, and other regional specialties. By combining traditional recipes with hygienic processing and modern packaging, Kokan Swad preserves the natural taste and freshness of every product. The company is committed to supporting local farmers while making genuine Konkan delicacies accessible to customers across India. Today, Kokan Swad continues to celebrate the region's rich culinary heritage by delivering wholesome, authentic, and high-quality products from farm to doorstep.
