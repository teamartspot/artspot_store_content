Umar Faruk Khatri is a master block‑printing artisan from Bagh (Dhar), widely recognised for his expertise in traditional Bagh prints and natural‑dye techniques. His practice involves hand‑carving wooden blocks, preparing natural pigments, and training local apprentices in hand‑printing methods. Products under his name include sarees, dress materials and home textiles that follow time‑tested Bagh motifs and colour palettes. Umar Faruk’s work is available through curated craft platforms and online marketplaces that champion master artisans.
