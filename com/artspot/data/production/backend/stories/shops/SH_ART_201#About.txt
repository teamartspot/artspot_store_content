GmShah (The Kashmir Art Store) is a Srinagar-based retail outlet specialising in Kashmiri textiles, including sozni and chain-stitch embroidered shawls, pherans and pashminas. The store serves locals and visitors seeking traditional Kashmir hand-embroidery and woven textiles, offering bespoke options and a range of finished garments. Their inventory highlights regional materials—pashmina and fine wool—and intricate handwork which is often executed by local craft families. GmShah focuses on authentic Kashmir techniques and sources goods from experienced artisans within Srinagar and surrounding craft clusters. The physical location in Polo View/Kothi Bagh provides visibility to tourists and local shoppers.
