Studio Nakshi Kantha began as a family‑run endeavour in the greater Kolkata region to rescue hand‑stitched kantha traditions that were fading under mechanisation and fast fashion pressures. The studio established links with village stitchers and patuas to source authentic embroidered panels and grew by selling through local fairs and online platforms. Over time it expanded its repertoire to include designer collaborations and contemporary product lines that appeal to urban customers, while keeping the core hand‑stitching methods alive.
