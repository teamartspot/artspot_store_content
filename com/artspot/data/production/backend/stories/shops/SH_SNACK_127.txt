Mapro was founded in 1959 by Kishore Vora, a pharmacist, in Panchgani, Maharashtra, with a simple idea of turning the region's fresh strawberries into delicious jams. The company's name, MAPRO, stands for Mahabaleshwar Products, reflecting its deep connection with the hill stations of Mahabaleshwar and Panchgani. Built on the philosophy of creating wholesome, nutritious, and innovative fruit-based products, Mapro has grown from a small family business into one of India's leading processed food brands. By working closely with local fruit farmers and combining premium ingredients with modern manufacturing, the company has played a key role in developing the region into a major fruit-processing hub. Today, Mapro is renowned for its fruit syrups, jams, crushes, chocolates, candies, and the iconic Mapro Garden, continuing a legacy of quality, innovation, and authentic fruit goodness for over 65 years.
