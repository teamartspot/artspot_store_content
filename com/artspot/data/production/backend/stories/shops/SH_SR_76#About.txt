At Phulkari Forever, we are dedicated to celebrating the soul of Punjab through its most cherished handicraft — Phulkari. Our mission is to revive this timeless embroidery by blending tradition with modern design sensibilities. Each of our creations is 100% hand-embroidered by skilled women artisans, crafted on natural fabrics to offer you sustainable, premium clothing that reflects the elegance of Punjabi culture.

Our collection spans Indian, Western, and Indo-Western silhouettes — designed for today’s wardrobe while preserving the spirit of Phulkari. Rooted in conscious fashion, our brand stands for zero synthetics, sustainable practices, and empowering women through livelihood and skill training.

When you choose Phulkari Forever, you not only embrace a piece of rich heritage but also become a part of a movement that uplifts communities and keeps tradition alive with every thread.
