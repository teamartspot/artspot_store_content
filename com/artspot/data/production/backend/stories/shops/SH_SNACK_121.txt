Marco Food Products was established in 1989 in Ratnagiri, Maharashtra, with a vision of bringing the world-famous Alphonso mango and other natural food products to consumers across India and international markets. Backed by decades of experience in mango cultivation, the company works closely with local farmers through contract farming to ensure premium-quality produce from orchard to packaging. Specializing in Alphonso mango pulp, frozen fruits, pickles, juices, syrups, and other food products, Marco combines traditional agricultural expertise with modern processing technology. Committed to quality, food safety, and sustainability, the company follows stringent manufacturing and hygiene standards while preserving the natural taste of its products. Today, Marco Food Products continues to promote the authentic flavors of Ratnagiri by delivering premium, export-quality food products worldwide.
