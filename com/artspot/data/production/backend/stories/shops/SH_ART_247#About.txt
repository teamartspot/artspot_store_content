Ramakrishna Veena Works Ramakrishna Veena Works (Andhra Pradesh) is a specialist workshop manufacturing the Veena, the classical Indian string instrument, supplying handcrafted veenas to musicians, schools and cultural institutions. The workshop combines seasoned teak and tuned sympathetic strings with traditional carving and finishing, producing instruments suited for concert use and academic instruction. Ramakrishna’s instruments are tuned and tested by experienced luthiers to meet tonal standards and often include custom ornamentation on the face and neck.
