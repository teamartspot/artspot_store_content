Kotgirwar Food Products was established in 2001 in Pulgaon, Maharashtra, with the vision of delivering high-quality food products that combine great taste, freshness, and purity. Under the leadership of Mr. Santosh Ratnakar Kotgirwar, the company steadily expanded from a local manufacturer into a trusted supplier serving customers across India. By using superior ingredients, modern manufacturing techniques, and strict quality control, the company has built a reputation for reliable and hygienic products with a longer shelf life. Supported by a skilled workforce and efficient logistics, Kotgirwar Food Products has consistently focused on innovation, customer satisfaction, and timely delivery. Today, the company continues to grow while maintaining its commitment to quality, ethical business practices, and excellence in the food industry.
