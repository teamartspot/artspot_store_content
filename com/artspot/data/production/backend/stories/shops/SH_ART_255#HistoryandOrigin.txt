Harish Kumar Soni Coming from a lineage of artisans in Madhya Pradesh, Harish Kumar Soni has carried forward the skills of bell metal working associated with the Bundelkhand region. His practice reflects centuries‑old traditions where artisans hand‑cast and finish utensils and decorative items. By showcasing his work at exhibitions and maintaining active production, he contributes to sustaining Bundelkhand’s craft heritage in contemporary markets.
