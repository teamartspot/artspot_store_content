Bombay Sweet Shop was launched in 2020 by Hunger Inc. Hospitality, the team behind celebrated restaurants like The Bombay Canteen and O Pedro, with a vision to reimagine traditional Indian mithai for the modern generation. Inspired by India's rich sweet-making heritage, the brand blends authentic recipes with contemporary flavors, creative presentation, and premium ingredients. Every sweet is handcrafted to preserve the nostalgia of classic mithai while offering a fresh and exciting experience. Beyond sweets, the brand celebrates Indian festivals, gifting, and shared moments through beautifully designed products and packaging. Today, Bombay Sweet Shop has become one of India's most innovative premium mithai brands, bringing the magic of traditional sweets to customers across the country.
