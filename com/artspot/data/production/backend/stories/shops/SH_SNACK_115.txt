KP Foods, operated by Jai Nagoba Food Industries, traces its roots to 1989 in Raigad, Maharashtra, with a vision of delivering high-quality, nutritious, and innovative food products. Starting as a manufacturer of rice poha, the company has expanded its portfolio to include roasted chivda, millet puffs, kurmura, flakes, gluten-free rice flour, and other healthy snack products. Under the leadership of Mr. Prakash Jain and the current management team, the brand has focused on combining premium ingredients with modern processing and stringent quality standards. Driven by innovation and customer satisfaction, KP Foods has built a strong presence in domestic as well as international markets. Today, the company continues to promote healthier snacking while delivering authentic taste, quality, and nutrition to consumers worldwide.
