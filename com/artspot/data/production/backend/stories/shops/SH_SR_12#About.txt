Chanderiyaan is an e-commerce platform created to bring the elegance and intricate beauty of Chanderi weaving to a global audience. Rooted in the historic town of Chanderi in Ashok Nagar District, Madhya Pradesh, this initiative celebrates the legacy of local artisans through an online showcase of their timeless craftsmanship. The platform features a diverse range of authentic Chanderi products—from classic ethnic wear to intricately handcrafted designer pieces—each narrating centuries of weaving traditions and cultural heritage.

At its core, Chanderiyaan bridges the gap between Chanderi’s artisan community and modern markets by enabling direct connections between creators and customers. It functions as a community-driven enterprise, owned and operated by local weavers' self-help groups through the Chanderi Weavers Integrated Information Resource Centre—a collaborative effort supported by Media Lab Asia, the Digital Empowerment Foundation (DEF), and the Department of IT, Ministry of Communications and IT, Government of India.

Chanderiyaan stands as a social entrepreneurial initiative, aimed at enhancing the sustainability and livelihoods of Chanderi’s weaving community. It empowers weavers by removing the layers of intermediaries, ensuring they receive direct profits from each sale. Every purchase made on Chanderiyaan contributes to strengthening this local heritage and uplifting the artisan economy.

Furthering its mission, Chanderiyaan—alongside DEF and Media Lab Asia—spearheads the Chanderi Weavers ICT Resource Centre, an initiative offering digital literacy and vocational training to weavers. This effort fosters inclusive, community-driven growth through the Chanderi Integrated ICT for Development Program (CIIDP), promoting long-term empowerment and economic resilience for the artisans of Chanderi.
