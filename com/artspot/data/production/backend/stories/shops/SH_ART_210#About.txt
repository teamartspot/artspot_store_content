Sri Vijayalakshmi Fine Kalamkari Arts, based in Srikalahasti, is a reputed studio producing fine pen‑kalamkari sarees and painted textiles, often featuring mythological and natural themes rendered in natural dyes. The studio has gained recognition among craft tourists and collectors for its detailed hand-painted panels and collaborative work with designers to adapt traditional iconography for contemporary textiles. Sri Vijayalakshmi also participates in local craft promotion, classes and demonstrations that help visitors understand the multi-step Kalamkari process.
