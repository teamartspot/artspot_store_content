R Agro was founded with a vision of bringing the authentic flavors of Konkan directly from farms to consumers while supporting local farmers and sustainable agriculture. The company specializes in premium Alphonso mangoes, fruit pulps, kokum products, coconut oil, and other traditional Konkan delicacies, ensuring freshness and traceability from farm to table. Guided by a farmer-oriented and sustainable farming approach, R Agro combines nature's bounty with modern processing to deliver consistent quality. The brand is committed to preserving the rich agricultural heritage of the Konkan region while making its products accessible across India and global markets. Today, R Agro continues to connect consumers with the natural goodness of Konkan through high-quality, authentic, and responsibly sourced food products.
