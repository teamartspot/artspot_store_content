Foodrite India, the food division of Radiant Indus Chem Pvt. Ltd., began its journey in 2008 with the vision of providing nutritious, high-quality processed food products to Indian consumers and the food service industry. Starting with the brands Mrs. Foodrite and Meal Time, the company steadily expanded its portfolio to include mayonnaise, sauces, jams, pickles, toppings, and beverage concentrates. Backed by a state-of-the-art FSSC 22000-certified manufacturing facility in Aurangabad, Foodrite combines innovation, food safety, and modern technology to deliver world-class products. Their journey reflects a commitment to improving consumers' lives through quality, customized food solutions, responsible business practices, and continuous innovation in the food processing industry.
