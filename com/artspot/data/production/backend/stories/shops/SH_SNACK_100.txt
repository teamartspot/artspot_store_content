Budhani Bros Waferwala was founded in 1955 by Madhavji M. Budhani in Pune, Maharashtra, with a vision of making fresh, crispy potato wafers using simple ingredients and traditional methods. Soon joined by his brothers Motilal and Mangaldas, the family transformed a small venture into one of Pune's most iconic snack brands. Built on the principles of quality, affordability, and excellent customer service, Budhani Bros earned the trust of generations through its signature potato wafers and potato mixtures. Over the years, the company adopted modern manufacturing while preserving the authentic taste that made it famous. Today, with over six decades of heritage, Budhani Bros continues to delight customers in India and abroad with its commitment to freshness, tradition, and quality.
