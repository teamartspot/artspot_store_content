Batua City Fashion Batua City Fashion is a Lucknow-based retail store with a longstanding reputation for batua (small potli/purse) and traditional handbag styles, serving local customers and bridal markets. The shop curates embroidered potli bags, small clutches and ethnic accessories often used in weddings and festive occasions. With decades of operation, Batua City Fashion maintains a local storefront and also liaises with bridal boutiques for bulk orders and customization.
