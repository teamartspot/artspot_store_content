Amba Bhadang began as a family business in the town of Jaysingpur, Maharashtra, and has been delighting snack lovers with its authentic taste since 1963. Over the last 60+ years, the brand has become a household name across the Sangli–Kolhapur region, earning the trust of multiple generations. Its signature Bhadang is made using crispy rice flakes, premium peanuts, and a unique blend of traditional spices that deliver a distinct Kolhapuri flavor. Inspired by India's beloved street food culture, Amba Bhadang combines heritage with quality to bring an authentic Maharashtrian snack to customers across the country.
