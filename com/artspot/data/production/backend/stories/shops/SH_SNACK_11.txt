Shree Guru Namkeen was established in 2013 under Reva Food Industries in Amravati with the vision of bringing the authentic taste of Maharashtrian namkeen to consumers across India. Founded by Shailesh Agrawal, the brand combines traditional snack recipes with modern manufacturing, using carefully selected ingredients and strict quality standards. Starting with a small range of products, the company steadily expanded its portfolio, built a strong dealer network across Maharashtra, and partnered with leading retail chains such as D-Mart. Today, Shree Guru Namkeen continues its journey by blending tradition, quality, and innovation while working towards becoming a nationally recognized and globally trusted packaged snack brand.
