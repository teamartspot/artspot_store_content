Amid the tranquil slopes of Avakali, where mountains open into valleys, and the still waters of Dhom Dam reflect the sky, Mauli Strawberry Farm grows more than fruit; it cultivates an experience. Here, over fourteen carefully nurtured varieties of organic strawberries flourish, each shaped by the cool climate and rich red soil that define Mahabaleshwar’s legacy. The journey begins months before harvest, with select mother saplings that some brought from distant lands, gently multiplied and replanted with care and precision. Nourished with natural manure and mindful practices, every berry ripens slowly, absorbing the character of its surroundings. From Sweet Charlie to rare seasonal varieties, each harvest carries both science and tradition in harmony. Beyond cultivation, the farm opens its doors to those who wish to walk the land, pick their own fruit, and understand its story. Even other renowned farms trust Mauli for its quality, making it a quiet cornerstone of the region. What reaches you is not just freshness, but a reflection of a place where nature, knowledge, and patience come together beautifully. Because here, every strawberry is not just grown, it is thoughtfully brought to life.
