The Indian Ethnic Co. brings the timeless heritage of Indian handmade clothing to today's world. Founded in 2016 by mother-daughter duo Hetal Desai (Chief Creative Designer) and Lekhinee Desai (Sales & Marketing), the brand draws on their shared passion for handloom and handicraft artisanship. They curate and deliver a vibrant collection—featuring sarees, kurtas, dresses, and dupattas—highlighting traditional techniques such as hand-block printing, natural dyeing, and handloom weaving. With two flagship stores in Mumbai and a dynamic online presence, the brand showcases textiles from India’s hinterlands, reimagined with a modern twist. Their mission is to make India's rich textile culture accessible globally, blending authenticity with contemporary appeal.
