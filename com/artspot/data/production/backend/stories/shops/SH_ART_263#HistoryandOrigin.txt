Agra Dhurrie Bhandar Exports Established in 1982, Agra Dhurrie Bhandar Exports grew during Agra's expansion as a textile hub beyond traditional carpet weaving. Leveraging regional weaving skills and dyeing capacity, the company scaled operations to meet global demand for cotton and mixed-fibre floor coverings. Their history reflects Agra's diversification in home textiles and the sustained demand for Indian dhurrie and rug craftsmanship in export markets.
