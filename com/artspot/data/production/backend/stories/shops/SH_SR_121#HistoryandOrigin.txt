The tradition of Eri silk in Lapngar began in the 1970s with artisan Kristina Dris Lyngdoh Thaiang, who introduced spinning and weaving to the village. Her legacy was carried forward by Kong Prissila Rymbai and later reignited in 2018 by Kong Iaishah Rymbai, whose efforts led to the founding of Eriweave. Supported by the Raid Lapngar Handloom Weaver Cooperative Society, Eriweave grew into a movement that revives age-old weaving traditions and uplifts local women artisans.
