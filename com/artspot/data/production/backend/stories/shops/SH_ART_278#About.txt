Fancy Emporium Fancy Emporium (Khurja) is a local handicraft and ceramic retail outlet in Khurja, Bulandshahr district — a town known as a ceramic hub. The shop supplies a range of pottery, decorative wares and household ceramics, serving both walk-in customers and wholesale buyers. Local listings identify the shop near key landmarks on the GT Road and show it as a recognised supplier to Khurja's ceramic market. Fancy Emporium is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
