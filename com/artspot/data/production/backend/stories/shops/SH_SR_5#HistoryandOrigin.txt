The creative spirit behind Fabulous Threads Pvt Ltd, Pearl Uppal, inherited her love for Indian needlecrafts and bespoke fashion from her mother, Kulbir. From a young age, Pearl was immersed in the world of custom design—never wearing anything off the rack. Every outfit she wore was thoughtfully imagined, personally sketched, and hand-sewn to perfection by her mother, tailored precisely to her measurements and dreams.

Their bond was stitched together with shared creativity—experimenting with hand-sewing techniques, hand-dyeing fabrics, and elevating each piece with intricate embroidery. From the first day of pre-nursery to college milestones, birthdays, and beyond, each special occasion was marked by a collaboration: Pearl would envision her dream outfit, and Kulbir would bring it to life with unrelenting care and craftsmanship.

Her wedding trousseau became a living archive of heritage artistry and timeless silhouettes, designed with intention and detail. Even today, Pearl doesn’t know her "standard size"—because her wardrobe has only ever known made-to-measure. Her signature style lives in the subtle space between a sheath and a shift dress, in her own unique shade of black that dances between charcoal and midnight. Her favorite neckline is a soft blend of a crew and a boat, always enhancing her shoulders and elongating her frame, with every fabric chosen to delight the skin and every design reflecting understated elegance.

This deeply personal approach shapes Fabulous Threads Pvt Ltd—a brand for individuals who know what they want, who appreciate quality, and who have the confidence to explore fashion that is crafted, not mass-produced.
