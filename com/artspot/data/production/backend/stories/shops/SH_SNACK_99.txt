Suhana began its journey in 1962 when Shri Dhananjay Datar's family started a small spice business with a vision of delivering pure, authentic Indian masalas. Built on traditional recipes and a commitment to quality, the brand steadily expanded from basic spices to a wide range of blended masalas, instant mixes, ready-to-cook products, and seasonings. By combining carefully sourced ingredients with modern manufacturing technology, Suhana has consistently delivered the rich taste of Indian cuisine while maintaining stringent quality standards. Over the decades, the brand has grown into a trusted household name in India and international markets, serving millions of consumers. Today, Suhana continues to preserve India's culinary heritage through innovation, authenticity, and an unwavering focus on quality.
