Established in 1968, the Kerala State Handloom Development Corporation Ltd (Hanveev) was born out of a need to protect traditional hand weaving from the rise of mechanized and automated looms. With the support of the State and Central Governments, Hanveev began as a mission-driven initiative to safeguard handloom artisans and the ancient craft of weaving.

Originating from Kannur (Cannanore) — known as the “Land of Looms and Lores” — Hanveev draws upon a centuries-old tradition of weaving that is deeply rooted in Kerala’s cultural identity. Over decades, the corporation has grown into a pillar of support for the weaving community, upholding the values of heritage, skill, and fair livelihood.

Today, Hanveev not only preserves this age-old art form but also innovates and adapts it to meet modern demands — ensuring Kerala's loom legacy is passed down, thread by thread, to future generations.
