Shivam Arts Export is a craft export/manufacturer brand based in Varanasi working with handloom, scarves, shawls and home textiles.
The company operates an online storefront and trade portal showing product ranges including meditation shawls, silk scarves and craft items.
Their public pages describe collaborations with local women artisans and community production models.
Shivam Arts has social profiles and a dedicated export-facing catalogue for international buyers.
Contact and product pages confirm active retail/export operations from Varanasi.
