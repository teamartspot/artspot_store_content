Minni's Chikki traces its roots to the famous chikki-making tradition of Lonavala, where authentic recipes have been passed down through generations. The brand was established with the aim of preserving the original taste of handcrafted chikki while maintaining consistent quality and hygiene. Using premium ingredients such as peanuts, jaggery, dry fruits, and sesame seeds, Minni's Chikki blends traditional methods with modern food processing. Over the years, it has expanded its range to include a variety of sweets and snacks without compromising on authenticity. Today, Minni's Chikki is recognized as a trusted brand, bringing the rich heritage of Lonavala's iconic chikki to customers across India.
