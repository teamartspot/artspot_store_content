Chitale Bandhu traces its roots to 1939, when Late Shri Bhaskar Ganesh Chitale began a humble milk distribution business in Maharashtra with a commitment to quality and purity. In 1950, Raghunath (Bhausaheb) Chitale and Narsinha (Rajabhau) Chitale established Chitale Bandhu Mithaiwale in Pune, expanding the family business into sweets and traditional snacks. The brand became an icon after introducing its famous Bakarwadi, which transformed it into one of Maharashtra's most loved food brands. Blending traditional recipes with modern technology and stringent quality standards, Chitale Bandhu has expanded its portfolio to include sweets, namkeen, dairy products, instant mixes, and ready-to-eat foods. Today, the brand is recognized worldwide for its authentic Maharashtrian flavors, carrying forward a legacy of trust, innovation, and excellence built over 85 years.
