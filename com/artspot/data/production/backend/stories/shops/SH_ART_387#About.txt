Arab Hasam Khatri’s Workshop is located in Nirona village of Kutch, Gujarat. 
It is associated with Rogan art, a rare hand-painting technique using castor oil and natural pigments. 
The Khatri family is internationally known for preserving this endangered craft. 
Tourists and researchers visit Nirona to see live demonstrations of Rogan painting at the workshop. 
The family’s work has been exhibited worldwide and recognized by the Government of India.
