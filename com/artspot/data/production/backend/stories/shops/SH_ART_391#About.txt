Khagen Goswami is a traditional mask maker from Majuli, Assam. 
He is documented in cultural reports as an artisan of Satriya masks used in bhawna performances. 
His workshop produces masks of mythological characters like Narasimha and Garuda. 
The masks are made from bamboo, clay and cloth, painted with natural colors. 
Khagen Goswami is recognized for passing this heritage to younger generations in Majuli.
