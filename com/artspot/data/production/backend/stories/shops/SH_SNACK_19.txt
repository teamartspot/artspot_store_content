Gagan Shegaon Kachori was founded in 1950 near the Shegaon Railway Station with the vision of serving delicious, authentic Shegaon-style kachoris to travelers and local customers. Under the leadership of Mr. Gagan Sharma, the family business has preserved its traditional taste while embracing modern food processing innovations. A major milestone in its journey was the introduction of frozen Shegaon kachoris, enabling customers to enjoy the signature snack with a shelf life of up to 90 days without preservatives. Backed by modern infrastructure, ISO 22000 certification, and a growing franchise network, the company has transformed a beloved local delicacy into a recognized brand across India while staying committed to quality, hygiene, and authentic flavor.
