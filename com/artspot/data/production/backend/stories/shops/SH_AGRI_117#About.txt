Sometimes, the journey forward begins by going back.
For Nik Enterprise, it started with a decision to return to Nagaland, not just to reconnect with roots, but to reimagine what those roots could create. Founded in 2022, the company brings together the richness of Northeast India and the discipline of global trade.
What emerges is a diverse offering of spices, herbs, rice, and native produce sourced directly from the region’s landscapes, where cultivation still follows natural rhythms and local knowledge.
Yet, the purpose extends beyond production. Built as a Pan-India and export-focused enterprise, it creates pathways connecting local farmers to wider markets while opening opportunities for communities to grow without leaving home.
Registered with FSSAI and the Spice Board of India, every shipment reflects both compliance and care, balancing authenticity with reliability.
Here, trade is not just the movement of goods; it is the movement of possibility.
