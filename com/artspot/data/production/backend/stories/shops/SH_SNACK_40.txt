Gokul Chips, a brand of Mahameru Foods Pvt. Ltd., was established with the vision of delivering fresh, delicious, and high-quality snacks made from carefully selected ingredients. Based in Jalna, Maharashtra, the company started by producing banana chips and potato wafers and gradually expanded its portfolio to include namkeens, spices, sweets, and ready-to-cook products. Built on a commitment to quality, hygiene, and authentic taste, the brand combines traditional snack recipes with modern manufacturing and packaging techniques. Today, Gokul Chips continues its journey by offering a diverse range of products while striving to bring the flavors of Maharashtra to customers across India through innovation, consistency, and customer satisfaction
