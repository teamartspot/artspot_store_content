Founded in 2001 by Sanjay and Kalpesh, this venture was born out of a deep-rooted legacy within the Khatri community—renowned for their centuries-old expertise in Tie & Dye, specifically Bandhani (Bandhej). Embracing both tradition and innovation, the duo set out to elevate Bandhani craftsmanship to a wider, more contemporary audience.

Starting with their first retail outlet in Jamnagar, Gujarat, they gradually expanded to other cities across the state. Young, passionate, and forward-thinking, Sanjay introduced corporate-style systems and a structured approach focused on creativity, quality, and innovation—reshaping Bandhani into a refined fashion art form.
