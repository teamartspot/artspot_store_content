Sarvaay was founded in 2019 by Dr. Vishal Sardeshpande and Dr. Madhavi Sardeshpande with a vision of transforming traditional jaggery production through science and innovation. The brand developed a patented Resource Efficient Jaggery Processing (REJP) technology to produce 100% chemical-free, free-flowing jaggery while preserving its natural minerals and taste. Beyond offering healthier jaggery products, Sarvaay aims to create sustainable income opportunities for rural communities by improving agro-processing technologies. By combining traditional wisdom with modern engineering, the company promotes hygienic, resource-efficient, and environmentally friendly food production. Today, Sarvaay continues to redefine natural sweeteners while empowering farmers and delivering premium-quality jaggery products to health-conscious consumers.
