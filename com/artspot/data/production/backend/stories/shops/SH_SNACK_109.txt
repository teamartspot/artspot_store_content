POORNA by Pune Spice Mill was founded in 2020 during the COVID-19 pandemic with a vision of bringing authentic, high-quality Indian spices and food products to every household. After extensive market research, the company was formally incorporated in October 2020 and dedicated its name to the city of Pune, celebrating its rich culinary heritage and traditions. The brand carefully sources premium spices and herbs from trusted suppliers across India and the world, using traditional recipes and time-honored techniques to preserve authentic flavors. Along with spices, POORNA has expanded its range to include cold-pressed oils, pickles, chutneys, and other pantry essentials, all focused on purity and quality. Today, POORNA continues its mission of delivering genuine, preservative-free flavors while sharing India's rich food heritage with customers across the country.
