Swadful Food Products was established in 2011 in Dhule, Maharashtra, with the vision of providing high-quality tea and later expanding into spices and papad for everyday households. The company began its journey with tea and, after earning customer trust through its consistent aroma, taste, and quality, diversified into traditional Indian spices and crispy udad papad. Built on a commitment to sourcing quality raw materials and maintaining uniform standards through modern processing, the brand has steadily grown across Maharashtra. Today, under the "Mr. Swadful" and "Mr. Cup" brands, the company continues its journey of combining authentic taste, quality, and innovation to become a trusted name in Indian kitchens.
