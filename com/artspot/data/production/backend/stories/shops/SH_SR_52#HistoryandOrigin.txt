Bandhaniwala Trading Co. traces its origins to the vibrant textile traditions of Gujarat, India. What began as a small venture focused on Bandhani craft has now grown into a well-recognized name across India and overseas. With deep roots in the art of hand-dyeing and textile embellishment, our brand emerged to meet the growing demand for authentic Indian ethnic wear crafted with love and legacy.

Over the years, we have combined traditional techniques with modern aesthetics, ensuring each product represents not just a garment, but a cultural statement. Our robust production process, managed under strict quality controls, ensures that every creation lives up to the promise of beauty, durability, and elegance.

To maintain the charm of our products, we recommend:

Dry clean only (petrol wash) for silk, chiffon, georgette, crape, and Gaji silk.

Machine or hand wash for cotton and synthetic fabrics.

Avoid twisting garments before or after washing to maintain their shape and quality.
