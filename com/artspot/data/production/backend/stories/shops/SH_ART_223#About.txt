Banjara Kasuti is a social enterprise dedicated to reviving the Lambani (Banjara) embroidery tradition, producing embroidered textiles and wearable craft items while providing stable incomes to rural women artisans. The organisation focuses on hand‑stitched Kasuti patterns adapted for contemporary markets and maintains a training programme that improves stitch quality and design uniformity. Products include embroidered stoles, garments and home textiles that combine traditional motifs with modern usability. Banjara Kasuti emphasises ethical wages and community empowerment as core pillars of its model.
