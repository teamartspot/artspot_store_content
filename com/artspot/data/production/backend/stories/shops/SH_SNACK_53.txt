Go Cheese is a premium cheese brand from Parag Milk Foods, created to make international-quality cheese accessible to Indian consumers. The name "Go" is derived from Gowardhan, reflecting the brand's heritage while symbolizing energy and a modern lifestyle. Manufactured at the company's state-of-the-art cheese plant in Manchar, Maharashtra, Go Cheese is produced under stringent quality standards using pure cow's milk. The brand has grown to become one of India's largest suppliers of cheese, serving households as well as hotels, restaurants, pizza chains, and street food vendors. With continuous innovation in products and packaging, Go Cheese offers a wide variety of cheeses that combine great taste, convenience, and international quality for Indian consumers.
