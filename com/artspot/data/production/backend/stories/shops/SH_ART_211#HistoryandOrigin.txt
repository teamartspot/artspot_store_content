Hastkala began as a small initiative to bring traditional Indian hand-painted textiles into mainstream fashion, drawing on artisans and weavers from Bihar and neighbouring craft centres. Over time the label consolidated supply chains from Bhagalpur silk weavers and local painters, formalising partnerships and online retail channels. The enterprise grew by curating region-specific craft narratives—such as Madhubani and Jhaar art—and packaging them for festival and bridal markets. Today Hastkala acts as a bridge between weavers, painters and urban consumers, aiming to sustain artisanal livelihoods through consistent market access.
