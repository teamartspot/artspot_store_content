Khalchian Wale Di Hatti Khalchian Wale Di Hatti is an Amritsar-based shop specialising in Phulkari suits, dupattas and Punjabi embroidered garments, located near Katra Jaimal Singh Market. The store supplies bridal trousseau items, festive wear and embroidered textiles prized for traditional Phulkari motifs and vibrant colours. Khalchian Wale Di Hatti caters to local shoppers and tourists visiting Amritsar’s traditional textile bazaars and offers tailoring and customization services.
