Bombaywala Sweets was founded in 1975 in Nagpur, Maharashtra, with a vision of serving authentic Indian sweets and namkeen made with uncompromising quality and traditional recipes. What began as a humble family business has grown into a trusted brand with over 50 years of taste and tradition, earning the loyalty of generations of customers. The company combines time-honored sweet-making techniques with modern manufacturing and hygiene standards to deliver fresh, premium-quality products. Over the years, Bombaywala has expanded its offerings to include sweets, namkeen, dry fruits, bakery products, and festive gift hampers. Today, the brand continues to celebrate Indian traditions by bringing authentic flavors, quality, and happiness to customers across the country.
