Usha Kalamkari Handcrafts is a Srikalahasti-based studio and online retailer specialising in Srikalahasti-style Kalamkari hand-painted textiles and sarees. The brand produces pen-kalamkari and block-kalamkari pieces, offering traditional mythological panels, sarees and home textiles made with natural dyes and hand-drawn motifs. Usha Kalamkari also provides contactable studio visits, workshops and custom orders, positioning itself as both artisan studio and exporter-friendly producer. Contact details and studio address are publicly listed for customer enquiries and wholesale orders.
