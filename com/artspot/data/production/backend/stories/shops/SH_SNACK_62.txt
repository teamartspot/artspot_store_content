Rambandhu traces its roots back to 1962, when Shri Ramchandra (Anna) Marathe started a small flour mill in Pune, Maharashtra, with a commitment to providing pure and high-quality food products. The business gradually expanded into spices, pickles, papads, instant mixes, and other traditional Maharashtrian foods, earning the trust of generations of customers. Built on the values of authenticity, quality, and innovation, Rambandhu combines time-tested recipes with modern manufacturing and strict quality standards. Over the decades, the brand has grown into one of Maharashtra's leading packaged food companies while preserving the homemade taste of its products. Today, Rambandhu continues to bring authentic Indian flavors to kitchens across India and around the world through its diverse range of trusted food products.
