Yadu Nandan Fashions is a forward-looking ethnic wear wholesaler and direct-from-factory supplier designed for the next generation of boutique owners, resellers, and online entrepreneurs. Based in Surat — India’s hub for sarees and bridal fashion — our mission is to connect global buyers with the finest Indian craftsmanship at competitive prices.

We specialize in bulk and resale-friendly collections of sarees, bridal lehengas, and imitation jewelry, blending tradition with trend. With streamlined logistics and worldwide shipping, we ensure that your orders move seamlessly from our floor to your store — anywhere across the globe.

Our catalog includes a wide range of sarees — from designer party wear and golden organza to classic Kanjivaram, Banarasi, and modern printed fusions — tailored to meet diverse customer preferences in the USA, UK, Australia, UAE, and beyond.
