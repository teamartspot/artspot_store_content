Kirti Gold is the flagship brand of the Kirti Group, which has been serving Indian kitchens with quality and healthy food products since 1970. Based in Latur, Maharashtra, the company has grown into one of India's leading manufacturers of edible oils and high-protein de-oiled cakes. Built on over 50 years of excellence, Kirti Gold combines advanced manufacturing technology with stringent quality standards to deliver pure, preservative-free products. The company follows a "Sampoorna Swadeshi" philosophy by sourcing indigenous crops, supporting Indian farmers, and promoting self-reliance. Today, with a strong distribution network across multiple states, Kirti Gold continues to provide trusted, nutritious products to millions of households.
