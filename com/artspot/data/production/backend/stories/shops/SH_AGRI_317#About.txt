Kishan Agro Product began as a family-led rice business with the aim of supplying authentic premium rice varieties from West Bengal to broader markets. Established in 2005 in Purba Bardhaman, the company was founded by entrepreneurs who wanted to improve quality standards in the rice trade by offering carefully processed aromatic and specialty rice varieties like Gobindobhog, Jeerakasala, and biryani rice. Over the years, the business grew from a trading operation into a recognized manufacturer, exporter, and supplier serving domestic and international customers. Their journey reflects a focus on quality, traditional rice heritage, and building trust in Bengal’s premium rice market.
