Mahakesar was formed as a farmer-led initiative under the Aamba Bagayatdar Sangh, Maharashtra, with the mission of bringing Kesar mango growers together on a single platform to solve cultivation and market challenges. The organization focuses on promoting advanced cultivation techniques, expert guidance, and training for mango farmers to improve quality and export readiness. It also works to build a strong Mahakesar brand for domestic and international markets while creating better post-harvest, processing, and export opportunities. Through festivals, seminars, and farmer support programs, Mahakesar aims to empower horticulturists and ensure fair value for Maharashtra’s Kesar mango growers.
