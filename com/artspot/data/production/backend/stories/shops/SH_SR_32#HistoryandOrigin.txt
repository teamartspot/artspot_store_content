Founded in 1959, Utkalika (OSCHC Ltd.) (originally established as a state-supported initiative) began with the intent to uplift Odisha’s artisan communities by enhancing their marketing access and livelihoods. Over the decades, the organization evolved into a key cultural institution, spearheading not just retail opportunities, but also development programs like raw material banking, skill training, GI registrations, and artisan ID facilitation.

Operating under the administrative wing of the Handlooms, Textiles & Handicrafts Department of the Government of Odisha, the organization has grown into a catalyst for sustainable heritage promotion. With the launch of its official website and digital presence, Utkalika (OSCHC Ltd.) continues to represent Odisha’s legacy on the world stage—honoring age-old traditions while embracing modern avenues.
