Studio Nakshi Kantha is a Kolkata‑area studio specialising in Nakshi Kantha and contemporary hand‑stitched textiles, offering a range of sarees, stoles and home textiles crafted with traditional running‑stitch embroidery. The studio sources fine cottons, linens and silks, and works directly with rural artisans to produce custom and ready‑to‑sell pieces. Studio Nakshi Kantha showcases a mix of ritual motifs and modern patterns, often photographed and sold via social channels and local boutiques. The studio also offers curated workshops and small trade collaborations to spread awareness about kantha heritage.
