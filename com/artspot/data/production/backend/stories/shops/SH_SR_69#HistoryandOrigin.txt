Founded with a clear vision to revive and sustain India’s handloom ecosystem, Sunasa draws inspiration from the villages where weaving has been a way of life for generations. These weavers, who embody centuries of skill and tradition, are at the heart of everything we do. Recognizing the urgent need to protect this art from extinction, Sunasa was established as a platform to bridge the gap between rural craftsmanship and global demand.

Our strategy is rooted in sustainable development—revitalizing village-based weaving communities by creating modern economic opportunities without compromising their traditional way of life. Sunasa functions as more than a brand—it is a movement and an institution that serves the collective well-being of weavers, celebrates Indian heritage, and advocates for conscious, meaningful fashion. By drawing wisdom from the past and integrating it into the present, we aim to safeguard the soul of Indian textiles for future generations.
