Dinshaw's was founded in 1932 by brothers Dinshaw Rana and Erachshaw Rana in Nagpur, Maharashtra, beginning as a small dairy business. Driven by a passion for quality and innovation, they expanded into ice cream, introducing delicious new flavours that quickly won the hearts of consumers. Over the decades, the company diversified into dairy products, bakery items, and namkeen while maintaining its commitment to purity, taste, and strict quality standards. With modern manufacturing facilities and a strong distribution network, Dinshaw's has grown into one of India's leading regional food brands, serving customers across multiple states. Today, the brand continues its legacy of delivering high-quality products while staying true to the values of trust, innovation, and customer delight.
