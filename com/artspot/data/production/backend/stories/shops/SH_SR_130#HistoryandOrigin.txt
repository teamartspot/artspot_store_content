Founded in 2008 by the North East Network (NEN) in partnership with local women artisans, Chizami Weaves began with just seven weavers and has since expanded across 17 villages in Phek district. The initiative was a response to declining traditional weaving, aiming to revive the portable loin-loom craft and create decentralized, home-based production. With design support from institutions like NID and growing market reach—online, exhibitions, and retail in major metros—the collective now sustains over 600 rural women, uniting cultural pride with economic empowerment
