Zantye Kaju, established in 1979, has built a strong reputation as one of Maharashtra's most trusted cashew brands, with over four decades of experience in cashew processing. The company was founded with the vision of delivering premium-quality cashew nuts without compromising on taste, freshness, or quality. By combining traditional expertise with modern processing methods, it has consistently produced high-grade cashew kernels for a wide customer base. Its commitment to sustainable production, superior quality, and customer satisfaction has helped the brand earn lasting trust and recognition. Today, Zantye Kaju continues to uphold its legacy by offering premium cashews and dry fruits while maintaining the highest standards of excellence
