Jhama Sweets was founded in the 1950s by Late Mr. Jhamamal Lulla, a post-Partition refugee who started a small sweet stall in Chembur, Mumbai, with a passion for authentic Indian sweets. Beginning with just a few traditional delicacies, the business grew through hard work, dedication, and an unwavering commitment to quality. Over the decades, the family has preserved its rich Sindhi culinary heritage while expanding the product range to include a wide variety of Indian sweets, savouries, snacks, and festive specialties. Today, under the leadership of the next generation, Jhama Sweets operates multiple outlets across Mumbai and continues to delight customers with fresh, hygienic, and authentic flavors. The brand remains a symbol of tradition, trust, and craftsmanship, serving generations of sweet lovers.
