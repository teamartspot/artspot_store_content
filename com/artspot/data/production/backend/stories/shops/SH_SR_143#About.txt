Boutique Dheu is a celebrated name in the Indian ethnic fashion space, known for its exquisite handloom and handcrafted collections. Founded in 2016 by designer Mrs. Gargi Paul, in association with Mr. Biman Paul, the boutique has grown into a dynamic retail brand now operating as Dheu Retail Private Limited. With a digital-first approach, Dheu is accessible via its e-commerce platform www.dheu.in, the Dheu mobile app (available on both Google Play Store and Apple App Store), and through major marketplaces like Amazon.

Renowned as one of the fastest-growing ethnic wear brands in India's small-scale retail industry—with a valuation of ₹10 crore—Dheu offers a curated selection of sarees, dhoti-kurta sets, couple and family ensembles, kids' ethnic wear, and handcrafted silver jewellery. Dheu’s in-house collections such as the Signature Designer Couple Set and Babu Mosai Dhoti-Kurta Collection reflect its dedication to blending tradition with modern aesthetics.

Customer centricity lies at the heart of Dheu, and it is this deep commitment to quality, authenticity, and personalized service that has helped the brand build enduring relationships with its patrons.
