In the vibrant raisin capital of Sangli, Rohini Raisins began as the vision of a determined entrepreneur who saw beyond local markets and imagined Indian agricultural excellence reaching the world. What started in 1994 as a focused trading venture gradually evolved into a trusted global name, powered by decades of expertise, strong farmer partnerships, and a relentless promise of quality and punctual delivery. Backed by modern infrastructure and deep industry roots, Rohini’s story is one of ambition transformed into reliability where every carefully sorted raisin carries the sweetness of tradition, precision, and a legacy built to cross borders.
