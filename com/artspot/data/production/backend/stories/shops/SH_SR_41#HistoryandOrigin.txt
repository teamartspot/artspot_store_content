The story of Sundarii Handmade began in late 2017, born from a desire to preserve India’s dwindling heritage crafts and uplift the communities that create them. As fast fashion took center stage, ancient textile traditions and the weavers behind them faded into obscurity. Sundarii Handmade emerged as a voice for these forgotten arts.

Rooted in the belief that skilled artisans deserve both recognition and fair economic opportunity, Varnika set out to create a bridge between rural craftsmanship and conscious global consumers. Through design, education, and transparent practices, we’ve helped bring lesser-known crafts like Ajrakh to wider audiences, while also championing other indigenous styles from across the country.

Our origins lie in empathy and awareness—a mission to replace silence with purpose. Today, with over six years of impact, we continue our efforts to make sustainable fashion more accessible, ethical, and beautifully meaningful.
