Merwans began its journey in 1930, when Mr. K. M. Irani and Mrs. D. K. Irani started a family bakery that quickly became known for its freshly baked cakes, pastries, mawa cakes, and cookies. Their son, Mr. Merwan Irani, carried the legacy forward by introducing the Merwans brand in 1972, with a vision of making fresh bakery products accessible to every household. Over four generations, the Irani family has blended traditional baking techniques with modern technology while maintaining uncompromising quality and affordability. From a single outlet in Andheri, Mumbai, Merwans has expanded to dozens of stores across Mumbai, Pune, and Bengaluru through company-owned and franchise outlets. Today, the brand continues to delight generations of customers with its heritage, trusted recipes, and commitment to delivering fresh, high-quality baked products every day.
