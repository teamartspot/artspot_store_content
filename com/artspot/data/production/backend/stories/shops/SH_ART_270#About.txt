Ajay Glass Emporium Ajay Glass Emporium (Ajay Glass Works) is a Firozabad-based glass manufacturer and emporium providing natural colored glassware, bottles and decorative items. The company operates manufacturing furnaces and offers an assortment of glass products for domestic and export markets. Ajay Glass emphasizes technical capacity and finishing, supplying both B2B and retail clients with custom and standard glassware solutions. Ajay Glass Emporium is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
