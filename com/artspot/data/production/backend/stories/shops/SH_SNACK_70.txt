Aazol was founded in 2021 by Apurva Purohit and Siddharth Purohit with a mission to preserve Maharashtra's rich culinary heritage while creating sustainable livelihoods for rural communities. The brand partners directly with women-led Self-Help Groups (SHGs), farmer collectives, and rural artisans to bring authentic, handmade Maharashtrian foods to consumers across India. By combining traditional recipes, small-batch production, and modern technology, Aazol ensures fair prices for producers and premium-quality, preservative-free products for customers. Inspired by the warmth and nostalgia of a grandmother's home ("Aazol" in Marathi), the brand celebrates forgotten regional recipes and wholesome ingredients. Today, Aazol continues to connect rural producers with urban markets, promoting traditional food, women's empowerment, and sustainable growth
