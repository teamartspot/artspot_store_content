From a small trading shop in the 1940s to tables across more than 80 countries, the journey of LT Foods is a story of vision, integrity, and enduring excellence. What began with the Arora family’s deep connection to farmers and fair trade practices evolved into a global legacy nurturing the finest Basmati rice from the fields of Punjab to the world.
Guided by generations of leadership, LT Foods transformed traditional grain trading into a modern “Farm to Fork” philosophy where transparency, sustainability, and innovation shape every harvest. With the creation of iconic brands like DAAWAT® and Royal®, the company redefined premium rice experiences, offering perfectly aged grains, exceptional aroma, and uncompromising consistency.
Today, LT Foods stands as a global food enterprise rooted in heritage yet driven by innovation, championing organic nutrition, responsible sourcing, and community growth. More than a business, it is a promise to nurture a world of goodness, grain by grain, generation after generation.
