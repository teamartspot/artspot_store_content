In the fertile land of Punjab, where the aroma of basmati rice has long defined tradition, Chaman Lal Setia Exports Limited (CLSEL) began its journey in 1974 with a vision rooted in integrity and quality. What started as a modest effort steadily evolved into a globally recognized name, known for crafting premium rice varieties that carry both flavor and heritage. What makes its story unique is its unwavering balance honoring age-old practices while embracing modern processing and global standards. Each grain reflects careful sourcing, precision milling, and a deep understanding of what quality truly means. Over the years, it has carried the essence of Indian basmati from local fields to international tables, building trust across borders. It is not just about exporting rice, but about preserving a legacy that continues to grow with time. In every serving, CLSEL offers a quiet blend of tradition, craftsmanship, and global vision.
