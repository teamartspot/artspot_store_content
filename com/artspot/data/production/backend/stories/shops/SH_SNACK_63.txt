Lijjat began its inspiring journey on 15 March 1959, when seven women in Girgaum, Mumbai, started making papads with a borrowed capital of just ₹80. Founded as Shri Mahila Griha Udyog Lijjat Papad, the cooperative was created to provide women with dignified self-employment and financial independence. Built on the principles of equality, self-reliance, and collective ownership, every member is treated as a partner in the organization. Over the decades, Lijjat has grown into one of India's most respected women's cooperatives, producing papads, spices, flour, bakery products, and other food items while empowering thousands of women across the country. Today, Lijjat is a symbol of trust, quality, and women's entrepreneurship, with its products reaching consumers in India and many international markets.
