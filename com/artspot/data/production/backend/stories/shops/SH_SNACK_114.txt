Kokan Meva was founded with a vision of bringing the authentic flavors of the Konkan region to customers across India through premium, traditionally prepared food products. Inspired by the rich culinary heritage of coastal Maharashtra, the brand offers specialties such as Alphonso mango products, cashews, kokum, jackfruit delicacies, spices, and homemade snacks. By sourcing quality ingredients directly from Konkan and following traditional recipes, Kokan Meva preserves the natural taste and freshness of every product. The company combines regional craftsmanship with hygienic processing and modern packaging to make Konkan's delicacies easily accessible. Today, Kokan Meva continues to celebrate the region's food culture while supporting local producers and delivering authentic Konkan flavors to homes across the country.
