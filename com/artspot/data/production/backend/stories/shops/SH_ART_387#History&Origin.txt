Rogan painting in Nirona dates back over 300 years, brought to Kutch from Persia. 
It involves freehand painting using a stylus and thick castor oil paste. 
The Khatri family, including Arab Hasam Khatri, has preserved this tradition across generations. 
Their artworks often feature the famous ‘Tree of Life’ motif. 
In recent decades, the family has received national awards and global recognition for sustaining Rogan art.
