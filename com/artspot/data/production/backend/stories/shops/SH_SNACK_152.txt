HOCCO was launched in 2023 by the Chona family, founders of Havmor Ice Cream, after returning to the ice cream business following the completion of a non-compete period. The brand, whose name stands for House of Chona's Collaborative, was created to blend the family's decades of ice cream expertise with a fresh, modern approach to flavors and experiences. Building on a legacy that dates back to 1944, HOCCO focuses on crafting premium ice creams using high-quality ingredients while staying true to its values of goodness, honesty, and purity. The company has rapidly expanded across India with innovative products, ice cream parlours, and a strong retail presence. Today, HOCCO continues to redefine the ice cream experience by combining tradition, innovation, and customer delight in every scoop.
