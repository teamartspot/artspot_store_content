Tower Masale, manufactured by Ansari Masala Industries, traces its origins back to 1952, when the company was founded with the vision of producing pure, authentic, and high-quality Indian spices. Starting as a regional spice manufacturer in Dhule, Maharashtra, it built its reputation on carefully selected ingredients, traditional spice blends, and consistent quality. Over the decades, the company expanded its portfolio to include a wide range of ground spices and blended masalas catering to households and the food industry. Today, Tower Masale continues its journey by combining over seven decades of expertise with modern manufacturing practices, delivering trusted flavors while preserving the rich culinary heritage of Indian cuisine.
