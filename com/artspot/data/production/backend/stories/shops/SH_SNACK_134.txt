Kohinoor Mill has a legacy built on decades of excellence, beginning its journey in 1948 as one of the oldest integrated textile manufacturers in the region. From a single manufacturing facility, it has grown into a large-scale operation producing over 100 million meters of fabric annually for leading global fashion brands. The company's growth has been driven by continuous investment in modern technology, skilled craftsmanship, and innovation. Sustainability, ethical manufacturing, and transparency remain central to its operations, reflecting its commitment to responsible textile production. Today, Kohinoor Mill continues to combine its rich heritage with environmentally conscious practices to create high-quality textiles for customers worldwide.
