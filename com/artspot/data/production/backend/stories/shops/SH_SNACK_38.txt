Swaraj Spice Processing Industries was established in 2016 in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, with the vision of delivering pure, high-quality Indian spices to customers in India and abroad. Founded under the leadership of Mr. Jeta Rathod, the company began as a manufacturer, exporter, and wholesaler of whole and ground spices, focusing on authenticity, hygiene, and consistent quality. By sourcing premium raw materials and adopting modern spice processing techniques, it has built a reputation for freshness and reliability. Over the years, the company has expanded its product range while maintaining a strong commitment to customer satisfaction and food safety, growing into a trusted name in the spice processing industry.
