Born in the quiet rhythm of the hills, Darjeeling Sips captures the soul of Darjeeling in every cup. Rooted in time-honored tea traditions and shaped by generations of careful selection, each sip reflects the mist, the altitude, and the elegance of the gardens it comes from.


Unrushed and refined, Darjeeling Sips is an invitation to slow down—to experience tea not as a routine, but as a moment of heritage, calm, and understated luxury.
