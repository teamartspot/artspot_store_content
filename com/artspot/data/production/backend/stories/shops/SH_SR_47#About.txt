At Parampara, we are dedicated to celebrating and reviving the timeless elegance of traditional Indian weaves, with a special focus on the iconic Kunbi sari—a symbol of Goa’s indigenous artistry and cultural heritage. Our mission is to bring these handcrafted textiles to both local and global platforms, offering authentic, sustainable, and organically made saris that carry a rich story in every thread.

Our Kunbi saris are thoughtfully crafted using natural, skin-friendly dyes derived from local ingredients such as indigo, manjishtha, iron oxide, and rice water. Woven in earth-toned hues with subtle borders and pallus, each sari is a testament to Goa’s traditional weaving legacy. We ensure every piece is crafted with precision by a team of skilled artisans, designers, and weavers, with zero environmental impact—using no electricity or water in the weaving process.
