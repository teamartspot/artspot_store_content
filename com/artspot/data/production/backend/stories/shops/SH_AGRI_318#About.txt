ADG Agrotech began its journey in 2012 in West Bengal with a focus on agriculture and allied food processing, building itself around the region’s rich rice-growing heritage. The company evolved into a producer and supplier of premium rice varieties, emphasizing quality processing, consistency, and authentic regional grain offerings. Rooted in agricultural expertise, the business works to connect traditional farming produce with wider domestic and commercial markets. Their journey reflects a blend of modern agri-processing and commitment to delivering trusted staple food products from Bengal’s farming ecosystem.
