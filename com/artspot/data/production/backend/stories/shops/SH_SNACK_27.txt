PAKALE-Ji Ramdev Namak was established in 2015 in Dhule, Maharashtra, with the vision of delivering pure, hygienic, and authentic Indian namkeen and salt-based products. From the beginning, the company focused on using premium ingredients, RO-purified water, oil-free processing, and modern packaging technology to ensure consistent quality and food safety. Built on the philosophy "My Nation, My Salt," the brand has steadily expanded its range of traditional snacks while earning customer trust through FSSAI and MSME-certified manufacturing practices. Today, PAKALE-Ji continues its journey by combining traditional Indian flavors with modern production standards, serving customers across India with a commitment to purity, taste, and reliability.
