RmKV is a name synonymous with innovation, elegance, and excellence in the world of silk sarees and family apparel. With a rich legacy spanning 100 years, RmKV has earned national recognition for its exceptional craftsmanship, pioneering designs, and commitment to quality. Our flagship showrooms in Chennai, Tirunelveli, Coimbatore, and Bengaluru offer an extensive collection of wedding silks, theme-based sarees, embroidered collections, and ready-to-wear garments for the entire family.

The RmKV Design Studio is at the forefront of creativity, having introduced several iconic innovations such as the 50,000-Colour Saree, Grand Reversible Saree, Durbar Krishna, Kural Ovium, and the Natural Silk and Lino Light-Silk collections. Customers can even design their own personalized silk sarees in a palette of over 50,000 hues, handwoven by RmKV’s master artisans.

In addition to sarees, RmKV showcases a wide selection of salwar kameez, men’s ethnic wear, children’s apparel, and other festive and casual attire — making it a complete family shopping destination.
