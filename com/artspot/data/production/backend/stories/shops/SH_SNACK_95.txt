Kayani Bakery was founded in 1955 by the Kayani brothers—Khodayar, Hormazdiar, and Rustom Kayani after they migrated from Iran to Pune, Maharashtra. Built on the rich traditions of Irani baking, the family established a bakery that quickly became famous for its freshly baked breads, cakes, and handcrafted biscuits. The brand earned legendary status through its iconic Shrewsbury Biscuits and Mawa Cakes, which have remained customer favorites for generations. Staying true to its heritage, Kayani Bakery continues to operate from its single outlet on East Street, Camp, Pune, preserving its original recipes, quality, and old-world charm. Today, it stands as one of Pune's most iconic bakeries, celebrated for over seven decades of baking excellence and timeless taste.
