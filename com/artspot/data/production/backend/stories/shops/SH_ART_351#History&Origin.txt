Wamda Wood Carving Shop the shop is part of Ladakh’s souvenir and craft circuit on Main Bazaar. Its offerings reflect the Buddhist-influenced woodcraft tradition of Ladakh, blending Tibetan and local motifs. Wamda Wood Carving Shop is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
