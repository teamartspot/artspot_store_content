The journey of IndianVillez began with a simple search in the USA for an authentic handloom saree—one that led us back to rural Odisha. There, we met Tosh Babu, a master of the Ikat weaving tradition, who despite his talent, was earning a meager living. With collaboration and trust, we helped him transition to creating high-quality handloom sarees that astonished even him. That first masterpiece sparked a movement. Today, Tosh Babu leads a team of karigars and earns a dignified income, and IndianVillez has grown to empower over 5000 artisan families and 50+ master weavers across India. Rooted in the ethos of “Crafted in India, Crafting for the World,” IndianVillez is not just a brand—it’s a revival of heritage, a source of livelihoods, and a celebration of India’s living traditions.
