In the serene tea valleys of Palampur, where Kangra’s legacy quietly endures, Green Buds™ Tea has been crafting its story since 1927. What began as one man’s devotion to tea has grown into a legacy of precision, passion, and enduring craftsmanship.
Rooted in its own gardens and nurtured through generations, every leaf is carefully selected, delicately processed, and refined through the discerning ritual of tasting where expertise meets intuition, cup after cup. Here, tradition is not hurried; it is honoured, perfected, and passed on.
From the freshness of green buds to the elegance of every finished blend, each cup reflects a balance of heritage and refinement crafted to meet global standards, yet deeply anchored in its origin.
Green Buds™ is more than tea it is a century-old promise, gently brewed into every sip, where consistency, character, and quiet luxury come together.
