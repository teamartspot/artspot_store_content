Prakash Masale began its journey in the 1940s, when Mr. Gyanoba Jadhav and his wife started preparing traditional spice blends at home and selling them from a small stall at Nehru Chowk (Mandai), Pune. Their signature Kanda Lasoon Masala quickly became a favorite in Maharashtrian households, laying the foundation for the brand. In the 1970s, the second generation, led by Mr. Dattatray Jadhav, expanded the product range and introduced modern spice-processing machinery in 1985, along with a wider retail and distribution network. Today, the third generation, led by Mr. Pavan Jadhav, continues the legacy by blending traditional recipes with state-of-the-art manufacturing while preserving the authentic flavors of Maharashtra. With over eight decades of heritage, Prakash Masale remains a trusted name, known for its natural, preservative-free spices and rich Maharashtrian culinary tradition.
