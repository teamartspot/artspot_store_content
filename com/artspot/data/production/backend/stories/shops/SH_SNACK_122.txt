Bhadang.com was launched by Madhumitra, a company established in 2005, with the vision of taking the iconic Sangli–Kolhapur Bhadang to snack lovers across India. Starting with retail stores in Pune, Sangli, and Kolhapur, the company brought together renowned local Bhadang brands on a single platform while preserving their authentic taste. Inspired by the rich culinary heritage of South Maharashtra, Bhadang.com celebrates the spicy puffed-rice snack that has become a regional specialty loved for generations. The platform is committed to offering genuine products made using traditional recipes, quality ingredients, and trusted local brands. Today, Bhadang.com continues to promote the legacy of Sangli's famous Bhadang by making this authentic Maharashtrian snack accessible to customers nationwide.
