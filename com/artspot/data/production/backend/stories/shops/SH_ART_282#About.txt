Rameshwar Singh Wooden Toys Rameshwar Singh Wooden Toys is a Varanasi workshop and retail name associated with traditional wooden toy-making — producing spinning tops (lattoo), small animals, dolls and utensils carved from mango, mahua and eucalyptus wood. Online listings and profiles show contact details and local addresses; the maker is known for keeping sustainable toy-making traditions alive and supplying festival and retail markets. Rameshwar Singh Wooden Toys is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
