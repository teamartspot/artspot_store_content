The legacy of Authentic Kashmir is deeply entwined with the centuries-old traditions of the region’s skilled craftsmen. Inspired by generations of artisans who have passed down their knowledge through time, the brand emerged as a tribute to Kashmir’s storied cultural and artistic heritage.

Built on the foundation of trust, quality, and authenticity, Authentic Kashmir was established to provide an avenue for genuine Kashmiri art to reach the world. Each creation is a reflection of ancestral techniques—from handwoven Pashmina and Kani shawls to delicately embroidered garments—that have defined the region’s identity for generations. Our journey continues to honor and revive these traditions, making them accessible to those who value the beauty of handmade, heritage-rich products.
