Prabhat Dairy began its journey in 1998 after becoming part of the Nirmal family, with the vision of building a world-class integrated dairy business in Maharashtra. Starting with the sale of fresh milk under the Prabhat brand in 1999, the company steadily expanded into value-added dairy products such as cheese, milk powder, paneer, ghee, yogurt, and flavored milk. Continuous investments in modern processing technology, quality certifications, and strong farmer partnerships helped it become one of India's leading dairy brands. Today, as part of the Lactalis Group, Prabhat Dairy continues its journey of delivering premium dairy products while maintaining its commitment to quality, innovation, and sustainable dairy development.
