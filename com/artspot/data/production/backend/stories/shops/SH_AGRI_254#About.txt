In the bustling market yards of Mangalvedha, where grains move as a rhythm of daily life, Mali and Company began its journey decades ago with a simple promise of quality that people can rely on. Established around 1980, it grew steadily as a trusted wholesaler of organic grains, flours, and everyday essentials. What makes its story unique is its grounded presence serving generations of customers with consistency rather than complexity. From jowar and wheat to staple groceries, each product reflects the richness of local agriculture and careful sourcing. Over the years, it has become more than a store, evolving into a dependable hub where farmers’ produce meets community needs. Rooted in simplicity and service, its journey is shaped by everyday trust built over time. In every grain it offers, Mali and Company carries a quiet legacy of reliability, nourishing homes one purchase at a time
