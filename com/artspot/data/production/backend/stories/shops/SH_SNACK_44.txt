The Kolhapur Zilla Sahakari Dudh Utpadak Sangh Ltd., popularly known as Gokul Milk, was established on 16 March 1963 as a cooperative dairy with the mission of improving the livelihood of milk producers in the Kolhapur region. Starting with the collection of just 700 litres of milk per day, it has grown into one of Maharashtra's largest dairy cooperatives. Today, Gokul operates a modern dairy with a processing capacity of 17 lakh litres per day, supported by satellite dairies, chilling centres, and an extensive procurement network. The cooperative is committed to providing fair returns to farmers while producing high-quality milk and dairy products using modern technology and strict quality standards. With decades of trust, Gokul continues to strengthen rural communities and deliver nutritious dairy products to consumers across India.
