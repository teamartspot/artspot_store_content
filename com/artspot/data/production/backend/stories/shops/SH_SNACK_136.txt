Shahee Namkeen's journey began with the dream of two retired servicemen, Mr. Vinayak Gadre and Mr. Nandkumar Kulkarni, who entered the namkeen business in 2006 by founding Shree Ganesh Foods after gaining industry experience. Starting from a small 700 sq. ft. facility with just seven employees, the business steadily expanded through continuous improvements in production and distribution. In January 2021, the next generation—Mr. Vaibhav Kulkarni and Mr. Sanket Gadre—launched Shahee Namkeen as a dedicated manufacturing unit, combining modern technology with traditional recipes. The company focuses on producing hygienic, high-quality farsan and namkeen at affordable prices while preserving authentic taste. Today, Shahee Namkeen is a recognized food brand in Maharashtra, committed to innovation, customer satisfaction, and steady growth.
