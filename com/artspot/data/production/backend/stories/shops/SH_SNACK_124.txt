Rajhans Namkeen began its journey in 1985 with a vision of offering pure, satvik, and delicious fasting (upwas) snacks to consumers across Maharashtra. Built on the belief that food is a form of service, the brand is dedicated to preparing products that combine authentic taste, purity, and spiritual satisfaction. Starting with a range of potato wafers and farali snacks, Rajhans has grown into one of Maharashtra's trusted names in ready-to-eat upwas products. By combining traditional recipes with hygienic manufacturing, continuous innovation, and strict quality standards, the company has earned the trust of generations of customers. Today, Rajhans Namkeen continues its mission of becoming Maharashtra's leading brand for fasting foods while delivering quality, trust, and authentic flavors in every pack.
