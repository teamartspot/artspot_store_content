In the millet-rich lands of Solapur, where forgotten grains quietly waited for their return, All Sow Great began as a heartfelt mission by siblings Shantanu and Rutuja Patil to bring millets back into everyday life. What makes their story unique is curiosity turned into purpose, questioning modern food habits and rediscovering the power of ancient grains like jowar and ragi. Guided by deep agricultural knowledge and nutritional insight, they transformed these humble crops into modern, wholesome foods that fit today’s lifestyle. Each product carries the richness of fiber, protein, and natural goodness, crafted to make healthy eating both easy and enjoyable. Rooted in sustainability and local sourcing, their journey supports farmers while reviving a grain once overlooked. It is not just about millet snacks, but about changing how people see food, simple, honest, and nourishing. In every bite, All Sow Great brings back a grain that was always great, just waiting to be remembered.
