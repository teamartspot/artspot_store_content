Amounee was born from a personal journey—a bridge between the founder’s life as a textile designer and her immersion in rural craft communities across India. Witnessing firsthand the quiet brilliance of artisans and the hardships they endure, the idea emerged: what if these makers could access global markets, share their stories, and thrive?

The name Amounee reflects this mission—uplifting the voices of unseen artisans and placing their work on a path to visibility and value. Drawing from the Gurmukhi phrase Amounee (“beyond silence”), the brand stands as an advocate for recognition, empowerment, and conscious commerce.

Since its founding, Amounee has partnered with over 75 craft clusters and impacted 2,500+ artisans—many of whom have become first-time digital entrepreneurs. From buying their first computers to sending children to college, these achievements are the true milestones of our journey.

At its heart, Amounee is about dignity through design. About craftsmanship over consumption. And about walking a shared path—where every thread tells a story worth hearing.
