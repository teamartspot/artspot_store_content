Seerat was founded by Neha Mohanty, a former judicial officer turned handloom advocate. A law graduate (B.B.A. LLB) from Bhubaneswar, Odisha, Neha chose to leave her legal career behind to follow her passion for Indian handlooms and the artisans behind them.

Inspired by her love for handmade textiles and a desire to promote sustainable livelihoods, she launched Seerat to give voice and visibility to rural weavers and traditional craft communities. From Odisha’s own weaving clusters to artisan hubs across India, Seerat has built long-standing partnerships grounded in trust, creativity, and shared values.

Rooted in heritage but inspired by innovation, Seerat has grown into a trusted name in handcrafted elegance — making meaningful fashion accessible, ethical, and always exquisite.
