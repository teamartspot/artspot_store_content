Kalanjali Emporium was founded in 1992 under the umbrella of the Margahita Group, which has diverse business interests including media, hospitality, food processing, and retail. Established with a mission to revive and preserve India’s vanishing crafts, Kalanjali has evolved from a single showroom into a cultural institution.

What began as a tribute to Indian craftsmanship quickly became a movement. Our team travels across India to connect with master artisans and heritage clusters—ensuring fair sourcing and bringing their artistry to a wider audience. For over three decades, we’ve played a pivotal role in promoting sustainable livelihoods for traditional craftspeople while providing customers with access to authentic Indian artistry.

With a firm foundation in tradition and an eye toward global reach, Kalanjali Emporium continues to uphold its commitment to celebrating beauty, empowering artisans, and preserving India’s timeless legacies.
