Founded in 2011, Brand Mandir emerged as a contemporary offshoot of Sai Silks Kalamandir Ltd., with a vision to celebrate and preserve India's diverse weaving traditions. The brand quickly grew into a trusted name, combining traditional craftsmanship with modern sensibilities.

Brand Mandir’s journey is deeply tied to the heritage of Indian textiles. By sourcing directly from local artisans and weaving clusters, it supports generational artistry and brings authentic regional styles—like Kanjivaram, Banarasi, Chanderi, Patola, and more—to a nationwide audience.

Through its showrooms and online platform, Brand Mandir continues to bridge the gap between tradition and trend, delivering curated collections that honor heritage while embracing evolving fashion narratives.
