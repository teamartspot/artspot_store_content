Kaka Pedhewale's story began as a family tradition in Wadhe, Satara, where handcrafted Kandi Pedhas were made using fresh cow's milk and traditional recipes. For decades, travelers on the Mumbai–Bengaluru (NH-48) highway have stopped at the shop to enjoy its authentic sweets. Over the years, the small family-run business has become a well-known destination for sweet lovers across Maharashtra. Every Pedha is still prepared by skilled halwais in heavy iron kadais, preserving its original taste and quality. Today, Kaka Pedhewale continues to blend heritage with modern convenience by delivering its traditional sweets across India while maintaining its commitment to purity and authenticity.
