The Kantha Store emerged from a desire to revive and market Kantha embroidery, a centuries‑old tradition from Bengal. By partnering with women’s self‑help groups and rural kantha artisans, the initiative created reliable market channels for hand‑stitched quilts and garments. Over time the brand expanded its catalogue, introduced curated collections and began participating in craft exhibitions and online marketplaces. The Kantha Store’s history is rooted in sustainable craft livelihoods—ensuring stitchers receive fair compensation while preserving unique regional motifs and techniques.
