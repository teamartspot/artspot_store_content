Patola by Prakash Bhai is a distinguished name in the world of handmade Patola sarees, proudly continuing a tradition that began in 1974 in Gujarat. For over five decades, we have been dedicated to preserving and promoting the exquisite craft of Patola weaving — a symbol of luxury, tradition, and fine artistry.

Crafted from pure Mulberry silk, each Patola saree we produce is a reflection of regal elegance and meticulous craftsmanship. Our artisans, who have inherited this intricate art through generations, bring to life the timeless beauty of Patola with every thread. Every motif and pattern is created with care, telling a story rooted in India's rich cultural heritage.

At Patola by Prakash Bhai, we honor the art form not only through our products but also through our passion — one that has been nurtured for over 77 years and continues to thrive in every weave.
