In the lush heartlands of Kerala, where rice is more than food, it is culture, memory, and everyday ritual. VMR Kuthira carries forward a legacy that began in 1965 with a humble village rice mill.
What started as a traditional craft has, over generations, evolved into a refined expression of quality where time-honoured milling meets modern precision, and every grain is treated with care from field to table.
Sourced directly from trusted farmers and shaped by the rich soils of Kerala, each variety reflects authenticity, retaining its natural aroma, nutrition, and the unmistakable taste of home.
At its heart, VMR Kuthira is a family legacy built on trust, integrity, and a quiet dedication to excellence where every grain tells a story of heritage, and every meal becomes a celebration of purity.
VMR Kuthira is more than rice; it is the essence of tradition, refined into a timeless luxury for the modern table.
