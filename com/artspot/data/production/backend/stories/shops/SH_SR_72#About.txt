At The Phulkari, we are passionate manufacturers of authentic Phulkari embroidery, blending tradition, craftsmanship, and innovation. Since our inception in 2016 in Amritsar, Punjab, we’ve been committed to reviving and reimagining one of India’s most treasured folk art forms. Each of our creations fuses vibrant colors, intricate stitches, and timeless motifs to celebrate the heritage of Punjab in a contemporary light.

Our collections are more than textiles—they’re woven stories. Inspired by nature, life, and divine symbolism, the flowers, birds, and geometric patterns in our designs speak of tradition, resilience, and beauty. Through every thread, we strive to carry forward a cultural legacy that connects generations and captivates hearts.
