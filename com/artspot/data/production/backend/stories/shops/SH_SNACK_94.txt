Kaka Halwai traces its rich heritage back to 1892, when Late Mr. Morappasheth Gadve (Kaka) founded a small sweet shop in Pune, Maharashtra, dedicated to authentic taste and traditional recipes. In 1977, the fifth generation of the Gadve family established Kaka Halwai Sweet Center (KHSC) on Tilak Road, giving the brand a new identity while preserving its legacy. Guided by the values of quality, hygiene, integrity, and innovation, the brand expanded steadily through franchises and modern manufacturing facilities. Today, with multiple outlets across Pune and surrounding cities, Kaka Halwai continues to prepare sweets and namkeen using premium ingredients, rigorous quality standards, and a strict no palm oil policy. For over 130 years, the brand has remained a symbol of trust, tradition, and authentic Maharashtrian flavors, delighting generations of customers.
