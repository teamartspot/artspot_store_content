Sri Sri Samaguri Satra in Majuli, Assam, is a famous monastery known for its mask-making tradition. 
The satra’s artisans produce masks depicting characters from epics like the Ramayana and Mahabharata. 
The satra functions as both a religious and cultural hub. 
It is frequently visited by scholars and tourists exploring Assam’s living traditions. 
Its mask-making tradition has been recognized by UNESCO as part of intangible heritage documentation efforts.
