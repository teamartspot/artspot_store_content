Jain Farm Fresh traces its origins to 1963, when Dr. Bhavarlal H. Jain laid the foundation of the Jain Group with a vision of transforming Indian agriculture through innovation and sustainable farming. Building on strong partnerships with farmers, the company expanded into food processing in 1994, pioneering the production of fruit purees, dehydrated vegetables, spices, and frozen foods using world-class technology. Over the years, Jain Farm Fresh has grown into a global food processing leader, exporting premium products to more than 120 countries while remaining committed to quality, food safety, and innovation. At the heart of its journey is the mission to empower farmers through fair pricing, advanced agricultural practices, and sustainable development, carrying forward its founder's philosophy of "Leave this world better than you found it."
