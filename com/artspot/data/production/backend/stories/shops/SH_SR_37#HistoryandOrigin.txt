The idea for Santiniketan was born out of a deep concern for the declining recognition and support for Bengal’s rural artisans. While the legacy of textile arts like Kantha stitching and Tussar weaving continues to thrive in talent, the artisans behind these crafts have struggled for fair compensation and visibility.

Founded as a digital platform rooted in Santiniketan’s cultural ethos, Santiniketan emerged to preserve this rich artistic tradition while ensuring sustainable livelihoods for creators. What began as a humble initiative to uplift local weavers has evolved into a trusted destination for authentic Bengal handlooms, reaching homes across the country and beyond.
