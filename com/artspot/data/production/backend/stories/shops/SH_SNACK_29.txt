Ugam Foods was established in 2023 in Dhule, Maharashtra, by Mr. Durgesh Sakriya with the vision of offering a wide range of high-quality traditional Indian snacks and namkeens. Backed by the Sakariya Group's two decades of experience in the food industry, the company combines rich culinary heritage with modern manufacturing and stringent quality standards. Starting with classic favorites such as chivda, mixtures, sev, chips, and other regional snacks, Ugam Foods has focused on delivering authentic taste, freshness, and consistency. Their journey reflects a blend of tradition and innovation, aiming to spread joy through flavorful snacks while building a trusted brand across India
