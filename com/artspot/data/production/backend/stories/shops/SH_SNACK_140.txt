Kokan Raja was founded with the vision of bringing the authentic flavors of the Konkan region to people across India through naturally processed fruit products and traditional delicacies. Inspired by the rich agricultural heritage of Konkan, the brand sources fresh local produce such as Alphonso mangoes, kokum, cashews, and other seasonal fruits. It combines time-tested recipes with modern processing techniques to preserve natural taste, quality, and freshness. The company remains committed to supporting local farmers while promoting Konkan's culinary traditions. Today, Kokan Raja continues to expand its range of premium products, delivering the true essence of Konkan to customers nationwide.
