In Assam, where the air is heavy with rain, and the soil carries generations of cultivation, tea has always been more than a crop; it is a way of life.
At Samiran Tea, this relationship with the land is kept deliberately simple. From the very beginning, the gardens have followed a fully organic path free from chemicals, guided instead by natural processes that allow the leaf to develop its true flavour and character.
Each harvest is handpicked and shaped with care, resulting in a range that moves from strong CTC brews to more delicate orthodox, green, and specialty teas, each reflecting a different side of Assam’s richness.
What makes it distinctive is not just what is produced, but how. The focus remains on preserving the balance between soil, plant, and people, ensuring that every cup carries both purity and intention.
There is nothing rushed here, only tea that grows, just as it should.
