Khurja Ceramic The Khurja ceramic industry has roots going back centuries, but its modern manufacturing and wholesale infrastructure expanded rapidly during the 20th century. Clusters of small units and larger factories in Khurja specialised in glazed and unglazed ceramics, growing into an ODOP/cluster identity that supplies domestic and export markets. Online portals and supplier directories now help Khurja manufacturers reach a national customer base. Khurja Ceramic is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
