Jagdish Lal Raj Soni From the Raj Soni community in Pratapgarh, Jagdish Lal Raj Soni continues the centuries-old practice of Thewa, which melds enamel painting and gold filigree. The craft is transmitted across generations, and masters like Jagdish Lal have been documented in craft histories and awards listings for their contribution to sustaining Thewa art. Jagdish Lal Raj Soni is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
