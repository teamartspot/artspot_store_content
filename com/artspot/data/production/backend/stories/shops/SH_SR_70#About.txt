Vasumatis is more than just a destination for timeless sarees — it is a movement to uplift the artisans behind India’s cherished weaving traditions. Rooted in values of ethical sourcing, empowerment, and cultural preservation, we directly collaborate with over 1500 weavers from villages across India. Our goal is to ensure fair compensation, promote sustainable livelihoods, and bring the finest handwoven sarees to your wardrobe.

From the intricate elegance of Kanjivaram and Banarasi silks to the vivid artistry of Paithani, Patola, and digitally printed satin sarees, every piece in our collection is a tribute to craftsmanship and heritage. At Vasumatis, we don’t just sell sarees — we share stories, celebrate artistry, and invite you to be a part of a conscious, tradition-rich journey.
