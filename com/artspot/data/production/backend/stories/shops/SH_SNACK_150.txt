Vadilal Ice Creams began its journey in 1907, when Vadilal Gandhi handcrafted ice cream using a traditional hand-cranked machine, laying the foundation for one of India's oldest ice cream brands. In 1926, the company opened its first ice cream parlour and introduced home delivery, making its products more accessible to customers. Over the decades, Vadilal grew through continuous innovation, launching iconic products like Cassatta, India's first fully automated ice cream candy line, and expanding its presence across the country and later to 26+ countries. The brand has consistently combined traditional values with modern technology, earning a reputation for quality, hygiene, and innovation. Today, Vadilal continues its century-long legacy of spreading happiness through premium ice creams, living up to its promise of "Har Dil Bole Waah Vadilal."
