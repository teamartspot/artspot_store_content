Havmor Ice Cream's journey began in 1944, when Satish Chona started making handcrafted ice cream in Karachi under the name "Have More." After the Partition of India, he rebuilt the business from scratch, eventually establishing the first Havmor outlet in Ahmedabad and earning customers' trust through his guiding principles of quality, honesty, and hygiene. Over the decades, the brand expanded by introducing innovative flavors and premium ice cream products, becoming one of India's most loved ice cream brands. In 2017, Havmor became part of LOTTE Wellfood Co. Ltd., combining its rich heritage with global expertise while continuing to preserve its original commitment to quality and innovation. Today, Havmor has a nationwide presence with thousands of retail outlets and flagship stores, delighting millions of customers with its wide range of ice creams.
