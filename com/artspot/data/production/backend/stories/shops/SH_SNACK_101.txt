Joshi Sweets was founded by Arvind Joshi, whose passion for authentic Indian sweets began with preparing pedhas at home and supplying them to established sweet shops in Pune. Encouraged by the positive response, he opened a small 4×4 roadside stall near Alankar Police Chowki, Kothrud, in 1992, laying the foundation for the brand. Built on an unwavering commitment to quality, freshness, and traditional recipes, the business steadily expanded from a tiny stall into one of Pune's most trusted sweet and farsan brands. Over the years, Joshi Sweets has grown to multiple outlets while preserving the authentic taste and values that earned generations of customer trust. Today, the brand continues to sweeten celebrations with handcrafted Indian sweets, snacks, and a legacy of tradition and excellence.
