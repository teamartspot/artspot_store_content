Venee Art Sculptures Mamallapuram has long been a centre for stone carving and sculpture; businesses like Venee Art grew in that context to cater to both local temple repairs and export commissions. Local trade directories and supplier profiles document the studio’s location and specialty in carved stone works. Venee Art Sculptures is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
