Palekar Food Products was founded in 1939 by Dadasaheb Palekar in Satara, Maharashtra, with a vision of delivering fresh, high-quality bakery products to local families. From its humble beginnings, the company pioneered bakery products in Satara and nearby regions, earning the trust of generations through homemade recipes and a passion for quality. Under the leadership of Shri Govindrao Palekar and now Shri Neelkanth Palekar, the brand has expanded its range to include breads, cakes, cookies, khari, pastries, rusks, and other bakery specialties. By blending traditional recipes with state-of-the-art manufacturing technology, Palekar has preserved its authentic taste while embracing innovation. Today, with over 85 years of heritage, Palekar Food Products continues to be a trusted bakery brand, bringing the warmth of tradition and quality to customers across Maharashtra.
