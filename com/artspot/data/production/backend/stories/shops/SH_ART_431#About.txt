Pragjyotika – Assam Emporium is a government-supported craft outlet in Guwahati. 
It displays Assam’s muga silk, eri textiles, bamboo products and bell metal crafts. 
The emporium provides a single-window shopping experience for authentic crafts. 
It participates in state-backed exhibitions and promotional campaigns. 
Pragjyotika is a flagship brand for Assamese cultural heritage retailing.
