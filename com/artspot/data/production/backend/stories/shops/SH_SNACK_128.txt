Malas Fruit Products was founded in 1958 by Taiyab Asgarali Mala in Mahabaleshwar, Maharashtra, with a vision of transforming the region's abundant fresh fruits into premium processed food products. Beginning with fruit crushes and syrups, the company pioneered fruit processing in the hill station while working closely with local farmers to promote sustainable fruit cultivation. Over the decades, Malas has expanded its portfolio to include crushes, syrups, jams, spreads, confectionery, bakery ingredients, honey, and food-service products, all crafted to retain the natural taste and quality of fruits. Combining decades of expertise with modern manufacturing and stringent quality standards, the brand has earned the trust of consumers across India. Today, Malas continues its fruitful journey by delivering nature-inspired products that celebrate freshness, innovation, and authentic fruit goodness.
