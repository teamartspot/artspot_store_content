Apsara Ice Creams began its journey in 1971 with a small outlet in Walkeshwar, Mumbai, where Nemchand Shah handcrafted ice creams using fresh fruits, buffalo milk, and traditional wooden sanchas. For decades, the family focused on quality and catered to local customers, weddings, and events before the second generation expanded the business in 2014. By embracing a franchise model, Apsara rapidly grew from a single store to 125+ outlets across India while introducing innovative flavors such as Guava Glory and Pani Puri Patakha. The brand continues to combine traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing, using premium ingredients and no artificial preservatives. Today, Apsara Ice Creams is recognized as one of India's leading handcrafted ice cream brands, known for its creativity, quality, and rich legacy.
