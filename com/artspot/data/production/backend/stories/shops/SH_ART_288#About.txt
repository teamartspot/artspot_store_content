Naqash Carpets Naqash Carpets (Naqash Textiles) is a Srinagar-based brand producing Kashmir hand-embroidered chainstitch textiles, rugs and crewel products. Their online catalogue shows crewel fabrics, cushions and rugs, and they operate both a local showroom presence and e-commerce pages. Naqash emphasises traditional chainstitch and Kashmiri motifs in contemporary formats for interiors. Naqash Carpets is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
