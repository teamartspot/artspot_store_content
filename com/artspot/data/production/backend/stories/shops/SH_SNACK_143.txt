Pethe Foods traces its origins to the vision of Late Captain Yashvantji Pethe, who left his Army career to fulfill his childhood dream of building a business. Using his savings, he started Lokmanya Tea Store in Solapur, which soon became the city's most trusted tea shop. Driven by a desire to help daily wage workers, he introduced affordable homemade spices, pickles, and chutneys, laying the foundation for the Pethe Bandhu brand. The family, especially Late Nirmalatai Pethe, preserved authentic homemade recipes, while the next generation expanded the business into the modern Pethe Foods brand. Today, Pethe Foods offers more than 60 traditional products, including its world-famous Shengdana Chutney, continuing its legacy of authentic taste, quality, and customer trust.
