Sankul Foods began its journey in 1992 as Sankul Gruh Udyog, founded by Sanjay Vasantrao Kulkarni with the vision of preserving Maharashtra's traditional homemade recipes. What started as a small home-based enterprise gradually evolved into Sankul Foods Pvt. Ltd., offering a wide range of flours, ready-to-eat foods, laddoos, chutneys, spices, and fasting (upwas) products. Built on authentic recipes, quality ingredients, and hygienic manufacturing, the company has earned the trust of generations of customers. Today, Sankul Foods continues its journey by blending traditional Maharashtrian flavors with modern food processing, making regional specialties conveniently available across India.
