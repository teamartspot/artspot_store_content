Selakiya Sculpture Works Mahabalipuram’s sculptural trade traces back to Pallava stone carving traditions; workshops such as Selakiya combine that heritage with contemporary commissions for temples, public art and collectors. Company pages and local craft listings highlight the studio’s decades-long practice and export-oriented commissions. Selakiya Sculpture Works is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
