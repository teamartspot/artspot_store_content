Kutchh Mohini Art Emporium is a Bhuj-based handicraft store selling embroidered textiles, leatherwork and decorative craft. 
It appears in Kutch craft directories and tourism shopping guides. 
The emporium offers souvenirs and traditional handmade items. 
Products reflect the craft diversity of Bhuj, from mirror work to woodwork. 
It serves tourists, collectors and local customers with verified artisan goods.
