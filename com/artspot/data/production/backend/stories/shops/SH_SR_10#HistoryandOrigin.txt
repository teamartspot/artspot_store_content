Founded in March 1986 by Jaya Jaitly, Dastkari Haat Samiti emerged to champion traditional artisanship and ensure sustainable livelihoods through self-initiated, craft-focused programs.

In 1994, the organisation conceptualised and launched Dilli Haat in New Delhi—a permanent urban haat that blends crafts, culture, and commerce, later replicated in over 30 cities.

Over more than three decades, it has curated over 150 craft bazaars, pioneered intercultural exchanges (with countries like Vietnam and Iran), and introduced calligraphy, craft maps, and specialized exhibitions such as "The Indian Crafts Journey".

Educational outreach has been central to its mission, with workshops in schools and design assistance for marginalized artisans; its impactful projects include the Akshara Project and creation of illustrated craft maps for Google Arts & Culture.

In recent years, Dastkari Haat Samiti embraced digital transformation—launching a revamped website, establishing an online shopping platform, and publishing books on craft design and tradition.
