Mahakrushi Farms was established with the vision of creating a strong farmer-led agricultural enterprise that connects quality Indian produce with global markets. Rooted in Maharashtra’s farming ecosystem, the company specializes in premium export-quality fruits, particularly bananas and pomegranates, backed by a network of dedicated grower members. With a focus on quality assurance, compliance, and efficient supply chain management, Mahakrushi ensures freshness from farm to international buyers. Their journey reflects a commitment to empowering farmers, promoting sustainable agriculture, and building a trusted name in agricultural exports.
