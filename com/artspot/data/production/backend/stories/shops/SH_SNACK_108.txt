New Poona Bakery was established in 1959 in Pune, Maharashtra, with a vision of offering fresh, high-quality bakery products at affordable prices. What began as a single bakery has grown into one of Pune's most loved bakery chains, with 100+ outlets serving generations of customers. The brand is renowned for its freshly baked breads, cakes, pastries, cream rolls, puffs, khari, and its signature Veg Pattice, all prepared with a strong focus on quality and freshness. By blending traditional baking methods with modern production and consistent quality standards, New Poona Bakery has earned the trust of families across the region. Today, it continues to be a household name in Pune, carrying forward a legacy of taste, quality, and customer satisfaction spanning more than six decades.
