Katarni.in was founded by Agro Heritage Private Limited, an agri-startup established in 2021 under the Government of India’s RKVY-RAFTAAR program and incubated at Bihar Agricultural University, Sabour. Led by farmer-entrepreneur Subodh Choudhary, the initiative was created to revive and promote Bihar’s traditional and GI-tagged crops, especially the heritage Bhagalpuri Katarni Rice. Combining generations of farming knowledge with modern processing and branding, the company works directly with farmers to preserve indigenous seeds and sustainable cultivation practices. From promoting Katarni rice at national trade fairs to earning the Plant Genome Saviour Community Award from the President of India, their journey reflects a commitment to agricultural heritage, farmer empowerment, and bringing authentic, chemical-free grains to consumers across India and beyond.
