Rooted in decades of trading heritage, SVP Chillies was founded in 1988 by Sri K. Venkata Apparao, carrying forward a family legacy shaped by deep market wisdom and a passion for quality produce. Guided by the experience of generations, the brand grew steadily from local trade to a respected presence in national and global markets.
From the vibrant chilli markets of Andhra Pradesh, SVP carefully sources and delivers produce known for its bold color, rich flavor, and uncompromising quality. Every batch reflects the insight, dedication, and resilience that have defined the family’s journey for over half a century.
Today, with the next generation leading the way, SVP Chillies continues to honor its heritage—bringing the authentic heat and character of India’s finest chillies to kitchens and industries around the world.
