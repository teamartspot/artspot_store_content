In the mist-laden hills of Coorg, where coffee has quietly flourished for decades, Mercara Gold Estate stands as a story of passion transformed into craftsmanship. What began as a modest plantation in the 1960s evolved through vision, care, and relentless pursuit of perfection into a boutique estate where every bean is nurtured with intention.
Guided by a deep respect for the craft, the estate embraced time-honoured processes while refining them with precision from handpicked harvests to in-house wet processing and roasting. Here, coffee is not rushed; it is curated layer by layer to reveal balance, depth, and character in every cup.
By bringing the journey from bean to brew entirely within the estate, Mercara Gold redefines authenticity, eliminating distance, preserving freshness, and offering coffee as it was meant to be experienced.
Mercara Gold Estate is more than coffee; it is an immersion into heritage, passion, and the quiet luxury of a perfectly crafted cup.
