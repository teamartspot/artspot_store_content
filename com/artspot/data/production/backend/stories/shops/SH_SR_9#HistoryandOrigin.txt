Founded in 2006 in Thane, Maharashtra, M International Exports emerged as a focused exporter, manufacturer, and supplier of premium sarees—specializing in thread-work, georgette, and printed chiffon varieties.

From its inception, the company embraced a customer-centric philosophy, building a robust pan-India presence and earning trust across the industry by consistently delivering quality and timely service .

Over the years, M International Exports has established collaborations with skilled artisans and weavers, blending traditional craftsmanship with contemporary designs to satisfy evolving market trends.

The brand's reputation was further bolstered upon being recognized as a verified seller on TradeIndia, affirming its commitment to transparency, product quality, and ethical business practices .

M International Exports grew from a regional player into a trusted national supplier, handling bulk orders for bridal, partywear, and casual sarees with efficiency, diligence, and scalable logistics.

Today, the company continues to innovate—integrating fresh design concepts with its core strength in thread and chiffon craftsmanship, while expanding its reach in both domestic and international markets.

With two decades of dedication, M International Exports remains driven by a founding vision to uplift the spirit of Indian sarees through superior quality, artistry, and customer trust.
