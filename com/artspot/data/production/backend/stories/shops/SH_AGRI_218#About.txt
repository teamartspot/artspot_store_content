Vartoh Organic, the flagship brand of MEVEDIR, is dedicated to promoting eco-friendly organic produce from Sikkim. They connect with local organic farms to bring you authentic products. From the untouched hills of Sikkim, where the soil has never known chemicals, Vartoh Organic began as a quiet effort to carry the purity of the mountains into everyday kitchens. Rooted in one of India’s first fully organic states, it brings together indigenous grains, pulses, and spices grown through generations of traditional farming. What makes this journey unique is its deep connection with local farmers, especially women cultivators, whose hands nurture every harvest with care and patience. Each product reflects the rhythm of the Himalayas, slow-grown, naturally processed, and untouched by artificial methods. From rare dals to bold spices, everything carries a story shaped by altitude, climate, and culture. It is not just about organic food, but about preserving a way of life that respects both land and people. In every pack, Vartoh offers more than nourishment it brings a piece of the mountains closer to home.
