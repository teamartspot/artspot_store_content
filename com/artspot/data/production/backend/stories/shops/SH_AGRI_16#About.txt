Founded in 1985 in the heart of Srinagar, Retaj Agro Farms was built on a simple devotion to honor the purity of Kashmir’s land and share its finest treasures with the world. From saffron fields bathed in autumn sunlight to orchards heavy with almonds and walnuts, much of what we offer is grown within our own farms, nurtured without chemicals, and guided by traditional wisdom.
Each strand of Retaj Saffron and every Snow Tree walnut or almond reflects careful cultivation, natural richness, and uncompromising quality. Celebrated for both flavor and wellness, our produce carries the depth, aroma, and vitality that only Kashmir’s soil can bestow.
Led by the vision of Mr. Ferooz Ahmad, Retaj Agro Farms continues to blend heritage with integrity offering nature’s finest harvests with quiet confidence and global reach.
