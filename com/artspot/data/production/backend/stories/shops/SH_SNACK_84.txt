Sapre Foods was founded in 1984 by Late Mrs. Shashikala Sapre with a vision of preserving and sharing the authentic flavors of traditional Maharashtrian cuisine. What began as a small family initiative has grown into a trusted brand known for its homemade sweets, snacks, festive delicacies, and ready-to-eat specialties. The company is committed to using carefully selected ingredients and time-honored recipes to deliver the same taste, freshness, and quality across every product. Blending tradition with modern food processing and nationwide delivery, Sapre Foods has made authentic Maharashtrian cuisine accessible to customers across India. Today, the brand continues to carry forward its founder's legacy of quality, trust, and the belief that there is "delight in every bite."
