Founded in 1956 under the Odisha Cooperative Societies Act, Boyanika Collective began with a modest turnover of ₹65,000 and a team of five. Over the decades, it has transformed into a powerful force in the handloom sector, recording a turnover of ₹128 crore by 2021–22.

Originally established to streamline the marketing of handloom products and supply quality raw materials to Odisha’s weavers, the organization evolved with time—embracing design development, technology upgrades, and export facilitation. It played a critical role in uniting weaver communities across districts, fostering entrepreneurship, and elevating regional weaves such as Bomkai, Kotpad, Sambalpuri, and Berhampuri to national prominence.

As it entered its 68th year in 2024, Boyanika Collective stands as a testament to cooperative strength, cultural pride, and sustainable livelihood generation in India’s handloom industry.
