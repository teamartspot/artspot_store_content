Aapla Jalgaonwala was founded with the vision of bringing the authentic taste of Jalgaon's famous banana chips to customers across India. What began as a local initiative has grown into a proudly Marathi brand, offering a wide variety of flavored banana chips and Khandeshi specialty products while celebrating the region's agricultural heritage. By sourcing quality bananas from Jalgaon and combining traditional recipes with modern processing and packaging, the company ensures freshness, consistency, and premium quality. Their journey reflects a commitment to promoting local flavors, supporting regional produce, and transforming a traditional snack into a recognized brand with a growing presence across India.
