Jayshankar Lamboti's journey began in the 1970s when severe drought forced farmer Shankar Khatal and his family to leave their village of Lamboti in search of a better livelihood. Refusing to give up, Mrs. Rukmini Khatal started a small roadside tea stall in 1975, despite facing social barriers as a woman entrepreneur. Her dedication and homemade snacks, including the famous chivda and chutneys, soon transformed the tea stall into Hotel Jayshankar, earning the trust of travelers and locals alike. As the business expanded across Maharashtra and beyond, the family named the brand Lamboti after their native village to honor its roots. Today, Jayshankar Lamboti continues to preserve authentic Maharashtrian flavors, carrying forward a legacy of tradition, perseverance, and family values.
