Everest Spices was founded by Shri Vadilal Bhai Shah, who registered the Everest brand in 1967 after starting with a small 200 sq. ft. spice shop in Mumbai. Driven by a passion for sourcing the finest spices, he built the company on the principles of purity, quality, and authentic taste. The brand began with just three products—Milk Masala, Garam Masala, and Tea Masala—and steadily expanded through innovation, research, and modern manufacturing. Over the decades, Everest has grown into India's largest manufacturer of pure and blended spices, earning the trust of millions of households. Today, Everest continues to preserve the rich flavors of Indian cuisine while delivering consistent quality to consumers in India and across the world.
