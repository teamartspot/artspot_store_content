Ghodawat Consumer Limited (GCL), the FMCG arm of the Sanjay Ghodawat Group, was established in 2003 with a vision of providing high-quality daily essentials at affordable prices. The company began its journey with edible oil and gradually expanded into rice, salt, atta, snacks, beverages, and other household staples under its Star brand. Driven by a legacy of trust and innovation, GCL has continuously broadened its portfolio through brands like TBH (To Be Honest) and Coolberg, catering to changing consumer preferences. Today, the company has become one of India's fastest-growing FMCG businesses, serving customers across India and exporting to multiple countries. Ghodawat Consumer continues to focus on quality, affordability, and sustainable growth while enriching everyday lives through its diverse range of products.
