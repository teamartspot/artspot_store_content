Sujata Mastani began its journey in 1967 as a small ice cream shop in Sadashiv Peth, Pune, founded by the Kondhalkar family with a vision of serving delicious, natural, and innovative desserts. Inspired by the traditional French Pot method of making ice cream without eggs, the brand introduced a unique version of the iconic Mastani, which soon became synonymous with Pune's dessert culture. Starting with just five flavors, Sujata Mastani has grown into a celebrated brand offering over 33 flavors across multiple outlets while maintaining its commitment to natural ingredients, taste, and quality. Built on decades of customer trust, innovation, and authentic recipes, the brand has earned recognition at both national and international levels. Today, Sujata Mastani continues to delight generations of customers by serving the iconic taste of Pune with every glass.
