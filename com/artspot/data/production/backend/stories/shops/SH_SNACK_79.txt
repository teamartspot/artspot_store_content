Orange N Orange was founded on 29 July 2015 in Nagpur, Maharashtra, by Mr. Dipak Singh with a vision of creating innovative fruit-based sweets and desserts. The brand introduced the unique concept of "Eating a Fruit, without actually Eating a Fruit," transforming fresh fruits into delicious products like Fruit Katli and ice creams served inside real fruit peels. Using fresh fruit juice and pulp without gelatin or agar-agar, the company focuses on natural, vegetarian, and authentic fruit-based treats. Driven by customer satisfaction and continuous innovation, Orange N Orange has also developed Ayurvedic herb-infused fruit sweets that combine taste with wellness. Today, the brand continues to redefine fruit confectionery by blending creativity, quality, and the natural goodness of fruits.
