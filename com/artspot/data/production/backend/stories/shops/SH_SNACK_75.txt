Suruchi Group was established in 1979 in Nagpur, Maharashtra, with a vision of providing consumers with pure, high-quality spices at the best value. Starting as a spice manufacturing company, it has grown into one of India's leading manufacturers and exporters of spices, chilli products, spice blends, and processed foods. Built on the philosophy of reliability and quality, the brand has expanded from selling a few kilograms of spices to processing hundreds of tonnes every month. With modern manufacturing facilities, direct sourcing from farmers, and stringent quality standards, Suruchi has earned the trust of customers in India and global markets. Today, Suruchi continues its mission of delivering authentic taste and premium-quality products while strengthening its presence worldwide.
