Bastar Art Gallery (Jagdalpur) is an exhibition space and retail gallery showcasing tribal art, contemporary interpretations of Bastar craft and works by regional artists. The gallery presents Dhokra metal sculptures, Dokra figurines, tribal paintings and mixed‑media works reflecting Bastar’s cultural narratives. It hosts rotating exhibitions, collaborates with local artists for artist‑in‑residence programmes, and provides a platform for collectors to engage with tribal art. The gallery also organises workshops and educational events to expose visitors to traditional making processes.
