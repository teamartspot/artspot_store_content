Murali Saree Emporium is a Hyderabad-based saree and handloom retailer (Dilsukh Nagar area) offering a wide range of sarees, dress materials and home textiles. The emporium has been serving local customers since at least the 1990s and maintains a storefront presence along with trade listings and supplier registrations. It caters to retail shoppers and small bulk orders for events and boutiques.
