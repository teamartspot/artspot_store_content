Heera Sweets has been delighting customers for over three decades, building a reputation for purity, authentic taste, and uncompromising quality. Founded in Nagpur, the brand blends traditional Indian sweet-making techniques with carefully selected ingredients to create timeless delicacies. Renowned for its signature Orange Burfi, Soan Rolls, Kaju Katli, and premium sweets, Heera Sweets has become a trusted name for festive celebrations and everyday indulgence. By combining traditional recipes with modern hygiene and quality standards, the company continues to preserve the rich heritage of Indian sweets. Today, Heera Sweets serves customers across India, carrying forward its legacy of craftsmanship, freshness, and authentic flavors.
