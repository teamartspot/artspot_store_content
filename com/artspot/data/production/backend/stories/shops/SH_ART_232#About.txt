Pembarthi Vishwakarma Handicrafts Pembarthi Vishwakarma Handicrafts represents local artisan workshops and families practising Pembarthi metalcraft—producing brass idols, utensils and decorative artefacts. The workshops combine lost-wax casting, sheet metal work and hand‑finishing to produce ritual objects and ornamental pieces sold locally and through trade networks. Products are often commissioned by temples, collectors and cultural buyers seeking authentic Pembarthi metalwork.
