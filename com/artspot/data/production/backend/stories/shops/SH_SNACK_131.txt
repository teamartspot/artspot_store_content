Govind Milk & Milk Products was established in 1995 in Phaltan, Maharashtra, with a vision to empower dairy farmers while delivering high-quality dairy products to consumers. Under the leadership of Mr. Sanjeev Naik Nimbalkar, the company grew from a regional dairy into a trusted brand with a pan-India presence. Its journey has been driven by modern processing technology, strict quality standards, and a commitment to providing fair value to farmers. Govind works closely with thousands of farmers, ensuring fresh, farm-sourced milk reaches consumers every day. Today, the brand is known for combining innovation, sustainability, and farmer welfare while offering a wide range of premium dairy products.
