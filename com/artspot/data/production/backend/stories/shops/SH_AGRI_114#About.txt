Long before produce reaches a market, it begins its journey in the quiet discipline of the fields. At Meheraj Export, that journey is carefully carried forward without losing what made it valuable in the first place.
Established in 2008 in Malda, a region known for its fertile abundance, the company works closely with growers to bring forward mangoes and farm vegetables that are naturally cultivated, thoughtfully selected, and handled with precision.
Each batch is handpicked, graded, and moved through a well-structured network designed not just for speed, but for consistency, ensuring that freshness and quality remain intact across every delivery.
With a presence across India and a reputation built on transparency and dependable supply, Meheraj Export operates at the intersection of agriculture and trust where every shipment reflects a promise kept.
