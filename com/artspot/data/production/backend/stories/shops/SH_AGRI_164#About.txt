In the misty hills of Mahabaleshwar, where strawberries first found their home in India, a family quietly nurtured a space where nature could be experienced, not just consumed.
Laxmi Strawberry Farm is not merely a farm; it is an invitation to step into the rhythm of the land. Here, visitors don’t just buy fruit; they walk the fields, handpick their own strawberries, and witness freshness at its origin. Every berry grows in the region’s unique climate, shaped by cool air and rich soil that define its sweetness. Beyond strawberries, the farm offers a vibrant spread of seasonal crops, each cultivated with care and natural methods. Perched amidst scenic views, the experience blends farming with quiet luxury where nature, simplicity, and comfort coexist. From field to hand, nothing is altered, nothing is rushed. What you take home is more than fruit; it is a memory of where it came from.
Because true indulgence is not bought; it is picked, felt, and lived.
