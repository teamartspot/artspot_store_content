Ganpati Food Processors was established in 2015 in Bhandara, Maharashtra, with the vision of delivering premium processed fruit and vegetable products backed by quality, freshness, and innovation. Founded by a team of young professionals with expertise in food processing, the company combines modern technology with strict quality control to manufacture frozen fruit pulps, slices, berries, juices, and other value-added products. By working closely with organic farmers and emphasizing natural ingredients over artificial flavors, the business ensures products retain their authentic taste and nutritional value. Over the years, Ganpati Food has expanded its presence across India and international markets, building a reputation for reliable service, customer satisfaction, and sustainable growth in the food processing industry.
