Launched in 2017 under Titan, Taneira was born out of a vision to revive and promote India’s rich textile heritage. With a commitment to sustainability and cultural preservation, Taneira has established “Weavershalas” across the country—dedicated spaces aimed at empowering artisans and ensuring a sustainable livelihood. These efforts reflect the brand’s mission to keep age-old weaving traditions alive while building a deep connection between craftsmanship and conscious fashion.
