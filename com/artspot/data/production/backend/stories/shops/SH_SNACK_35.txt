Mayur Namkeen was established with the vision of bringing the authentic Khandeshi taste to snack lovers through premium-quality namkeen at affordable prices. Inspired by the rich culture and flavors of the Khandesh region, the brand blends traditional recipes with carefully selected ingredients to create snacks that reflect the area's unique spice, sweetness, and tanginess. From the beginning, the company has focused on natural ingredients, hygienic processing, eco-friendly practices, and quality packaging to ensure freshness and great taste. Today, Mayur Namkeen continues its journey by preserving regional culinary heritage while offering a wide range of savory and sweet snacks that celebrate the true flavors of Khandesh.
