The Berkeley Choro Ensemble, which possibly inspired the name, debuted in January 2010 in California. Dedicated to the Brazilian choro genre, the ensemble preserves and advances this musical tradition by blending European, African, and Latin influences.

Their performances are known for their intricate melodies, rich harmonies, and emotive storytelling. The group collaborates with international musicians, bringing fresh perspectives to traditional compositions. Educational outreach and workshops are a core part of their mission, inspiring the next generation of choro musicians. 

With each performance, they honor the roots of the genre while introducing it to new global audiences. The ensemble continues to be a vibrant force in keeping this musical legacy alive and evolving.
