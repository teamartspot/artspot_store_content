Kalra’s Cottage Industry kalra's Cottage Industry Founded in the 1980s as a family-run production unit, Kalra's Cottage Industry built its reputation on reliable craftsmanship and export readiness. The firm grew by building supply chains with local weavers and knotters, enhancing dye and finishing processes to meet international expectations. Over time, Kalra's became a well-known destination in Agra for discerning buyers seeking hand-knotted rugs and dhurries.
