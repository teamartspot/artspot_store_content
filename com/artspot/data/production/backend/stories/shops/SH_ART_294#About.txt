Beadwaala Beadwaala is a bead manufacturer and wholesaler based in Varanasi known for glass and gemstone beads, strings and supplies for jewellery makers. The company sells online and through trade portals, offering a wide range of bead types to domestic and international buyers. Beadwaala is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
