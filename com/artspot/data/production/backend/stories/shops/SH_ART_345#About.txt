Mrignayani is the Madhya Pradesh government emporium (M.P. Govt Emporium) with a permanent retail presence in Bhopal.
The emporium stocks traditional Madhya Pradesh handicrafts, textiles, tribal artworks and curated artisan products.
Official contact details and a government/emporium web presence confirm its storefront at New Market, TT Nagar in Bhopal.
Mrignayani serves as a state emporium to help market local crafts and provide a retail outlet for artisans.
Public pages and the official emporium website corroborate the emporium's address and product categories.
