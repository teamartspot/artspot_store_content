Kelve Masale was founded by Chef Kuldeep Vartak in Palghar, Maharashtra, with a vision of bringing authentic Maharashtrian flavors to every kitchen. Inspired by his journey from chef to entrepreneur, he created the brand to solve the everyday challenge of preparing delicious, traditional meals with ease. Kelve Masale is committed to producing 100% natural spices, free from preservatives and additives, using only premium-quality ingredients. By blending traditional recipes with modern manufacturing and strict quality standards, the brand serves both home cooks and professional chefs. Today, Kelve Masale continues to preserve Maharashtra's rich culinary heritage while delivering pure, flavorful spice blends trusted by customers across India.
