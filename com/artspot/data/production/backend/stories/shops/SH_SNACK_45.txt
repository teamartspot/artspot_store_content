Kusakka Foods was created to preserve the authentic flavors of Maharashtra through traditional recipes, premium ingredients, and handcrafted spice blends, flours, and chutneys. The brand's journey began with Shobhatai Doijad, whose passion for home-style cooking won the hearts of family and friends for years. At the age of 65, she transformed her lifelong dream into reality by launching Bhakar Restaurant & Spices in 2022, laying the foundation for Kusakka Foods. Her dedication to preserving traditional Maharashtrian cuisine continues through every product made with purity and without artificial additives. Supported by food creator Rohit Khot, Kusakka Foods combines culinary heritage with modern reach, bringing the rich taste of Maharashtra to homes across the country.
