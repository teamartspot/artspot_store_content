Sandur Kushala Kala Kendra (SKKK) is a long‑standing NGO and craft centre in Sandur, Karnataka, specialising in Lambani embroidery, cane work, stone carving and local weaving traditions. The centre supports more than a hundred artisans—primarily women—and provides training, design inputs and market linkages for rural craft producers. SKKK sells products through its outlets and participates in exhibitions to promote Lambani and regional craft forms. The organisation focuses on livelihoods, skill training and sustaining Lambani cultural heritage through collaborative projects.
