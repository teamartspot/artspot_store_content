Husseny Bakery was founded in 1932 by Late Mr. Mirdehghan S. Murteza in Bhawani Peth, Pune, with a vision of bringing freshly baked breads, biscuits, cakes, and pastries to the community. Built on traditional Irani baking techniques, the bakery quickly earned a reputation for its quality, authenticity, and warm customer service. For over 90 years, four generations of the family have preserved its heritage while continuing to use the iconic wood-fired oven that gives its products their distinctive taste. Renowned for its Brun bread, Mawa Cakes, Shrewsbury biscuits, khari, and Christmas cakes, Husseny Bakery has become a beloved landmark in Pune. Today, the brand continues to uphold its legacy of craftsmanship, tradition, and uncompromising quality, delighting generations of customers.
