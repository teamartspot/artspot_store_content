Babu's Laxminarayan Chiwda traces its origins to 1985, when Mr. Suresh Choudhari started a small business in Pune, Maharashtra, with a passion for preparing authentic Maharashtrian chiwda and snacks. Built on traditional family recipes, the brand quickly earned a loyal customer base through its consistent quality, freshness, and distinctive taste. Over the years, the company has expanded its range to include a variety of chiwda, namkeen, sweets, and festive snacks while preserving the original homemade flavors. Combining premium ingredients with hygienic manufacturing and modern packaging, Babu's continues to deliver products that reflect Maharashtra's rich culinary heritage. Today, the brand is trusted by customers across India for its authentic taste, quality, and decades-long tradition.
