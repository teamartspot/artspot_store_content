In the misty hills of Madikeri, Coorg, where the air is rich with the aroma of spices, coffee, and cocoa, Choco Coorg Spice brings together tradition and modern indulgence. Rooted in the heritage of Kodagu, the brand celebrates the region’s timeless craft, transforming its finest ingredients into products that are authentic, fresh, and thoughtfully curated.
From handpicked spices and aromatic filter coffee to homemade chocolates and natural wellness products, every creation reflects purity, care, and the spirit of Coorg’s land. Crafted in-house and guided by traditional values, each offering preserves the richness of flavor while embracing contemporary tastes.
More than a store, Choco Coorg Spice is an experience where heritage meets innovation, and the essence of Coorg is delivered with elegance to homes across India and the world.
