Saeem Sitarmaker is a Miraj-based workshop crafting sitars and related string instruments. 
The studio focuses on handcrafted construction, setup and repair for students and performers. 
Listings and cluster references associate the name with Miraj’s instrument-making community. 
The shop serves classical musicians as well as music schools in the region. 
Its work reflects Miraj’s reputation for dependable luthiery and tonal quality.
