Eastern India Rice traces its roots to a family rice business that began in the early 1960s, when the founders entered the wholesale rice trade in North Bengal, gradually building deep expertise across regional markets. Recognizing the fragmented premium Gobindo Bhog rice market, the family established Eastern India Rice Mill in Purba Bardhaman with modern milling technology to deliver consistent, high-quality rice. Built on generations of rice-trading experience, the company developed premium brands like Malik Deenar and Double D.P., focusing on authentic aroma, quality, and reliability. Their journey reflects a blend of traditional family legacy and modern processing, bringing Bengal’s renowned Gobindo Bhog rice to customers across India and beyond.
