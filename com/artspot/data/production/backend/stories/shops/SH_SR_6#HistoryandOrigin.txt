Rooted in the historic weaving traditions of Lucknow, Saman Emporio was born from a desire to modernize Chikankari. Since 1999, the brand has championed inspired embroidery that respects tradition while catering to current fashion sensibilities, earning its place as a leader in luxury Indian wear.

Over the years, Saman Emporio has built a community of skilled artisans, preserving age-old techniques while encouraging creative expression. Each piece reflects a commitment to quality, craftsmanship, and design innovation. With a focus on ethical production, the brand supports sustainable livelihoods and artisan empowerment. 

Saman Emporio continues to push the boundaries of heritage fashion, creating styles that resonate with the global, modern woman. As it moves forward, the brand remains deeply anchored in its roots—honoring a craft that has stood the test of time.
