Kanpur Saddle House Kanpur Saddle House developed within Kanpur's historic leather manufacturing ecosystem, where tanneries and saddlery workshops evolved into export-oriented manufacturers. The business professionalised production and quality control to meet international standards while preserving skilled leathercraft techniques through apprenticeship and workshop training. Kanpur Saddle House is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
