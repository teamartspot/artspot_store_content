In the mist-covered hills of Wayanad grows a rice cherished for generations, Jeerakasala, the fragrant soul of Kerala’s most treasured biryanis. Under the trusted legacy of Double Horse, Kerala’s heritage food brand since 1959, this delicate grain is brought from field to kitchen with uncompromising care.
Known for its subtle aroma, slender texture, and ability to absorb spices with grace, Wayanad Jeerakasala transforms every meal into an occasion. Carefully sourced and precision-processed using world-class technology, it preserves both the authenticity of tradition and the assurance of modern quality.
With every grain, Double Horse continues its promise of honest food, timeless flavour, and the quiet luxury of Kerala’s culinary heritage, served to tables across the world.
