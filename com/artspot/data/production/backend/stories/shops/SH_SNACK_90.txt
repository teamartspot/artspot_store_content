Shivam Foods and Spices was founded in 2003 by Mr. Balaji Baliram Mundhe in Parbhani, Maharashtra, with a vision of bringing authentic Indian spices to every kitchen. Starting as a regional spice manufacturer, the company steadily expanded across Maharashtra and neighboring states through its commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. Backed by modern manufacturing facilities and a skilled team, Shivam Masale produces hygienic, flavorful, and premium-quality spice blends using strict quality control standards. Rooted in trust, tradition, and continuous innovation, the brand strives to preserve the rich taste of Indian cuisine while meeting evolving consumer needs. Today, Shivam Masale continues its mission of becoming a globally trusted name for pure, aromatic, and authentic Indian spices
