Miraj, Maharashtra, is a historic center for Hindustani instrument making. 
Local luthiers established a reputation for sitar and tanpura craftsmanship across generations. 
Workshops like Saeem Sitarmaker keep traditional hand tools and wood selection practices alive. 
Artisans source seasoned gourds and timbers to achieve stable resonance and projection. 
The craft lineage connects regional maestros, instrument makers and performance traditions.
