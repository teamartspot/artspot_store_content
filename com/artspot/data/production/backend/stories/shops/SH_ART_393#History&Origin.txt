The brand name means ‘Pot Paper’ in Assamese, symbolizing handmade creativity. 
It arose from efforts to revive handmade paper crafts in Assam. 
Its founders partnered with artisans familiar with indigenous papermaking traditions. 
By combining design innovation with heritage techniques, Kumbhikagaz connects tradition to modern eco-lifestyles. 
It has grown as part of Assam’s eco-entrepreneurship movement in recent years.
