Along the tidal backwaters of Kerala, where earth meets the rhythm of the sea, Pokkali is not just cultivated it is lived.
Rooted in a centuries-old tradition, this rare grain grows in harmony with nature, nourished by saline waters, monsoon tides, and a unique cycle where rice and prawns sustain each other in perfect balance.
Untouched by chemicals and shaped entirely by its ecosystem, Pokkali carries a character that cannot be replicated, rich in nutrition, resilient by nature, and deeply connected to its origin.
Recognised with a GI tag and celebrated as a climate-resilient heritage crop, it stands as a quiet symbol of sustainable abundance.
This is not just rice, it is a living legacy of land, water, and wisdom.
