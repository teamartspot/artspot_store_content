Talwar Leather Studio Talwar Leather Studio grew from small artisan beginnings into a recognised leather workshop in Indore. Over the years the studio formalised production while keeping hand-finishing at its core — a balance that helped it serve retail walk-ins and larger trade enquiries. Local directories and social pages record the business as a neighbourhood leather specialist that extended services to custom manufacturing and exports. Talwar Leather Studio is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
