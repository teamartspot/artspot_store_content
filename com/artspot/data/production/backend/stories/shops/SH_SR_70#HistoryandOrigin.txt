Founded in 2020, Vasumatis was born out of a desire to bridge the gap between India’s skilled handloom weavers and saree connoisseurs worldwide. Our story began with a simple yet powerful idea: eliminate middlemen, reach the artisans directly, and create a platform that values both quality and integrity.

Starting with direct partnerships in the weaving villages of Tamil Nadu, particularly the Kanchipuram region, Vasumatis has expanded to support weaving clusters across Banaras, Dharmavaram, Narayanpet, and beyond. By working closely with the artisans and placing orders directly at the source, we not only ensure the authenticity of every weave but also create meaningful, lasting relationships rooted in mutual respect and shared vision.

Today, Vasumatis stands as a proud advocate for traditional Indian handloom, where every thread speaks of legacy, and every saree empowers its creator.
