The roots of Deepam Silks trace back three generations of master weavers, originally serving royal households in Mysore. In 1972, under the leadership of M. Chandrashekar, the iconic brand opened its first retail store on Commercial Street, Bengaluru, marking its official entry into silk retail.

Over the subsequent decades, Deepam has built a nationwide reputation, growing into one of Bengaluru’s most cherished silk destinations. Today, it empowers over 2,000 skilled weavers across weaving clusters—from Kanchipuram and Varanasi to Uppada and Paithani—promoting authentic craftsmanship and weaving artistry.
