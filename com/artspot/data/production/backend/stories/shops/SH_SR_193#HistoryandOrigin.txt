Islie by Priya Jain began with a vision to create couture that reflects India’s artistic heritage while embracing contemporary sensibilities. Known for intricate designs, superior craftsmanship, and a bespoke shopping experience, our couture collections have adorned countless celebrations and special moments.

Encouraged by the heartfelt response from our loyal clientele, we expanded into Pret fashion with the launch of Meher by Islie. This new chapter celebrates effortless grace and modern silhouettes without compromising on our commitment to quality and detail.

Together, Islie and Meher are not just clothing brands – they are a celebration of stories, traditions, and the evolving beauty of Indian fashion.
