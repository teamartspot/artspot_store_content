Founded in 1987 in the heart of Jaipur, Pratibha Sarees began as a small store opened by an 18-year-old entrepreneur with a vision. What started as a humble setup has grown into a beloved brand known for cultural richness and craftsmanship. Built on decades of customer trust and the charm of Jaipur’s heritage, we continue to evolve—merging tradition with trends while staying true to our roots in hand-worked ethnic wear.
