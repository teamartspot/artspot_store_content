In the historic city of Bijapur, where centuries-old spice traditions fill the air with aroma and character, Umadi Foods began its journey in 1969. Born from the rich culinary heritage of Karnataka, the brand carries forward recipes shaped over generations and inspired by the region's vibrant spice culture.
At Umadi Foods, tradition is not merely remembered it is practiced. Using time-honoured methods of blending and preparation, every chutney powder, masala, and spice retains its natural fragrance, depth, and authenticity. The result is a taste that feels both timeless and unmistakably genuine.
For over five decades, Umadi Foods has remained devoted to purity, craftsmanship, and the soulful flavors of home cooking bringing the heritage of Bijapur to kitchens with care, quality, and quiet pride.
