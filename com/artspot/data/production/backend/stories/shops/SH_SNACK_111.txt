Nilon's was founded in 1962 by Shri Suresh B. Sanghavi in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, beginning as a humble family venture with a vision of bringing authentic, high-quality food products to Indian households. What started with pickles has grown into one of India's fastest-growing processed food companies, expanding into spices, instant mixes, cooking pastes, sauces, and ready-to-cook foods. Built on the values of quality, innovation, and trust, Nilon's combines traditional Indian flavors with modern manufacturing and stringent quality standards. Over the decades, the brand has earned the confidence of millions of consumers and established a strong presence in both domestic and international markets. Today, Nilon's continues its journey of delivering flavorful, convenient, and high-quality food products under its promise of "Nilon's Hai Toh Achcha Hai."
