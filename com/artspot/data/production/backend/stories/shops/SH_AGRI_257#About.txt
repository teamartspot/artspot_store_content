In the coastal embrace of the Konkan, where kokum has long been a cherished part of everyday life, Kokum Shokum began as a heartfelt tribute to a fruit deeply rooted in tradition. What makes its story unique is its singular devotion transforming this humble, tangy fruit into refreshing experiences that carry both nostalgia and wellness. Inspired by age-old recipes and the natural cooling essence of kokum, each creation reflects authenticity without losing its modern charm. From sun-soaked orchards to carefully crafted products, the journey is one of preserving regional heritage in a form today’s generation can enjoy. It is not just about a beverage or a product, but about bottling a slice of coastal warmth, tradition, and refreshing simplicity in every sip.
