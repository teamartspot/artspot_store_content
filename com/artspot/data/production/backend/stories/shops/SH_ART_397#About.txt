Shrujan Creations is based in Kutch, Gujarat, and is known for promoting traditional embroidery. 
It is linked to the NGO Shrujan, which supports women artisans in Kutch villages. 
Products include hand-embroidered garments, home décor and accessories. 
Shrujan Creations maintains outlets and exhibitions showcasing authentic Kutchi craft. 
It has been featured in heritage and artisan development reports nationally.
