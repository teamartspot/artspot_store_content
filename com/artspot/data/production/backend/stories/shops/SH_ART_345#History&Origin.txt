Mrignayani is a state-run emporium created to showcase and sell Madhya Pradesh crafts and to support artisan livelihoods.
Government listings and the emporium website provide operational details and the emporium's retail focus.
The emporium has been an institutional retail outlet for regional crafts for multiple decades (official records and directories).
Public resources show event participation, contact phones and its New Market/TT Nagar retail location.
