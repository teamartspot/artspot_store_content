The Kunbi sari traces its roots to the Kunbi community of Goa, one of the region’s oldest indigenous groups. Traditionally worn by Kunbi women, this sari is simple yet dignified—featuring a plain, handwoven body in earthy reds and browns with contrasting pallus. It was historically crafted using local materials and dyed naturally, making it both eco-conscious and culturally symbolic.

Over generations, this humble weave has become a treasured part of Goan identity. At Parampara, we honor that legacy by reviving the traditional techniques used to create these saris—handwoven on eco-friendly looms, dyed with plant-based ingredients, and handled with care by artisans, textile experts, and weavers. Each Kunbi sari is not just a garment, but a continuation of a timeless cultural narrative.
