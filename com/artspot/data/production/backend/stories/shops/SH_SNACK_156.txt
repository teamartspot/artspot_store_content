Induben Khakhrawala's journey began in 1965, when Induben Jhaveri, a homemaker from Ahmedabad, started making and selling homemade khakhras to support her family. Guided by her simple philosophy of "selling only what she would gladly feed her own children," she earned the trust of customers through uncompromising quality and authentic taste. What started as a small home-based venture gradually grew into one of Gujarat's most iconic snack brands, expanding its range from traditional khakhras to namkeens, sweets, bakery products, and more. The brand continues to blend traditional recipes with modern production while maintaining its commitment to freshness, quality, and customer satisfaction. Today, Induben Khakhrawala is a globally recognized name, carrying forward the founder's legacy of trust, innovation, and authentic Gujarati flavors.
