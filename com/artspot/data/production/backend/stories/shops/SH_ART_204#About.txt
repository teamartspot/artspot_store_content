New Kashmir Zari is a Maharajganj-based embroidery and textile outlet in Srinagar known for zari, tilla and traditional Kashmiri embellishments used on pherans, shawls and ceremonial garments. The shop offers a mix of ready-made pieces and bespoke orders and serves customers across Srinagar through in-person sales and directory listings. New Kashmir Zari maintains ties with local embroiderers and often sources fabrics and decorative trims from regional suppliers. The shop emphasizes handwork and traditional finishing, keeping Kashmir’s rich embroidery vocabulary visible to local buyers and visitors.
