Zari Poshak (operated by Aiman Jehan) is a specialty boutique in the Safakadal area of Srinagar focusing on traditional Kashmiri bridal and ceremonial wear featuring tilla, zari and aari embroidery. The boutique showcases lehengas, pherans and ornate garments made with local metallic-thread techniques, often custom‑stitched to client specifications. Zari Poshak combines contemporary silhouettes with heritage Kashmiri embellishment, catering to both local ceremonies and tourists seeking authentic Kashmir garments. The store maintains active engagement via social platforms and WhatsApp for inquiries, highlighting ready pieces as well as bespoke creations. Their work supports local embroiderers and keeps traditional tilla and zari methods in practice.
