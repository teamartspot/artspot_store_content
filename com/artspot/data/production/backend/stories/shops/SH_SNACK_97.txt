Goodluck Cafe was established in 1935 by Hussain Ali Yakhshi on Fergusson College Road, Pune, and is one of the city's oldest and most iconic Irani cafés. Founded with a vision of bringing people together through delicious food and warm hospitality, it quickly became a favorite gathering place for students, families, artists, and travelers. Over the decades, the café has earned legendary status for its Irani chai, Bun Maska, Bun Omelette, and signature non-vegetarian dishes, while preserving its authentic heritage and timeless charm. Today, under the leadership of Mr. Ghasem Yakhshi, Goodluck Cafe has expanded through a franchise model while remaining committed to the quality, tradition, and hospitality that have defined it for over 90 years. It continues to stand as a cultural landmark where generations of customers come together to enjoy its enduring legacy.
