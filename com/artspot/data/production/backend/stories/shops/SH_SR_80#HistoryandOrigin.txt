The journey of Vayan began in 2016 when Mrs. Harshita Jindal, newly married and settled in Chhattisgarh, found herself captivated by the region’s raw artistic energy — especially the stunning handwoven Tussar Silk of Champa. Moved by the talent of local artisans and the untapped beauty of their work, she envisioned a brand that would give this textile the recognition it deserved.

Harshita, with her academic background in International Business, became deeply involved in the design and production process, collaborating directly with weavers to ensure each creation reflected the authenticity of its roots.

Her sister and co-founder, Mrs. Vartika Baranwal Rajgaria, joined her soon after. A postgraduate in Marketing and Sales, and a creative soul by nature, Vartika shaped Vayan’s digital and visual identity. Together, the sisters merged tradition with innovation — Harshita focusing on production and artisan collaboration, while Vartika brought the brand’s story to life for a global audience.

As the love for Vayan grew, so did its offerings. Drawing inspiration from their roots in Bhadohi, near the famed Banarasi weaving hub of Varanasi, the duo expanded the collection to include Banarasi Silk. Each Banarasi piece at Vayan continues the brand’s mission — to celebrate craft, empower artisans, and foster a deeper connection between tradition and today’s world.

From humble beginnings to a growing community of heritage textile lovers, Vayan is a living legacy woven with care, passion, and purpose.
