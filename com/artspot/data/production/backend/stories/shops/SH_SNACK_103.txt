Ramprasad Best Chiwda was founded in 1913 in Pune, Maharashtra, making it one of the city's oldest and most iconic chiwda brands. Beginning as a small family-run business, it earned a loyal following through its signature Cornflakes Chiwda and Poha Chiwda, prepared using traditional recipes and premium-quality ingredients. For more than a century, the brand has remained committed to preserving the authentic taste and freshness that made it famous across generations. While embracing modern packaging and wider distribution, Ramprasad has stayed true to its original recipes and uncompromising quality. Today, it continues to be a trusted name in Maharashtrian snacks, carrying forward a legacy of over 110 years of taste and tradition.
