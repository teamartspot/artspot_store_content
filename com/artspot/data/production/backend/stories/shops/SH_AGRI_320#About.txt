Basumoti Rice traces its roots to 1997, when the business began as Bardhaman Agro Products, building strong relationships with paddy farmers in West Bengal. In 2009, the partnership evolved into Bardhaman Agro Products India Pvt. Ltd., expanding with modern rice processing and a stronger market presence. Later, the Basumoti brand was launched in 2018 to deliver premium rice varieties with a focus on quality, purity, and consistency. Built on decades of rice trade experience, the company has grown into a recognized supplier of Gobindobhog, Tulaipanji, Basmati, and other specialty rice varieties for domestic and international markets. Their journey reflects a blend of agricultural heritage, farmer partnerships, and modern food manufacturing excellence.
