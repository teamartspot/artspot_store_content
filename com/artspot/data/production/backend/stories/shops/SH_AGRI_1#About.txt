Founded in 1931, Nathmull’s Tea carries a legacy that began with a journey—from Rajasthan to the mist-covered hills of Darjeeling. What started as a humble tea trade grew through generations, guided by patience, craftsmanship, and enduring relationships with celebrated tea gardens.


At a time when tea was traded by trust rather than auctions, the Nathmull's built a reputation for authenticity and excellence. Today, that legacy lives on at Nathmull’s Tea & Sunset Lounge—a space where time slows, sunsets glow, and every cup tells a story of heritage, refinement, and Darjeeling’s finest teas.
