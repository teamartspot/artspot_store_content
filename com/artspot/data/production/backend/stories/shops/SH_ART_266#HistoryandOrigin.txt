Batua City Fashion Originating as a small family store in Lucknow's Janpath market, Batua City Fashion grew by catering to the city's bridal and ceremonial clientele. Its name became synonymous with quality potli bags and accessory craftsmanship, blending Lucknowi embroidery and embellishment techniques for festive markets. The store's longevity reflects Lucknow's deep association with textile crafts and wedding goods.
