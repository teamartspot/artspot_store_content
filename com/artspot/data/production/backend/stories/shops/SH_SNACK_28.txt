ChhayaKart was founded as a social enterprise with the vision of empowering Self-Help Groups (SHGs), women entrepreneurs, Gruh Udyogs, and rural businesses by providing them with an online marketplace for their locally made products. The platform connects rural producers with customers across India, helping them expand their businesses while preserving traditional homemade and handmade products. From papads, pickles, and millets to authentic snacks and regional specialties, ChhayaKart promotes India's rich local food heritage through a farm-and-home-to-consumer model. Their journey reflects a strong commitment to rural entrepreneurship, women's empowerment, and sustainable livelihoods by enabling small producers to reach wider markets through digital commerce.
