Pride of Cows was launched by Parag Milk Foods as India's first premium single-origin, farm-to-home milk brand, offering consumers fresh milk directly from its own dairy farm near Manchar, Maharashtra. The brand was created with a vision to provide pure, unadulterated milk with complete traceability from farm to doorstep. Produced at one of India's most advanced dairy farms using international standards of animal care, feeding, and milking, every batch is processed with strict quality control. Pride of Cows follows a subscription-based home delivery model, ensuring milk reaches customers within hours of milking while preserving its natural freshness. Today, the brand is known for its commitment to purity, premium quality, and innovation, setting a benchmark in India's premium dairy segment.
