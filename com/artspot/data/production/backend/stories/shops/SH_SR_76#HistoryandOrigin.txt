Phulkari, derived from the Punjabi words "phul" (flower) and "kari" (work), is a centuries-old embroidery tradition that expresses stories, emotions, and celebrations through bold, geometric floral patterns. Traditionally embroidered using “patt da dhaaga” (silk thread), Phulkari adorned every milestone in a woman’s life — from births to marriages — becoming an heirloom passed through generations.

Phulkari Forever was born from a deep desire to preserve this artistic legacy and share it with the world. With roots in Punjabi customs and craftsmanship, the brand is an effort to give Phulkari a new lease of life by adapting it to contemporary fashion without compromising its essence. We continue this legacy by working closely with rural women artisans, training them, and providing dignified employment to ensure that Phulkari remains not just a craft — but a living tradition.
