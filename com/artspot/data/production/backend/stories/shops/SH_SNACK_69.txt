Theobroma was founded in 2004 by sisters Kainaz Messman Harchandrai and Tina Messman Wykes, who opened their first patisserie at Cusrow Baug, Colaba, Mumbai. The brand, whose name means "Food of the Gods" in Greek, was created with a vision of offering world-class brownies, cakes, pastries, and desserts made with the finest ingredients. What began as a small neighborhood bakery has grown into a pan-India chain with stores across more than 30 cities, while staying true to its commitment to quality and innovation. Inspired by Kainaz's passion for baking and resilience after a career-ending back injury, Theobroma has become one of India's most loved premium bakery brands. Today, it continues to delight millions of customers with handcrafted baked goods that combine international standards with heartfelt craftsmanship.
