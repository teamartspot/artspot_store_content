Aneri Baag is a farm-to-consumer mango brand created to bring premium, naturally grown mangoes from India’s top orchards directly to customers. What began as a first attempt to share “juicy goodness” with loved ones has grown into a curated mango business sourcing from major mango-growing regions like Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Uttar Pradesh. The brand emphasizes handpicked, carbide-free mangoes, strict quality checks, and fresh delivery with export-ready logistics. Their journey reflects a commitment to authentic taste, farmer partnerships, and delivering naturally ripened mangoes with farm-fresh quality to homes and global buyers.
