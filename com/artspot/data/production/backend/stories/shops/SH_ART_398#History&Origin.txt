Bhuj is a hub of Kutchi handicrafts, especially embroidery, block printing and leatherwork. 
Emporiums like Kutchh Mohini Art connect local artisans to urban and global markets. 
They preserve generational skills while packaging crafts for tourism. 
The retail outlets reflect the region’s identity as a center of artisan entrepreneurship. 
This ensures continuity of Kutch’s heritage crafts in contemporary commerce.
