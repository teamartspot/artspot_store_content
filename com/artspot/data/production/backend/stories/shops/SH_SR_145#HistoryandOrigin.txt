Founded with a passion to revive and support traditional Indian weaving communities, Online Naksha started its journey with a mission to bring the timeless charm of Bengal handlooms to a wider audience. Rooted in West Bengal, the brand grew out of a desire to bridge the gap between local artisans and contemporary buyers. Over the years, it has evolved into a trusted online destination for authentic handloom sarees, proudly upholding the values of heritage, craftsmanship, and cultural pride.
