GJ Foods (Jinmata Food Processors LLP) was established in 2014 in Dhule, Maharashtra, with the vision of manufacturing premium spray-dried fruit, vegetable, herb, and specialty food ingredients for the food and beverage industry. Starting with a modern processing facility, the company focused on delivering consistent quality through controlled manufacturing, in-house quality systems, and internationally recognized certifications such as ISO 22000, HACCP, and HALAL. Leveraging Dhule's rich agricultural belt, GJ Foods sources fresh raw materials locally to produce reliable, high-performance ingredients for industrial applications. Over the years, the company has built long-term partnerships with food manufacturers by combining innovation, technical expertise, and a commitment to quality, becoming a trusted supplier in both domestic and global markets.
