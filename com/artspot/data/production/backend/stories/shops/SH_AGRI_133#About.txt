Before there were tea rows, there was a forest.
In 1926, a vision took shape in Assam when Jadav Prasad Chaliha cleared dense woodland known as Korangani, transforming it, against all odds, into a thriving tea estate. What began as an act of determination soon became a lineage, carried forward through generations who shaped not just a plantation, but a standard of quality.
Through time, the story evolved. From the pioneering leadership of one of Assam’s earliest women tea planters to becoming a recognised name at the Calcutta Tea Auctions, Korangani grew with both resilience and refinement.
Today, the journey has shifted from estate to experience. With a focus on blending, tasting, and packaging, every tea is carefully curated in-house, ensuring that what reaches you carries the same consistency, freshness, and character throughout the year.
Rooted in heritage, yet shaped for the present, Korangani continues to bring garden-fresh teas to a wider world, balancing tradition with accessibility, without losing the essence of where it all began.
