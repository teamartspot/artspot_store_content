Wagh Bakri Tea's journey began in 1892, when Shri Narandas Desai acquired tea estates in South Africa and built expertise in tea cultivation despite facing racial discrimination. Forced to return to India in 1915, he carried a letter of recommendation from Mahatma Gandhi, which inspired him to establish The Gujarat Tea Depot Co. in Ahmedabad in 1919. In 1934, he introduced the iconic Wagh Bakri brand, symbolized by a tiger and a goat sharing a cup of tea, representing equality, unity, and harmony. Over the decades, the company expanded from local tea depots to packaged teas, international exports, tea lounges, and a nationwide presence while maintaining its commitment to quality. Today, Wagh Bakri Tea remains one of India's most trusted tea brands, carrying forward a 130+ year legacy of bringing people together over a cup of tea.
