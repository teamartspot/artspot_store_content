In the coastal lands of Ganjam, where the air itself carries a natural fragrance, KHR Kewda began as a humble family legacy rooted in tradition. Started by their grandfather, the distillery has spent over three decades transforming delicate kewda flowers into pure, aromatic essence with care and honesty. Each bloom is sourced directly from local farmers, preserving both livelihood and authenticity in every drop produced. The process is slow and thoughtful, allowing the natural scent to unfold without compromise, just as it has been done for generations. Kewda, known for its sweet, rose-like yet uniquely fruity aroma, holds a special place in India’s cultural and aromatic heritage. What makes their story special is not just the product, but the trust they have maintained with nature, with farmers, and with every customer they serve. Every bottle carries more than fragrance; it carries time, tradition, and a quiet promise of purity.
