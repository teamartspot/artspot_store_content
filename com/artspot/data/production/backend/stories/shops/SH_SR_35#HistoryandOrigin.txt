Founded in 1954, Sambalpuri Bastralaya Co-op emerged during the post-independence era with the mission to uplift the handloom weaving community of Western Odisha. Recognizing the rich artistry of Sambalpuri tie-and-dye weaving, the society was established to organize, train, and support rural weavers with steady income and market access.

Over the years, it grew into an apex cooperative—setting up 40 production units, a dyeing center in Bargarh, and 43 dedicated retail outlets. The society ensured fair wages, AZO-free dyeing practices, and continuous product innovation. By bridging artisans with consumers through exhibitions and agents across India, it helped transform local artistry into a nationally recognized craft.

Today, Sambalpuri Bastralaya Co-op proudly stands as a guardian of Sambalpuri handloom heritage—blending centuries-old weaving traditions with modern quality standards and community-first values.
