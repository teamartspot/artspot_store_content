Bastar Handicraft (Jagdalpur) supplies tribal Dhokra metalwork, terracotta, bamboo and bell‑metal products from the Bastar region, working with local craft families to produce traditional ritual objects and decorative pieces. The unit lists its products across regional directories and services both retail customers and institutional buyers. Bastar Handicraft emphasizes lost‑wax metal casting (Dhokra) and tribal motifs, often custom‑making items for export and domestic decor markets. The shop operates from Jagdalpur market complexes and coordinates directly with artisan clusters in surrounding villages to source materials and finished goods.
