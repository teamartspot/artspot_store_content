Katraj Dairy, officially Pune Zilla Sahakari Dudh Utpadak Sangh Ltd., was established in 1960 with the vision of creating an organized milk collection network for dairy farmers across Pune district. Starting with the collection of just 30,000 litres of milk per day, it has grown into one of Maharashtra's leading cooperative dairies, handling over 2 lakh litres daily. The cooperative empowers thousands of rural milk producers by ensuring fair procurement while delivering pure, hygienic dairy products to consumers. Equipped with modern processing facilities and stringent quality standards, Katraj Dairy manufactures a wide range of products including milk, ghee, shrikhand, paneer, butter, lassi, and ice cream. Today, Katraj Dairy remains a trusted household brand, combining cooperative values, quality, and innovation to serve generations of customers across Maharashtra.
