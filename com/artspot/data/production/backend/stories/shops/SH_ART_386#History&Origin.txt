Sawantwadi has a rich history of lacquered wooden toys and playing cards (Ganjifa). 
The Bhonsle royal family historically patronized toy-making workshops, encouraging artisans to settle in the town. 
Shops like Raj Stores & Gift Center continue this legacy by selling toys produced by local craftsmen. 
These crafts are recognized as part of Maharashtra’s cultural heritage. 
The shop’s products represent continuity between historic Sawantwadi traditions and present-day craft retailing.
