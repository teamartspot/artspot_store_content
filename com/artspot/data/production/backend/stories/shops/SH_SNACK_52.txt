Gowardhan was founded in 1992 by Parag Milk Foods in Manchar, Maharashtra, with a mission to support dairy farmers and make better use of surplus milk. The brand emerged after local farmers suffered heavy losses due to frequent "milk holidays," when cooperative dairies stopped collecting milk. Starting with traditional dairy products like ghee, paneer, dahi, and milk, Gowardhan quickly earned a reputation for purity, quality, and authentic taste. With India's largest dairy farm and modern processing facilities, the company combines advanced technology with time-tested recipes to produce high-quality dairy products. Today, Gowardhan is one of India's most trusted dairy brands, continuing its commitment to empowering farmers while delivering nutritious dairy products to millions of consumers.
