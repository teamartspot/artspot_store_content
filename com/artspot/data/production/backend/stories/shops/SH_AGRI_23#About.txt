Rooted in the fertile soils of Malabar, Haritham Foods began its journey over two decades ago with a simple belief: healthy food, healthy life. Founded by Shri M. K. Sethumadhavan and carried forward with vision and precision by Shri K. V. Viswanathan, the brand has grown from humble beginnings into a trusted name grounded in integrity and purpose.
From carefully selected farms to state-of-the-art facilities in Calicut, Haritham blends traditional methods of refinement with modern technology. Every grain, pulse, and spice is thoughtfully sourced, hygienically processed, and meticulously packed  preserving homely taste while meeting today’s kitchen standards.
Guided by respect for the land and a customer-first philosophy, Haritham Foods stands for nourishment that is honest, wholesome, and crafted with care. A legacy born from the soil, refined by innovation, and delivered with trust.
