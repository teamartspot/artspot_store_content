Open Secret was founded in 2019 by Ahana Gautam with a mission to "Unjunk India, One Snack at a Time." Inspired by the care and nutrition that mothers provide at home, the brand set out to create delicious snacks made with clean, wholesome ingredients and complete transparency. Open Secret believes that healthy eating should never compromise on taste, offering products free from unnecessary additives while keeping nutrition at the core. From cookies and nut mixes to protein-rich snacks, the company continues to innovate to solve everyday health challenges for Indian consumers. Today, Open Secret is one of India's fastest-growing healthy snacking brands, combining great taste, honesty, and better nutrition for every family.
