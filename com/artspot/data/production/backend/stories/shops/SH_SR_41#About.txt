Sundarii Handmade is more than just a sustainable fashion brand—it's a conscious movement dedicated to reviving traditional Indian crafts and empowering the artisans who keep them alive. What began as a heartfelt endeavor in December 2017 has grown into a vibrant community advocating for mindful fashion choices rooted in heritage and responsibility.

At Sundarii Handmade, we bring you more than just beautiful apparel. We offer timeless pieces made with purpose—products that reflect not only craftsmanship and elegance, but also a commitment to environmental well-being and social equity. Every purchase contributes directly to the livelihoods of skilled artisans and their families, while helping reduce the carbon footprint.

Our collections spotlight India’s rich handicraft traditions—Ajrakh, Dabu, Ikat, Bagru, Bandhani, and more—curated with an eye for distinctiveness and authenticity. We uphold a strict zero-waste policy by repurposing leftover fabric into wallets, scarves, and patchwork garments—uniting sustainability with creativity.

With patrons across the globe, our journey has been shaped by the continued support of individuals who choose thoughtful consumption. For us, sustainable fashion isn’t a trend—it’s a lifestyle we deeply believe in and strive to share.
