GmShah began as a family-run retail business in Srinagar aiming to preserve and trade Kashmiri textile arts. Over time the shop established relationships with local embroiderers and weavers who continue to produce traditional pieces. The store's history is tied to Srinagar’s long-standing shawl and embroidered textile trade, and it contributes to sustaining local craftsmanship by providing a direct retail channel and bespoke orders.
