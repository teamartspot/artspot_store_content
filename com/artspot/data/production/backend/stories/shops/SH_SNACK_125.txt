R A Food Products, popularly known as R A Masale, was founded in 1972 by Shri Ramchandra Vasant Divate (Bhau) and Smt. Vijaya Ramchandra Divate (Aai) with a vision of delivering authentic Maharashtrian flavors. The journey began with the production of a small batch of Onion Garlic Chutney, driven by an unwavering commitment to quality and traditional recipes. Over the past 50+ years, the family-run business has grown into one of Maharashtra's leading manufacturers of spices, ready-mix masalas, papads, pickles, and other food products. By combining modern manufacturing with time-tested recipes and responsibly sourced ingredients, the company has earned the trust of generations of customers. Today, R A Masale continues to uphold its legacy of quality, authenticity, and customer satisfaction while expanding its reach across India.
