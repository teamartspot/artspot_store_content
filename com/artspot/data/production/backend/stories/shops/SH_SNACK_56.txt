Gokuldas Gathiawala was established in 1924 in Mumbai and has been serving authentic Indian farsan, namkeen, and sweets for over nine decades. What began as a traditional family business has grown into a trusted household name, known for preserving the authentic taste of classic Gujarati snacks. The brand is committed to preparing its products fresh every day using quality ingredients and time-tested recipes, ensuring consistent taste and freshness. While staying rooted in tradition, Gokuldas Gathiawala has embraced modern retail and online delivery, making its products available across India and internationally. Today, the brand continues to delight generations of customers with its rich heritage, uncompromising quality, and authentic flavors.
