Warana Milk traces its roots to the visionary Warana cooperative movement started by Late Shri Tatyasaheb Kore, which transformed the socio-economic condition of rural Maharashtra. In 1968, Warana Dairy was established to provide a sustainable source of income for small and landless farmers through dairy farming. Over the years, it has grown into one of India's leading cooperative dairies, processing up to 10 lakh litres of milk per day while maintaining high quality and food safety standards. The brand is renowned for its wide range of dairy products, especially its record-setting Warana Shrikhand, along with milk, butter, ghee, cheese, paneer, lassi, and milk powder. Today, Warana Milk continues to empower farmers while delivering trusted, high-quality dairy products to consumers in India and international markets.
