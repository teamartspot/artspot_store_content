V.P. Bedekar began as a small grocery shop in Mumbai in 1910, founded with a vision of providing authentic, high-quality spices and pickles to local households. Over the past 100+ years, the family-run business has grown from a neighborhood store into a globally recognized Maharashtrian food brand. Staying true to its founders' values, Bedekar continues to prepare products using premium ingredients, authentic recipes, and time-tested methods. Encouraged by generations of loyal customers, the company has expanded its range from traditional pickles and chutneys to spices, frozen foods, instant mixes, and other comfort foods. Today, V.P. Bedekar carries forward its rich culinary heritage while bringing the taste of home to customers across India and around the world
