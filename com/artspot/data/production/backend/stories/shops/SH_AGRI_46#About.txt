In the heart of Karnataka’s legendary spice belt lies Byadgi, a land celebrated for chillies that glow with deep crimson colour and distinctive character. Namma Byadgi was created to bring this heritage directly from farmer fields to kitchens across India and the world.
Sourced through the iconic Byadgi APMC market, every chilli and spice blend carries the authenticity of traditional farming and the vibrancy of India’s chilli culture. From naturally sun-dried whole pods to carefully crafted chilli powders, each product reflects meticulous selection, purity, and respect for the farmers who nurture these crops.
At Namma Byadgi, tradition meets precision, delivering bold colour, balanced heat, and uncompromising quality in every batch. It is more than a spice; it is the proud story of Byadgi’s red gold, shared with the world.
