Sree Siddarameshwara Agro Industries traces its roots to 1944, when its promoters began operating edible oil mills in pre-independent India, building decades of expertise in the agro-processing industry. In 2004, the company established its first solvent extraction plant in Nanded, Maharashtra, marking the beginning of its modern manufacturing journey. Under the leadership of Mr. K. Srinwas, the company expanded its portfolio to include soya products, groundnut meal, sunflower products, rice bran products, and other value-added agro products. Backed by modern processing facilities and a strong focus on quality, Sree Agro serves both the food and animal feed industries with high-protein, reliable products. Today, the company continues to build on its long-standing legacy by combining experience, innovation, and quality to serve customers across India.
