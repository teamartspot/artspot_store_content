Raghuvir Sweets & Namkeen was founded in 1970 by Late Mangalji Bhai Popat, who began with a humble venture that soon became a beloved part of Amravati's food culture. Over the years, the business expanded from refreshments to sweets, namkeen, bakery products, and complete food outlets, growing alongside the city while preserving its traditional taste and quality. Each milestone—from Raghuvir Refreshments (1993) and Raghuvir Mithaiyan (1995) to Raghuvir Namkeen, Bakery, and Food Zone—strengthened its reputation as a trusted household brand. Today, spanning three generations, Raghuvir continues its journey of blending tradition, innovation, and exceptional quality, living up to its motto of being "Hissa Har Khushiyon Ka" (A Part of Every Happiness).
