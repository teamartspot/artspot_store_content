Mahanand is the flagship brand of the Maharashtra Rajya Sahakari Dudh Mahasangh Maryadit (MRSDMM), established in 1983 as the apex cooperative dairy federation of Maharashtra. The brand was created to unite the state's dairy cooperatives, ensure fair returns for milk producers, and supply consumers with pure, high-quality dairy products. Sourcing milk directly from cooperative societies, Mahanand has built a reputation for freshness, affordability, and strict quality standards. Over the years, it has expanded its portfolio to include milk, ghee, butter, cheese, dahi, lassi, ice cream, and other dairy products while continuing to support thousands of dairy farmers across the state. Today, under the guidance of the National Dairy Development Board (NDDB), Mahanand is focused on modernizing its operations and strengthening Maharashtra's cooperative dairy ecosystem.
