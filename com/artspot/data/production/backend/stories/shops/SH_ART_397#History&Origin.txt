Shrujan was founded in 1969 by Chandaben Shroff to revive Kutch embroidery. 
It documents, trains and supports women artisans across communities. 
Shrujan Creations emerged as the retail and design arm of this initiative. 
Its collections preserve more than 40 embroidery styles from Kutch. 
The project has become a model for craft revival, balancing heritage with sustainable livelihoods.
