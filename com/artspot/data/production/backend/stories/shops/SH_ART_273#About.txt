Shah Rasheed Ahmed Quadri Shah Rasheed Ahmed Quadri is a Padma Shri-awarded Bidriware artisan from Bidar, Karnataka, celebrated for his mastery in bidri metal inlay and revival of sheet-work motifs. His practice includes producing traditional bidriware boxes, trays, and decorative objects that bear Persian-influenced motifs and zafrani inlay. Quadri's workshop trains apprentices and supplies pieces to collectors and cultural institutions across India and abroad. Shah Rasheed Ahmed Quadri is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
