The Pattamadai Fine Mat Wcs Ltd Pattamadai mats have been woven for more than two centuries, with the tradition attributed to the Labbai Muslim community of Pattamadai. The mats are crafted from Korai grass harvested from the Tamirabarani riverbanks, processed into fine strands, dyed, and woven by hand. The craft gained GI status in 2012‑13 to protect its heritage. The cooperative has played a key role in training artisans, organising production, and preserving this unique weaving tradition.
