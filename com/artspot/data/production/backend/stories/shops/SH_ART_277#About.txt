Gagan Leather House Gagan Leather House is an Indore-based leather accessories maker and retailer offering jackets, bags and bespoke leather garments. The business operates from a local storefront and is visible on social and trade listings as a supplier to local markets and small wholesalers. Their product range typically includes biker-style jackets, waistcoats and stitched leather goods that emphasise workmanship and material quality. Gagan Leather House is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
