The Directorate of Handicrafts and Handlooms (D.H.H) is Sikkim’s premier institution dedicated to preserving and promoting the state’s rich cultural and artistic heritage. Headquartered in Gangtok, D.H.H serves as a vibrant hub for artisans, where traditional skills are nurtured, and local craftsmanship is celebrated. From handwoven textiles to intricate woodwork, every creation emerging from D.H.H reflects the cultural pride, spiritual essence, and artistic brilliance of Sikkim.
