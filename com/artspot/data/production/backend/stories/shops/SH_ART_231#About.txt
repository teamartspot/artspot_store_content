Pembarthy Metal Handicrafts Pembarthy Metal Handicrafts is the digital storefront and collective brand for the Pembarthi metalcraft tradition—brass and bell-metal handicrafts made in Pembarthi village (Jangaon district), Telangana. The site and associated makers sell traditional brass figurines, utensils, decorative idols and templeware handcrafted by the Vishwakarma metalworkers of Pembarthi. Pembarthi pieces are known for sheet‑metal repoussé, chasing and stamping techniques adapted to religious icons and household decor. The platform promotes maker stories and links buyers to artisans working within the Pembarthi community.
