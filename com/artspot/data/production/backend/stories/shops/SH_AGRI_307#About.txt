Swanand Homemade Food Products began from a simple passion for creating delicious yet healthy homemade foods, encouraged by appreciation from friends and family. What started over a decade ago as an experiment with natural ingredients like chikoo, amla, lemon, and mango gradually grew into a dedicated food venture in Bordi, Maharashtra. Focused on chemical-free, preservative-free products, the brand offers a range of traditional homemade specialties crafted with health and authenticity in mind. Their journey reflects a commitment to natural living, quality food, and bringing wholesome homemade goodness to customers seeking healthier choices.
