In the basmati heartland of Karnal, where the fragrance of rice has long defined heritage, Dunar began its journey in 1956 as a small dream sown by Lala Duli Chand Gupta. What makes its story unique is its transformation from a humble family-run venture into one of the world’s leading basmati exporters, without losing its core values of trust and quality. Over the years, generations carried this vision forward, turning challenges into milestones and tradition into global presence. Each grain reflects a journey shaped by innovation, modern processing, and deep-rooted experience in rice cultivation. From local fields to kitchens across continents, it carries the aroma of India beyond borders. It is not just a business, but a legacy built over decades with patience and purpose. In every serving, Dunar offers more than rice it offers a story of ambition grounded in heritage.
