Rasna's journey began in 1976, when Areez Khambatta introduced a soft drink concentrate that made refreshing beverages affordable for Indian families. Originally launched as Jaffe, the brand was later renamed Rasna, inspired by the Hindi word "Ras" (juice), making it more relatable to Indian consumers. Its iconic "I Love You Rasna" campaign transformed the brand into a household name and a cherished part of childhood memories across generations. Over the years, Rasna expanded its product portfolio, strengthened its manufacturing and distribution network, and established a presence in 60+ countries, while retaining its leadership in the soft drink concentrate category. Today, Rasna continues its legacy of innovation, affordability, and quality, remaining one of India's most trusted beverage brands.
