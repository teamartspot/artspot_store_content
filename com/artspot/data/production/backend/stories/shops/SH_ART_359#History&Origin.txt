Goodluck Musical Handicraft registered in 2018, the firm operates within Amroha’s long-established percussion cluster, serving local musicians and wider markets. Goodluck Musical Handicraft is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
