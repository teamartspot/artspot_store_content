Since 1988, Bowlekar Cashew has been crafting a story rooted in determination, tradition, and the rich cashew heritage of Sindhudurg. Founded by first-generation entrepreneur Suresh Bowlekar, the brand grew from humble beginnings into a recognized name in the cashew industry, blending deep expertise in sourcing, processing, and quality craftsmanship. With a strong team and modern processing capabilities, Bowlekar carries the essence of Maharashtra’s coastal abundance to customers near and far, turning every cashew into a symbol of dedication, heritage, and authentic taste.
