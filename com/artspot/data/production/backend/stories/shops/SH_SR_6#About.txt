Saman Emporio celebrates the delicate elegance of Lucknawi Chikankari—an embroidery tradition with Persian roots that found its timeless expression in the courts of Awadh, evolving gracefully from the 16th century to the present day. At Saman Emporio, we strive to blend this rich heritage with a contemporary design sensibility, creating a harmony of the classic and the modern.

At the heart of our philosophy is a deep commitment to empowering underprivileged women artisans. Under the guidance and vision of Mrs. Rukhsar, we offer a platform for these skilled women to showcase their talent, sustain their craft, and weave their stories into every thread.

Our creations, adored by women of all ages, embody sophistication and cultural pride. Every garment is a testament to fine craftsmanship—carefully handcrafted to tell a story of tradition, innovation, and empowerment.

Saman Emporio is on a mission to share this centuries-old art form with the world, transforming Chikankari into a global and multicultural experience. Our luxurious pieces seamlessly fuse age-old techniques with modern-day aesthetics, resulting in timeless ensembles that exude grace and elegance.
