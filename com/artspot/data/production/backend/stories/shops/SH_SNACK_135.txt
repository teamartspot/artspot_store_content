Latkar Sweets was established in 1920 in Satara and has been preserving the rich tradition of authentic Maharashtrian sweets for over a century. The brand is best known for its iconic Satari Kandi Pedha, prepared using time-honored recipes and premium ingredients such as pure ghee, jaggery, and high-quality sugar. Through generations, it has remained committed to delivering traditional taste without compromising on quality. What began as a local sweet shop has grown into a trusted name, offering a wide range of classic Indian sweets while celebrating Satara's culinary heritage. Even today, Latkar Sweets continues to uphold its legacy of craftsmanship, authenticity, and customer trust.
