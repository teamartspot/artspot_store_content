What began as a quiet passion among a group of seed savers slowly grew into something far greater: Farmseller, a bridge between forgotten traditions and modern growers. For over three decades, they traveled through villages, gathering rare native seeds that carried stories of resilience, flavor, and heritage. Each seed was not just collected but carefully nurtured on its own land, preserving its authenticity and strength. When the challenge of sustaining this passion arose, they transformed it into a platform that could share these treasures with the world. Today, every packet sent out is a piece of that journey, untouched by hybrids, rooted in tradition, and chosen for its ability to thrive. Their story is not about selling seeds, but about protecting biodiversity and keeping India’s farming legacy alive. With every plant that grows, a forgotten story finds its way back into the soil.
