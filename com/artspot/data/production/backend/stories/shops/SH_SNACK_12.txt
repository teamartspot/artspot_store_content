Krishna Foods was established in 2018 in Amravati, Maharashtra, with the vision of crafting delicious, high-quality ice cream using modern manufacturing practices. From the beginning, the company invested in state-of-the-art infrastructure and adhered to strict food safety, hygiene, and quality standards, earning registrations and certifications including FSSAI and ISO 9001:2015. What started as a regional ice cream manufacturer has grown into a trusted brand offering a wide variety of cones, cups, bars, and family packs. Their journey reflects a commitment to innovation, premium taste, and delivering joyful ice cream experiences while maintaining the highest standards of quality and safety.
