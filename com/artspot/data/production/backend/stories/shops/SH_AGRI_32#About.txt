In the sun-kissed orchards of Nagpur, where the land has nurtured citrus for generations, the story of Vantara Farms began more than 75 years ago. What started with a grandfather planting the first orange saplings on ancestral soil has grown into a living legacy, one rooted in patience, care, and respect for nature.
Guided by traditional farming wisdom and enriched by sustainable practices, each orange is carefully nurtured in Nagpur’s distinctive climate and mineral-rich soil. The result is fruit celebrated for its vibrant sweetness, aroma, and natural character.
At Vantara Farms, every harvest is handpicked at peak ripeness and delivered directly from the orchard to home, pure, fresh, and untouched by artificial processes. It is more than fruit; it is a heritage of the land, preserved and shared with quiet pride.
