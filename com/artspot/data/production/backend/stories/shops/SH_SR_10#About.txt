Dastkari Haat Samiti is a pioneering non-profit organisation dedicated to uplifting India’s traditional crafts and artisans. Founded in 1986, we operate without external funding—relying instead on contributions from artisans and supporters to sustain our work.

With a strong democratic structure and transparent operations, we have empowered over 30,000 artisans through marketing platforms, educational initiatives, and cultural collaborations.

Our flagship initiative, Dilli Haat, has become a national model for craft bazaars, offering artisans direct access to customers in vibrant, accessible marketplaces. We also organise craft workshops, exhibitions, and online showcases, enabling artisans to innovate, share skills, and reach wider audiences.
