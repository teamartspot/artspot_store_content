House of Madhuram began its journey in 1998 as a small home-based initiative with the blessings of Shree Gondavlekar Maharaj. In 2016, the next generation transformed it into a professionally managed business by establishing the Shree Siddhivinayak Industries Group, which later evolved into the House of Madhuram brand. The founders initially built their customer base by personally selling traditional snacks and delicacies in housing societies, government offices, and banks. During the 2020 pandemic, they introduced a pure vegetarian home-delivered tiffin service, which became a successful extension of their business. Today, House of Madhuram serves traditional snacks, meals, and large catering orders for weddings and events, continuing its journey with a strong commitment to purity, authentic taste, and customer trust.
