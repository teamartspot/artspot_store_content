Khandeshi Masala began as a small street shop in Jalgaon, where its ready-made spice blends became popular for their authentic Khandeshi taste, a distinctive regional flavor of Maharashtra. As demand grew, the business established its own manufacturing unit, marking the beginning of the Khandeshi Masala brand. In 2010, Shanti Spices Pvt. Ltd. was formed to expand operations professionally while staying true to its founding values of quality, tradition, nutrition, and safety. Later, in 2015, manufacturing was separated under Ambika Foods, while Shanti Spices managed a growing nationwide distribution network. Today, Khandeshi Masala continues its journey by blending traditional regional recipes with modern automated production, delivering authentic spices through hundreds of dealers across India.
