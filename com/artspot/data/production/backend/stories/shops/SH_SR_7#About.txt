B. Chorosch Ensembles isn’t a textile brand—it’s a musical collective, originally known as the Berkeley Choro Ensemble. Given the context, it appears unrelated to handcrafts or the fashion industry. However, its dedication to preserving and promoting the traditional Brazilian genre of choro reflects the same reverence for heritage and artistry seen in handcrafted cultures.

The ensemble blends classical training with folkloric rhythms, creating a sound both rooted and innovative. Through collaborations and performances, B. Chorosch Ensembles fosters cultural exchange and musical storytelling. 

Much like artisan traditions, their music honors history while embracing modern interpretation.
