Founded with a passion for preserving and promoting India’s rich cultural legacy, iTokri works directly with over 10,000 artisans and craftspeople across the country. What started as a modest initiative has grown into the largest curated online portal for Indian handicrafts, featuring over 100,000 products and adding more than 500 new items daily.

By blending tradition with technology, iTokri empowers artisan communities while offering customers a chance to experience the diversity and beauty of Indian heritage through every handcrafted piece.
