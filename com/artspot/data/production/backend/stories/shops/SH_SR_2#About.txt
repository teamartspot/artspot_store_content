Veer Sons. was founded in 1987 with a heartfelt mission to preserve and promote the timeless beauty of Chikankari hand embroidery.

Merging heritage craftsmanship with contemporary aesthetics, we create authentic Chikankari pieces on premium, quality-tested fabrics. Every design is brought to life by skilled artisans, inspired by the wisdom of experienced professionals and the fresh vision of emerging designers.

With over three decades of excellence in apparel manufacturing, our founder, Mr. S.K. Jain, has played a pivotal role in shaping the landscape of hand-embroidered fashion. His leadership has enabled Veer Sons. to set new standards in artisanal apparel.

We believe that just like our garments, every individual is unique and deserves to be celebrated.
