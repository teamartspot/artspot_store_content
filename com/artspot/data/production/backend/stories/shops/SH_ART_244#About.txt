Paray’s Walnut Wood Carvings Paray’s Walnut Wood Carvings (Jammu & Kashmir) specialises in intricately carved walnut wood home furnishings, panels and decorative objects reflecting Kashmiri inlay and carving traditions. Artisans produce doors, window panels, jewelry boxes and furniture with hand‑carved vines, chinar leaves and paisley motifs. The workshop often supplies hotels, collectors and restoration projects seeking traditional Kashmiri woodwork. Quality carving and seasoned walnut timber are central to the studio’s reputation.
