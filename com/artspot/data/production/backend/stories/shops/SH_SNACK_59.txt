Monginis traces its origins to a small Italian confectionery established in 1902 in Mumbai by the Mongini family, who introduced European-style cakes and pastries to the city. In the 1950s, the business was acquired by the Khorakiwala family, who transformed it into an Indian brand with a vision of making high-quality cakes and confectionery affordable and accessible to everyone. Through continuous innovation and a strong franchise network, Monginis expanded rapidly across India while maintaining strict quality standards. Today, the brand operates hundreds of exclusive cake shops and offers a wide range of cakes, pastries, chocolates, snacks, and savouries. With over a century of heritage, Monginis continues to celebrate life's special moments by delivering freshness, taste, and happiness to millions of customers.
