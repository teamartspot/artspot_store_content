Saurashtra Farsan traces its origins back to 1923, when Late Shri Narayanbhai Patel started a small farsan shop in Dadar, Mumbai, with the simple aim of bringing smiles through delicious food. His son, Late Shri Bhagwanbhai Patel, expanded the humble shop into a renowned destination for traditional snacks loved by generations. Today, the business is led by Mr. Amit Patel, with the fourth generation, Mr. Shrey Patel, continuing the family's legacy. The brand is dedicated to preserving the authentic flavors of Saurashtra by preparing farsan and sweets using fresh, pure ingredients and time-tested recipes. After more than a century, Saurashtra Farsan remains a trusted name, offering customers a genuine taste of tradition and quality.
