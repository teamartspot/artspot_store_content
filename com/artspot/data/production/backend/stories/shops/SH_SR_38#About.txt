At Amounee, we’re more than just a brand—we’re a movement that connects the soul of rural craftsmanship with a world that values authenticity, sustainability, and human stories.

Founded by a textile designer with years of grassroots experience in artisan villages, Amounee is built on deep respect for the weavers, dyers, embroiderers, and creators who bring India’s cultural legacy to life. We’ve watched women transform thread into poetry under dim lanterns, artisans send their first digital invoices, and families rise from scarcity to stability through their craft.

Each handmade piece in our collection is a celebration of skill, resilience, and heritage. This is not mass-produced fashion. This is intentional, mindful artistry—made with heart, and meant to last.

By choosing Amounee, you’re not just buying a product—you’re investing in the future of India’s handmade traditions and the dignity of those who sustain them.
