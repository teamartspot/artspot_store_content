Villianur Terracotta Works Villianur Terracotta Works (Pondicherry) produces large‑format terracotta sculptures, lamps, garden figures and detailed deity icons using the region’s unique fine green clay. Artisans mould, carve and fire pieces that range from miniature figurines to multi‑foot statues, utilising local clay and sand to achieve distinctive reddish‑brown finishes. The work is known for detailed surface ornamentation and the capacity to create large hollow forms that are lightweight yet robust. Villianur units supply local temples, public art commissions and boutique décor markets across South India.
