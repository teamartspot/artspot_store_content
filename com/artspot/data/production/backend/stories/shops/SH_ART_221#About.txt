Shivanya Handicraft (Jaipur) is a reputed producer of hand‑block printed textiles and Chanderi/Chanderi‑mix sarees, offering a selection of traditional and contemporary fabrics for retail and wholesale customers. The company emphasises natural fibers, craft authenticity and long‑standing production techniques that suit festive and bridal wardrobes. Shivanya maintains online storefronts and trade contacts to reach national buyers and curators. Their catalogue includes sarees, dress materials and furnishing fabrics reflecting Rajasthan’s print heritage.
