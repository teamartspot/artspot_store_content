Kashmir Oriental Carpet Enterprises Kashmir Oriental Carpet Enterprises (Carpets of Kashmir) is a long-standing trader and manufacturer that promotes hand-knotted and handmade Kashmiri carpets and rugs. The company operates an online presence and retail connections in major cities; its collections include silk and wool knotted carpets with traditional Persian-influenced motifs. Kashmir Oriental Carpet Enterprises is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
