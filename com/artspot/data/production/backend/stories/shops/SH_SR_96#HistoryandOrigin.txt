Founded in 2003 by Ms. Jeyasree Ravi, Palam Silks was born from a simple yet bold idea—to transform the perception of Kanchipuram silk sarees from exclusive bridalwear to versatile, everyday elegance.

At the time, silk sarees were mostly reserved for muhurthams (wedding ceremonies), with most women owning just one heirloom piece. Challenging this norm, Jeyasree envisioned Kanchipurams as adaptable to any occasion. This belief led to the launch of four distinct lines:

Casual Kanjeevarams

Designer Kanjeevarams

Bridal Kanjeevarams

Concept Kanjeevarams

While preserving the sanctity of the silk and its weave, she dared to experiment with colors, motifs, and layouts, making the fabric more relevant to modern sensibilities. Recognizing the discomfort caused by the weight of traditional sarees, she collaborated with her master weaver to re-engineer the fabric, ultimately giving birth to Lightweight Kanjeevarams—a revolutionary innovation in the silk industry.

Today, Palam stands as a brand that respects tradition but isn’t afraid to innovate, offering sarees that are timeless, wearable, and incredibly elegant.
