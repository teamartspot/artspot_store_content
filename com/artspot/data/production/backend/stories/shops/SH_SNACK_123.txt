Chitale Dairy was founded in 1939 by Late Shri Bhaskar Ganesh Chitale in Bhilawadi, Sangli, with a vision of supplying pure, high-quality milk while improving the lives of dairy farmers. What began as a small milk pasteurization unit has grown into one of India's most trusted dairy brands through decades of innovation and customer trust. Built on a farmer-first philosophy, the company works with over 50,000 farmers, providing veterinary care, technical guidance, and sustainable dairy practices. Combining traditional values with modern technology, Chitale Dairy produces a wide range of milk, ghee, butter, paneer, shrikhand, cheese, and other dairy products under strict quality standards. Today, with over 87 years of dairy excellence, Chitale Dairy continues to enrich lives by delivering purity, nutrition, and authentic taste to millions of consumers.
