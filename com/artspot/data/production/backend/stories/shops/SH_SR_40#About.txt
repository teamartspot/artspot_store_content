Founded in 2017, Taneira is a celebration of India’s timeless textiles and heritage craftsmanship. A premium ethnic wear label, Vivira brings together handpicked sarees, elegant dress materials, and ready-to-wear kurtas—all made from pure, natural fabrics. With a presence across major cities and an expansive online platform, Vivira is a destination where tradition meets style for today’s Indian woman.

Vivira offers curated collections from over 100 weaving clusters across the country, spotlighting the rich diversity of Indian artistry. Whether it’s for festive occasions, everyday elegance, or professional attire, each piece is crafted with precision to suit every facet of a woman’s life.

Designed for the rooted yet contemporary individual, Vivira embodies a design philosophy that merges classic Indian techniques with modern silhouettes, ensuring each garment is not just worn—but remembered.
