Ruchkar was founded by Chef Tushar Deshmukh and Priti Deshmukh with the vision of preserving and sharing authentic Maharashtrian cuisine with food lovers everywhere. What began as a passion for traditional recipes has grown into a trusted food brand offering handcrafted spices, chutneys, masalas, and ready-to-cook products. Drawing inspiration from generations of family recipes and regional culinary traditions, Ruchkar emphasizes authentic taste, premium ingredients, and uncompromising quality. The brand combines traditional cooking methods with modern packaging to make home-style Maharashtrian flavors accessible to every kitchen. Today, Ruchkar continues its mission of celebrating Maharashtra's rich food heritage while delivering convenient, flavorful products to customers across India.
