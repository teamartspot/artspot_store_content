The India Craft House is a digital platform dedicated to preserving and celebrating some of the world’s oldest and most intricate craft traditions. Founded in 2010 in Gurgaon, India, we began our journey as a social enterprise with a singular purpose: to showcase and support the unparalleled artistry of Indian artisans. Every handcrafted product we offer is a reflection of this purpose — steeped in heritage, shaped by skill, and thoughtfully reimagined for the contemporary world. We are committed to empowering artisans across the country by helping them create sustainable livelihoods while keeping their age-old traditions alive. Each piece carries not only the beauty of traditional Indian craftsmanship but also the story and soul of the artisan behind it.
