Rajmilan Mithai is one of Jalna's oldest and most trusted sweet brands, built by the Rajpurohit family, whose legacy spans three generations of craftsmanship and dedication. What began as a traditional mithai business grew through perseverance, an unwavering commitment to quality, and authentic recipes, earning the trust of generations of customers. Over the years, the brand expanded its offerings to include premium dry-fruit sweets, Bengali sweets, mava sweets, namkeen, and festive delicacies while preserving its rich heritage. Today, Rajmilan Mithai continues its journey by blending tradition with modern retail, carrying forward a legacy of taste, trust, and authenticity that has become synonymous with celebrations in Jalna.
