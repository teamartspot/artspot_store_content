Gurpreet Phulkari Store Gurpreet Phulkari Store evolved from small-scale family retail in Patiala’s historic bazaars into a recognised phulkari emporium, serving generations of customers. Over the years the proprietors strengthened ties with rural Phulkari stitchers to ensure a regular supply of authentic embroidered goods. The store’s continuity reflects Patiala’s role as a regional centre for Punjabi textile traditions and the sustained demand for hand‑embroidered ceremonial garments.
