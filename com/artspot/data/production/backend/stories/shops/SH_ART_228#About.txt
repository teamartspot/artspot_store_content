Shree Om Jari Shree Om Jari is a Surat-based manufacturer of polyester and metallic zari threads, embroidery yarns and related textile inputs serving the garment and embroidery industry. The company supplies a wide range of metallic yarns, kasab threads and speciality monofilaments used by zari and embroidery workshops across India. Products are marketed to textile manufacturers, hand-embroidery units and exporters who use zari for embellishment on sarees and ceremonial garments. Shree Om Jari positions itself on product quality and consistent filament manufacture for downstream users in Surat’s large textile value chain.
