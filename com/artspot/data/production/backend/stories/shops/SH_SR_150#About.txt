Mehers Handlooms is a renowned name in the world of traditional Orissa handlooms, celebrated for its exceptional craftsmanship and dedication to preserving the heritage of handwoven sarees. Led by the visionary duo, Shilpaguru Padmashree Chaturbhuj Meher and his son Shri Sankar Kumar Meher, the brand has played a pivotal role in reviving and popularizing the intricate Ikat (Bandha) weaving art. Their unmatched creativity and contribution have earned them national recognition, placing Mehers Handlooms as a trusted destination for authentic, handcrafted textiles.
