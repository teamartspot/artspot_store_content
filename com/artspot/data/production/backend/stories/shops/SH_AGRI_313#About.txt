Radhaa Aamrai began with a vision to establish Vidarbha’s first large ultra-high-density Kesar mango orchard in Amravati, combining traditional farming wisdom with modern agricultural innovation. Built by founders Dr. Archana Gole and Dr. Sachiin Gole, the venture focuses on premium organic Kesar mango cultivation using natural farming methods and advanced irrigation techniques. With over 10,000 mango trees spread across extensive farmland, the brand emphasizes chemical-free, naturally ripened, export-quality produce. Their journey reflects a commitment to sustainable farming, scientific cultivation, and delivering authentic Maharashtra-grown Kesar mangoes directly to customers in India and global markets.
