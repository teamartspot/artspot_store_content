Rajam Snacks began its journey on the bustling streets of Mumbai, where its founder started with a humble cart, preparing authentic South Indian namkeen using cherished family recipes. With determination and a passion for traditional flavors, the snacks were sold door-to-door, winning customers through their freshness and homemade taste. Guided by the vision of creating the finest South Indian snack brand, Rajam Snacks has grown from those modest beginnings into a trusted household name. The company combines time-honored recipes with modern manufacturing to preserve the rich culinary heritage of South India while meeting contemporary tastes. Today, Rajam Snacks continues to delight customers with premium-quality snacks that bring people together through authentic flavor and tradition.
