Kondaji Chivda was founded in 1920 by Kondaji Gunaji Wavre in Nashik, Maharashtra, after he left his government job to create a unique Maharashtrian snack. Starting by selling his handmade chivda door-to-door, he won customers through its authentic taste and quality. Over four generations, the family has preserved the original recipe while expanding the brand across Nashik and beyond. Today, Wavre Bandhu continues the legacy by offering traditional varieties such as Kondaji Chivda, Makhmal Chivda, and Krushna Bhel, prepared with premium ingredients and time-honored methods. With more than a century of heritage, Kondaji Chivda remains one of Nashik's most iconic snack brands, celebrated for its authentic flavor and enduring tradition.
