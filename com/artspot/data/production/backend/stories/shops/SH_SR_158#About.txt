Khatushyam Fashion Private Limited, under the brand name KCPC Bandhani, is a distinguished name in the world of Bandhani and Gota Patti craftsmanship. Known for pushing creative boundaries while preserving traditional essence, the brand has transformed the face of women’s ethnic fashion in India. With continuous innovation at its core, KCPC Bandhani offers an exquisite range of Marwadi Pila Chunri, Stoles, Odhanas, Kurtis, Dupattas, Sarees, Suits, and Lehengas — each piece crafted with precision and rich cultural artistry.

Our garments stand out for their thoughtful design, premium quality, and a perfect blend of contemporary style with traditional grace. By maintaining affordability without compromising on quality, we’ve built a product range that resonates with women across generations and lifestyles.
