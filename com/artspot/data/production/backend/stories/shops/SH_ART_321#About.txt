Himalayan Weavers is a Dehradun-based handloom and textile store specialising in Kashmiri shawls, pashminas, and handwoven textiles sourced from Himalayan weaving traditions. The boutique stocks shawls, wraps and woollen accessories and caters to tourists and domestic buyers seeking authentic Himalayan textiles. The store participates in local fairs and maintains supplier links with weaving communities in the hills.
