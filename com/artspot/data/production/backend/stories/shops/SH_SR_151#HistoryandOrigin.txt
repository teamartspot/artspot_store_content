Rooted in the cultural heart of Odisha, Sanskriti Cuttack was born from a desire to keep the spirit of traditional handloom weaving alive. Odisha, known for its centuries-old weaving legacy, has long been a hub of intricate techniques and symbolic patterns. Sanskriti Cuttack draws from this rich history, sourcing directly from regions like Gopalpur, Berhampur, and tribal communities to bring authentic sarees to the world. The brand was founded on the principles of sustainability, artisan empowerment, and cultural preservation — bridging the past and the present through timeless handloom creations. Each piece is a tribute to Odisha’s artistic spirit and enduring craftsmanship.
