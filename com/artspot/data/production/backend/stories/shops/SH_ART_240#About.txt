Bagru Block Printers Bagru Block Printers refers to the cluster of artisans and cooperative units in Bagru that practice centuries‑old hand‑block printing, notably Dabu and Syahi‑Begar techniques. These printers produce saris, dress materials, furnishings and home textiles using vegetable dyes and carved wooden blocks. Collections from Bagru embody natural hues, resist‑dye textures and classic tree‑of‑life and floral motifs associated with the region. Many Bagru printers also offer workshops and experiential visits for visitors interested in traditional print methods.
