Shyama bandhani center shyama Bandhani Centre Shyama Bandhani Centre is a Jamnagar-based Bandhani retailer and design house specialising in traditional tie-dye textiles and sarees. The shop showcases a large collection of Bandhani patterns and is listed on local directories with shop details and contact information for retail buyers and visitors. Shyama bandhani center is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
