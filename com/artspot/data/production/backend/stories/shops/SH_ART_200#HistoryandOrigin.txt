Founded in the 2010s in Gurgaon, The India Craft House began as a small effort to connect traditional artisans with modern retail and e-commerce channels. Over the years it expanded its network across multiple states and craft clusters, formalising partnerships with community groups and cooperatives. Its growth reflects the broader movement to digitise craft sales and improve market access for rural producers. By investing in design, quality control and storytelling, the platform has helped elevate lesser-known craft forms into premium consumer segments.
