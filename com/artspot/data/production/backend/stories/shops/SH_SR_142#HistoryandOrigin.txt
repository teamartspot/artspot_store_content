Established in 2003 by Mr. Phalguni Das and his two brothers, Santu and Mantu, Kaniska began as a humble shop near the historic temples of Bishnupur in Ward No. 3. Inspired by a family tradition of saree-making and a deep-rooted passion for craftsmanship, the founders took on the challenge of building a brand that celebrated local weaving traditions.

Initially offering Bishnupur Silk, Than, Tant Print, Baluchari, and Swarnachari sarees, the shop quickly earned the trust of customers and tourists alike. In 2012, Kaniska expanded to a larger store, increasing its collection and enhancing the shopping experience. By 2013, the store was officially reopened at its current location with a stronger commitment to preserving Bishnupur’s handloom heritage.

Driven by dedication, quality, and customer trust, Kaniska has grown into not just a local favorite but also an emerging online presence—bringing the legacy of Bishnupur’s handwoven sarees to customers across the globe.
