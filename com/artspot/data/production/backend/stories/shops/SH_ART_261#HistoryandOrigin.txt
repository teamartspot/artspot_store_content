N.Rajendran Veenai house The N. Rajendran Veenai House evolved from family expertise in veena-making in Thanjavur, a noted centre for classical music and instrument craftsmanship. Over the years the workshop adapted traditional carving methods to produce concert-grade veenas and to service the demands of modern performers. The shop’s continuity is rooted in local luthier apprenticeships and long-standing relationships with music academies and temple musicians in the region.
