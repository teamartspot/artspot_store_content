Karachi Sweet Mart was founded in 1948 by Tarachand Athwani, who rebuilt his life and business in Pune after the Partition of India. Starting from humble beginnings, he established the brand with a commitment to preserving authentic Sindhi and Indian sweets, savouries, and bakery products using traditional recipes and uncompromising quality. The next generation strengthened this legacy, transforming Karachi Sweet Mart into one of Pune's most trusted names for festive sweets, snacks, and corporate gifting. Blending decades of craftsmanship with modern hygiene, packaging, and large-scale production, the brand continues to serve families, businesses, and institutions across India. Today, with over 75 years of heritage, Karachi Sweet Mart remains a symbol of resilience, tradition, and authentic taste.
