Naturals Ice Cream was founded in 1984 by Raghunandan S. Kamath, who transformed his lifelong knowledge of fruits, gained while helping his father, a mango vendor, into a unique ice cream business. Starting with a small 400 sq. ft. outlet in Juhu, Mumbai, he introduced the concept of ice cream made with just milk, fruits, and sugar, without artificial flavours, colours, or preservatives. The brand quickly became known for its authentic fruit-based flavours and commitment to natural ingredients. Over the years, Naturals has grown into one of India's most loved artisanal ice cream brands, with outlets across the country while staying true to its original philosophy. Today, Naturals continues to delight generations of customers by celebrating the natural taste of fresh fruits in every scoop.
