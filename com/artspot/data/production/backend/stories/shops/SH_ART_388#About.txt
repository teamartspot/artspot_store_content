Gaatha Handicrafts is an Ahmedabad-based social enterprise promoting Indian crafts and artisan products online. 
It functions as an e-commerce platform connecting rural craftspeople with urban and global buyers. 
The initiative emphasizes authenticity, fair trade and cultural storytelling in handicrafts. 
Gaatha lists a wide range of verified craft categories including textiles, pottery, metalwork and woodwork. 
It has been covered in media and recognized as a major online platform for Indian crafts.
