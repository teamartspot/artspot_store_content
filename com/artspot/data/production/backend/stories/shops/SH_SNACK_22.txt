Manjiri Masaale was established in 2018 with the vision of delivering authentic Maharashtrian spices made from carefully selected, high-quality ingredients. Under the leadership of Mr. Shankar Dange Patil, the company began manufacturing specialty products such as Kala Masala, Dry Red Chilli Powder, and Teja Red Chilli Powder, focusing on purity, rich aroma, and traditional taste. Built on a commitment to quality and customer satisfaction, the brand combines time-tested spice blends with hygienic processing and modern packaging. Today, Manjiri Masaale continues to grow as a trusted spice manufacturer, bringing the homemade flavors of Maharashtra to kitchens across India.
