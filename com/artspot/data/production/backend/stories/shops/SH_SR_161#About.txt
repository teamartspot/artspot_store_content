BandhaniTreasure is a proud branch of our parent unit, Kachchh Art Gallery, based in Gandhidham, Kachchh, Gujarat. Since 1984, we have specialized in creating exquisite handcrafted Bandhani products including sarees, lehenga-cholis, dupattas, dress materials, and fabrics. Our mission is to offer an exceptional customer experience and outstanding value, while passionately exceeding expectations. Each purchase you make directly supports a community of around 275 skilled women artisans who are the heart of our craft.
