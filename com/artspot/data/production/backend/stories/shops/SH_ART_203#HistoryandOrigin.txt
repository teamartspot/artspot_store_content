Cilwana Studio registered as a private limited company in Jammu & Kashmir and grew from the founders’ interest in revitalising Kashmiri textiles through contemporary design. Its directors, Nusaiba and Adil Masood, leverage local artisan skills and family networks to produce embroidered apparel. As a relatively new venture, Cilwana focuses on creating market access for Kashmiri craftsmanship while fostering design-led adaptations of traditional techniques.
