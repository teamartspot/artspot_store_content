Established in 2025 in Beed, Maharashtra, Shree Ganeshay Import Export began as a fresh vision to carry the richness of India’s farmlands beyond borders, transforming naturally grown fruits, vegetables, peanuts, and turmeric into trusted global offerings. Built on a promise of freshness, quality, and wholesome nutrition, the company reflects the energy of a new-age agro enterprise rooted in traditional farming values. With a worldwide outlook and a dedication to delivering nature’s best with care, Shree Ganeshay’s story is one of ambition taking root quickly, where every harvest becomes a symbol of purity, reliability, and India’s agricultural abundance.
