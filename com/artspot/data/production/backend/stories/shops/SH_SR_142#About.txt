Kaniska is one of the oldest and most reputed garment shops in Bishnupur, located in the Bankura district of West Bengal, India. Known for its exquisite collection of handmade Baluchari and Swarnachari sarees, Kaniska is a trusted name among saree enthusiasts and tourists visiting the culturally rich town of Bishnupur—famous for its terracotta temples and traditional crafts.

Situated at Lalbandh, near Pamananda College in Bishnupur Center, the shop offers a versatile collection of sarees that blend heritage craftsmanship with contemporary elegance. With its own manufacturing unit, Kaniska ensures unmatched quality, showcasing hand-stitched sarees crafted by skilled local artisans. Every piece reflects the rich weaving legacy and artistic soul of Bishnupur.
