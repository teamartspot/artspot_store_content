RT Patil Yalgud Kolhapur trades in Hupari–Yalgud silver jewellery with a focus on anklets and chains. 
Yalgud artisans supply components and finished pieces to nearby retailers. 
The shop’s assortment includes oxidized textures and polished finishes for different tastes. 
Customers include local families and traveling buyers sourcing cluster-made ornaments. 
The business aligns with Hupari’s reputation for dependable silver craftsmanship.
