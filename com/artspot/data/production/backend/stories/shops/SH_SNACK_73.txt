Ajit Bakery was established in 1955 in Nagpur, Maharashtra, with a vision of offering fresh, high-quality bakery products and traditional sweets to customers. What started as a family-owned bakery has grown into one of Nagpur's most trusted bakery brands through its commitment to taste, quality, and innovation. Over the decades, the company has expanded its product range to include breads, cakes, cookies, rusks, namkeen, sweets, and customized celebration cakes. By blending traditional recipes with modern baking techniques and maintaining strict hygiene standards, Ajit Bakery has earned the loyalty of generations of customers. Today, with multiple outlets across Nagpur, the brand continues to delight families with fresh, delicious, and affordable bakery products.
