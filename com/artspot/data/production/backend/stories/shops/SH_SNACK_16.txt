Suraj Masale was founded by the Ujawane family with the vision of bringing authentic Indian spice flavors into every household through high-quality, traditionally crafted masalas. Inspired by a passion for enhancing everyday cooking, the company built the "Suraj" brand on the principles of uncompromising quality, consistency, and rich regional taste. Guided by the leadership of Mr. Jayant Ujawane and honoring the legacy of Late Shri Jainarayan Surajlal Ujawane, the business has grown into a trusted spice manufacturer in Bhandara. Beyond producing premium spices, the company also contributes to local employment and community development while continuing its commitment to preserving the authentic flavors of Indian cuisine.
