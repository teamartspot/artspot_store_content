In 1974, in the emerald fields of Kerala, two farmers chose courage over compromise. When their harvest was undervalued, T.V. Pathrose and Mary Pathrose decided to mill their own paddy and bring it directly to families who would appreciate its true worth. From that quiet act of determination, Mother’s Food was born.
What began as a humble rice mill grew, grain by grain, into a trusted name carried across generations. Rooted in respect for farmers and guided by the warmth of home-cooked tradition, Mother’s Food blends time-honoured wisdom with modern precision, ensuring every product is pure, honest, and nourishing.
Today, from rice to flours, spices, and wholesome kitchen essentials, each offering carries the same promise first made in those Kerala fields: food crafted with integrity, served with care, and worthy of every family table.
Because when nourishment matters most, it feels like Mother’s Food.
