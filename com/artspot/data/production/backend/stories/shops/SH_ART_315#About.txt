K. Lakshmi Narayan & R. Krishnamurthy are noted artisan names associated with Karuppur and Kallakurichi’s wood carving and Kalamkari ecosystems; they represent master craftsmen and workshops producing temple panels, carved furniture and wood‑carved iconography. Their work is supplied to temples, galleries and private commissions, reflecting the technical skill of the Karuppur woodcraft cluster. Listings in craft directories and GI documentation reference skilled families and workshops in the Karuppur area that supply such carved works.
