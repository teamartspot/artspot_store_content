Ramdev Food Products began its journey in 1965, when Shri Hasmukhbhai Patel started a small spice business with a vision of delivering pure, high-quality Indian spices to every household. Built on values of authenticity, trust, and uncompromising quality, the company steadily expanded from a local enterprise into one of India's leading spice and food brands. By combining traditional spice-blending expertise with modern manufacturing technology and strict quality standards, Ramdev has diversified into spices, blended masalas, hing, instant mixes, and other food products. Over the decades, the brand has earned the confidence of millions of consumers through its consistent focus on purity, taste, and innovation. Today, Ramdev Food Products continues to carry forward its legacy by bringing authentic Indian flavours to homes across India and around the world.
