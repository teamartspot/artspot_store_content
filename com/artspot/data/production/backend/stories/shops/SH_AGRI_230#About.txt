In the fertile landscapes of Assam, where agriculture shapes everyday life, Amberland Farmers Producer Company Limited began its journey in 2022 as a collective effort to uplift farming communities. Rooted in Hojai, it brings together farmers with a shared vision to grow, process, and deliver agricultural and horticultural produce with purpose and care. What makes its story unique is its wide horizon, handling everything from cultivation to packaging and export, turning local harvests into global opportunities. Each spice, grain, and fruit reflects the richness of the region, backed by improved practices and shared knowledge. Connecting farmers to better markets and resources, it creates not just produce, but progress. More than a company, it is a growing network where collaboration drives change. In every harvest, Amberland carries a quiet mission to transform agriculture into a path of collective prosperity.
