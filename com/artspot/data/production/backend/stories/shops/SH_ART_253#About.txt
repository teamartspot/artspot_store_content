The Pattamadai Fine Mat Wcs Ltd The Pattamadai Fine Mat Wcs Ltd produces the iconic Pattamadai mats (Pattamadai Pai) woven from Korai grass. These mats are celebrated for their fine weaving, softness, and cooling qualities. Popular as ritual mats, floor coverings, and ceremonial gifts, they are also customised with names or designs for weddings and special occasions. Based in Tirunelveli district, Tamil Nadu, the cooperative has sustained the craft and brought it to broader domestic and global audiences.
