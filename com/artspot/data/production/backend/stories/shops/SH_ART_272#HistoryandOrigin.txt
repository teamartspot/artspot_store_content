Surendra Saddlery Surendra Saddlery emerged from Kanpur's leather trade and saddlery heritage, growing into a supplier for regional equestrian needs. The firm invested in production processes and export compliance to widen its client base, while maintaining artisanal training for saddle-making and leather finishing skills. Surendra Saddlery is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
