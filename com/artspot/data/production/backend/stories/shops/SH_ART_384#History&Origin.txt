Sawant Bhonsle sawantwadi has long been known for wooden toys and Ganjifa cards, historically patronized by the Sawant Bhonsle royal family. The craft continues in the region today. Sawant Bhonsle is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
