Two decades ago, DS Agrifoods began with a simple yet ambitious vision to bring the world the true taste of India’s finest rice. From the fertile plains of Pilibhit in Uttar Pradesh, nestled near the foothills of the Himalayas, the journey began with dedication, craftsmanship, and a deep respect for the grain.
Over the years, tradition has been refined by modern precision. Advanced milling technology from Japan and Germany works alongside meticulous quality care to ensure every grain reflects purity, aroma, and perfection. From everyday meals to celebratory feasts, DS Agrifoods curates rice that elevates the dining experience.
Family-owned yet globally minded, DS Agrifoods blends heritage with innovation guided by trust, sustainability, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Each harvest carries the same promise that began the journey: authentic rice, crafted with integrity, and shared with the world.
