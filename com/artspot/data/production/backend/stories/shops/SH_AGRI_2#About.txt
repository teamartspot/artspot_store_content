Rooted in Darjeeling’s timeless tea heritage, Mayukh Tea is a celebration of craft, patience, and provenance. Born from decades of close relationships with revered tea gardens, each blend reflects a legacy shaped by trust and an uncompromising pursuit of excellence.


At Mayukh Tea, this heritage unfolds slowly—over warm brews, lingering conversations, and golden sunsets. Here, tea is not rushed; it is savored. Every cup carries the calm elegance of the hills and the quiet luxury of Darjeeling’s finest harvests.
