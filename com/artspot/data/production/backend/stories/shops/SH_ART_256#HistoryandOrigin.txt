Bundelkhand Bell Metal Handicraft The Bundelkhand bell metal tradition is centuries old, with Datia and Tikamgarh as its main centres. Artisans use traditional moulding and casting processes, often working in small workshops or households. Historically, these items were part of local household and ritual culture, and later became recognised for their artisanal value. The craft has been included in state and national craft promotion schemes to support its survival in modern markets.
