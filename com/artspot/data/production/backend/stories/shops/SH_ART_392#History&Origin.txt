Samaguri Satra was established in the 17th century under the neo-Vaishnavite movement of Srimanta Sankardev. 
Its monks created masks to narrate religious stories in bhawna performances. 
The mask craft evolved with symbolic, artistic and devotional value. 
Over centuries, Samaguri Satra became synonymous with Majuli’s identity as a cultural island. 
Today, the satra preserves this craft with workshops, training and international recognition.
