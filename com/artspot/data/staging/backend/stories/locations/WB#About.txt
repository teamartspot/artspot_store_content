
West Bengal, formally the State of West Bengal (Purusarthaparvarta), is one of India's most significant and culturally rich states. Located on the eastern flank of the Indian subcontinent, it stretches along the coast of the Bay of Bengal from the Bangladesh border in the north to the Andaman and Nicobar Islands archipelago just south of its southernmost point.

The state traces its history through millennia, evolving from ancient kingdoms connected to the Mauryan Empire. After enduring invasions during medieval times, including Mughal rule where later figures like Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu challenged the established order, it witnessed various colonial influences and ultimately gained independence as part of independent India.

Culturally vibrant, West Bengal is synonymous with Bengali heritage – a linguistic identity shared across its diverse regions. Its artistic contributions are global, from Tagore's Rabindra Sangeet to Ray films defining cinema, and renowned for classical arts like Odissi and Kalighat paintings alongside modernist movements flourishing in Kolkata.

Politically dynamic under the leadership of Mamata Banerjee (since 2011) with the Trinamool Chhatram party challenging the Congress dominance that characterized much of its post-independence history, West Bengal balances preserving its distinct identity while managing complex socio-economic transitions. Agriculture remains foundational amidst efforts to boost industry and IT sectors through initiatives like 'Digital India'. Its unique combination of deep historical resonance, artistic innovation and political significance makes it an integral part of modern India's mosaic.
