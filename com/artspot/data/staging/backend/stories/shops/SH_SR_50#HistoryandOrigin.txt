Wendell Wendell Rodricks began his journey in an unexpected place—with a degree in catering and his first job in Muscat in 1982. But it was fashion that called to him. Leaving behind a high-paying job, he studied design in Los Angeles and Paris, returning to India in 1988 to help shape an industry still in its infancy.

By 1989, he launched his eponymous label in Mumbai and quickly rose to fame with his minimalist aesthetic. He designed for major names like Garden Vareli and Lakme Cosmetics, and was the first Indian invited to international fashion platforms including IGEDO and Paris Fashion Week. He even redefined everyday fashion by designing police uniforms and leading the movement for eco-friendly, wearable couture.

Wendell Rodricks also played a key role in organizing the inaugural Lakme Fashion Week in 2000 and mentored future talents such as Schulen Fernandes. His creative pursuits went beyond the runway—he acted in films, contributed to travel and food journals, and authored celebrated works like Moda Goa and The Green Room.

In his later years, Wendell devoted himself to the dream of preserving Goa’s rich sartorial heritage. He restored his 450-year-old home in Colvale into the Moda Goa Museum and Research Centre, housing over 800 rare exhibits. Though he passed away in 2020, his work—rooted in design, activism, and storytelling—lives on through The Wendell Rodricks Legacy, an enduring ode to his life's journey and purpose.
