Saklain's Coterie Saklain's Coterie is a well-known small retail store in Polo View/Pool View market, Srinagar, specialising in natural cosmetics, saffron, essential oils and niche Kashmiri specialty products. The store also serves as a curated boutique for visitors seeking authentic regional produce and handmade gifts, with active social listings and positive visitor reviews. Saklain's Coterie is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
