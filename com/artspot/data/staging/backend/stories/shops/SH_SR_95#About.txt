Sundari Silks, established in 1998 in Chennai, has deep roots in a family with over 80 years of experience in traditional silk craftsmanship. The brand curates an exclusive range of handwoven silk sarees—such as Kanjivaram, Banarasi, Mysore silk, and more—blending timeless elegance with modern design sensibilities.

Their retail presence now spans premium showrooms in Chennai (T. Nagar & Adyar) and Mumbai (Vile Parle). Sundari Silks is renowned for its immersive retail experiences—every store features thoughtfully designed interiors that elevate the ambience and showcase each silk piece's story.

They further enrich their collection with artisanal jewelry, bridal wear, and summer wardrobe staples, all featuring light, breathable weaves for every season.
