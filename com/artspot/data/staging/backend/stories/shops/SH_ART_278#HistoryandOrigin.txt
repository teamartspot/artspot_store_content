Fancy Emporium Fancy Emporium is part of Khurja’s long ceramic commerce tradition: small family-run emporia have sold locally produced pots, tableware and decorative items for decades. The store’s presence on local directories and social platforms underscores its role as a neighborhood dealer within Khurja's wider pottery ecosystem, supplying both retail and trade demands. Fancy Emporium is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
