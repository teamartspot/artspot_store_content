In the misty highlands of Chikmagalur, where coffee first took root on Indian soil, Kalmane Koffees carries forward a legacy that began in 1879, one of the earliest estates cultivated by Indian hands.
Across generations, this estate has quietly perfected the art of coffee nurturing Arabica beans in rich mountain soil, and transforming them through time-honoured methods into brews of remarkable depth and balance. From handpicked harvests to small-batch roasting, every step is guided by patience, precision, and an intimate understanding of the bean.
What sets Kalmane apart is its seamless journey from estate to cup, where freshness is preserved, authenticity is protected, and every sip reflects its true origin.
Kalmane Koffees is more than a brew; it is a century-old story, gently roasted into every cup, where heritage, craftsmanship, and the quiet luxury of fine coffee come together.
