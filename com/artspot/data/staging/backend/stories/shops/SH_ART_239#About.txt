Ranjana Hand Printers Ranjana Hand Printers (Bagru, Jaipur) manufactures hand‑block printed sarees, dress materials and home textiles using traditional Bagru blocks and natural dyes. The unit offers a variety of prints—from Dabu resist patterns to Syahi‑Begar alizarin styles—and sells to retailers and direct customers. Ranjana emphasizes artisan skill, hand‑carved blocks and small‑batch production to preserve authenticity and texture in each piece. The company supplies both domestic boutiques and online craft platforms that curate Bagru textiles.
