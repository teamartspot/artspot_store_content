The journey of Anita Dongre began with a vision to redefine luxury through the lens of tradition and purpose. Inspired by India’s rich textile legacy and motivated by a deep respect for artisanship, Ridhima Verma set out to create a label that honors both the craft and the community behind it.

What started as a small design studio has grown into a respected fashion house with a global presence. The label has carved its identity by integrating handwoven fabrics, sustainable practices, and a modern design ethos.

Rooted in Indian aesthetics and influenced by global sensibilities, Anita Dongre has become a name synonymous with elegance, ethics, and excellence—empowering not just the wearer, but also the hands that create each masterpiece.
