Boyanika Collective, officially known as the Odisha State Handloom Weavers Cooperative Society Ltd., has been a cornerstone of Odisha’s handloom ecosystem for over 68 years. Operating under the aegis of the Handloom, Textiles & Handicrafts Department, Government of Odisha, the organization functions as a dedicated marketing and support platform for the state’s Primary Weavers Cooperative Societies (PWCS).

Since its inception in 1956, Boyanika Collective has worked tirelessly to uplift over 30,000 weavers and an equal number of allied artisans by offering access to raw materials, modern tools, design innovation, and robust market linkages. Through its network of member societies, self-help groups, master weavers, and award-winning artisans, the collective ensures that Odisha's handwoven traditions are not only preserved but also promoted on national and international platforms.

Its commitment extends to organizing National Handloom Expos, buyer-seller meets, and export-driven production initiatives, making it a leading name in the revival and global recognition of Odisha's textile artistry.
