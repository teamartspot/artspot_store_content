In the lush, rain-kissed hills of Coorg, where spice trails have shaped history and flavor for centuries, Green Coorg Spices brings the essence of this land directly from plantation to pantry. Rooted in a region celebrated for its rich spice heritage, the brand bridges the journey between local farmers and discerning kitchens with authenticity and care.
From handpicked Malabar pepper, fragrant cardamom, and other prized harvests to artisanal delights like honey, coffee, and homemade specialties, each offering reflects the purity, aroma, and character of Coorg’s fertile soil. Carefully sourced, rigorously selected, and thoughtfully delivered, every product carries the freshness of its origin.
More than a spice destination, Green Coorg Spices is a celebration of Coorg’s living heritage where tradition meets thoughtful craftsmanship, and every flavor tells a story of land, culture, and quiet excellence.
