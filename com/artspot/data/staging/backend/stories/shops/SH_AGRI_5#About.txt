Established in 1940 in the heart of Darjeeling, The Tea Emporium stands just below the historic grounds where Dr. Campbell first planted tea seeds over a century ago—marking the dawn of Darjeeling tea. From these mist-covered hills emerged a tradition that would define the world’s finest teas.


Guided by generations of experience and refined by expert tea tasters, The Tea Emporium carefully selects exceptional early-season teas from renowned gardens each year. Rooted in trust and elevated by craftsmanship, their legacy has been shaped by one belief—true reputation is earned through uncompromising quality and customer satisfaction.


Today, across retail, wholesale, and global exports, The Tea Emporium continues to offer timeless teas—authentic, elegant, and unmistakably Darjeeling
