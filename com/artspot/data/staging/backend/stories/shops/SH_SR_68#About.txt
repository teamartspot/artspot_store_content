At Craftick, our core mission is to celebrate and preserve the exquisite art of Tilla work, a regal embroidery tradition deeply rooted in Kashmiri culture. The name "Craftick" reflects our reverence for skill, capturing the essence of the craftsmanship behind each hand-embroidered creation.

Craftick was founded on the belief that true art demands time, patience, and dedication. As someone raised in a family that understood these values, I was entrusted with the responsibility of taking this heritage forward. Every piece—from Pashmina shawls to sarees, suits, dupattas, and pherans—is a tribute to the mastery of artisans who continue to keep this rare and beautiful craft alive.
