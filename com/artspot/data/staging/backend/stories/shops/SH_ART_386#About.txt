Raj Stores & Gift Center is a Sawantwadi-based retail shop dealing in traditional wooden toys, lacquerware and household gift items. 
The store appears in local trade listings and has been mentioned in directories connected with Sawantwadi craft. 
It offers a range of decorative and functional wooden articles, often painted in bright lacquer colors. 
The shop caters to both local customers and tourists who visit Sindhudurg district. 
Raj Stores & Gift Center reflects the living tradition of Sawantwadi’s famous toy market.
