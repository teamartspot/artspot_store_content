River of Rugs River of Rugs grew out of Bhadohi’s carpet cluster, which has been a centre for carpet weaving for decades and contributes heavily to India’s carpet exports. Established to connect local weavers with larger buyers, the company professionalised production workflows including design, dyeing and finishing. Over time, River of Rugs expanded its reach through online listings and participation in trade channels, helping artisans access wider markets. The business reflects broader modernization in Bhadohi where traditional skills are combined with export-oriented manufacturing.
