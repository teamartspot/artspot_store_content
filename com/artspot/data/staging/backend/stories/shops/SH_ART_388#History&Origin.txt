Gaatha was founded in Gujarat by design and management professionals aiming to document craft heritage. 
The enterprise draws inspiration from the Sanskrit word “Gaatha,” meaning story. 
It collaborates with NGOs, artisan clusters and state craft councils to preserve living traditions. 
The platform highlights craft origins, techniques and artisan communities through curated content. 
By linking storytelling with e-commerce, Gaatha has helped revive interest in endangered crafts across India.
