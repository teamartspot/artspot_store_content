The India Craft House is a Gurgaon-based social enterprise and online platform dedicated to promoting India’s traditional handcrafts to domestic and international customers. It curates a wide range of artisanal products—textiles, home décor, and lifestyle items—sourced directly from craft communities, cooperatives and master artisans. The organisation emphasizes fair trade practices, transparent pricing and sustainable production, working closely with makers to improve livelihoods and preserve techniques. Through collaborations with designers and craft clusters it adapts heritage forms for contemporary markets while maintaining artisanal authenticity. The India Craft House also runs initiatives to document craft processes and supports artisan welfare programs.
