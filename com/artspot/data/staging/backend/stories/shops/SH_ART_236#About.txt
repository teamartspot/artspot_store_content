Phulkari Creations Phulkari Creations is a Punjab-based retailer and wholesaler of Phulkari embroidered dupattas, suits and home textiles, known for hand-embroidered traditional motifs and contemporary design variants. They supply retail customers and bulk orders for boutiques, and maintain a presence on social platforms and local business directories for direct sales and enquiries. Their product range includes bridal Phulkari, decorative wall hangings and stitched garments reflecting Punjab’s embroidery heritage.
