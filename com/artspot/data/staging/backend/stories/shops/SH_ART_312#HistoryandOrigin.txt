Gajam Govardhana hails from Puttapaka where the Telia Rumal double‑ikat technique has been practised for generations; he helped revive and popularise the craft nationally. His career includes awards such as the Padma Shri and UNESCO recognition, and the gallery embodies decades of experimentation in dyeing and ikat design. The Telia Rumal tradition itself involves oil‑treating yarns and precise resist‑dyeing before weaving, a practice preserved and demonstrated at the gallery.
