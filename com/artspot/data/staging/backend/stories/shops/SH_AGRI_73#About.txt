In the quiet embrace of the Himalayas, where mist drifts gently over emerald slopes, Kangra Tea House revives a legacy once treasured by connoisseurs across the world. From the storied valleys of Himachal Pradesh, where tea has flourished since the 19th century, each leaf carries the memory of a heritage nearly lost and now lovingly restored.
Handcrafted by skilled hands and nurtured in pristine mountain air, these rare teas are organically grown, delicately processed, and defined by a refined, floral elegance unique to the Kangra terroir.
At its heart, Kangra Tea House is a return to authenticity, to craftsmanship, and to the people behind every harvest. By bringing value back to local estates and preserving this vintage cottage tradition, the brand transforms every cup into a quiet indulgence where heritage lingers, and luxury unfolds sip by sip.
