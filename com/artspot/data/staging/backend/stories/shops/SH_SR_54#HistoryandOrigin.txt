Founded in Surat, the heart of India’s textile industry, Dhananjay Creations emerged from a deep-rooted passion for ethnic fashion and a desire to bring accessible luxury to every woman’s closet. Our journey began with a small team and a big dream — to create a label that stands for quality, variety, and value.

Surat’s rich legacy in fabric manufacturing and garment design gave us the perfect foundation to launch. From sourcing fine materials to building strong supplier networks and investing in modern production methods, we’ve steadily grown to serve customers across India and beyond. Our origins keep us grounded in tradition, while our designs speak to a contemporary, global audience.
