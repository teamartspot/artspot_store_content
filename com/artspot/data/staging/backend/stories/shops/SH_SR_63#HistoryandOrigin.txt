Founded in Surat — the heart of India’s textile industry — Ajmera Fashion was established with a vision to simplify and revolutionize the wholesale ethnic wear market. What began as a small family-run operation has grown into a full-fledged wholesale powerhouse, driven by innovation, trust, and a passion for Indian fashion.

With roots going back to traditional saree trading, we have evolved to offer a complete range of Indian womenswear. Our team works closely with skilled artisans and manufacturers across the country to bring timeless pieces and current trends to market — all while supporting local craftsmanship and expanding global reach.
