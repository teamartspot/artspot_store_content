Manjal, M.Rm.Rm. Cultural Foundation The M.Rm.Rm. Cultural Foundation was established to preserve and promote the Chettinad region’s art, architecture, and crafts. Manjal emerged as part of this initiative, aiming to provide artisans with market access and to educate visitors about Chettinad culture. Over the years, the foundation has become instrumental in craft revival, awareness programs, and exhibitions that showcase Tamil Nadu’s intangible heritage.
