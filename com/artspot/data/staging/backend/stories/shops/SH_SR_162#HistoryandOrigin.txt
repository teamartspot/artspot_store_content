The Patola saree is one of India’s most treasured handloom traditions, originating in Gujarat and historically associated with royalty and nobility. This double ikat weave, known for its geometric precision and vibrant patterns, is among the most complex forms of textile art in the world.

Our journey began in 1974 with a deep reverence for this age-old craft. Since then, we have been committed to reviving and sustaining the Patola weaving tradition, specifically the Rajkot Patola style — known for its single ikat technique and vibrant, symmetrical motifs.

Each Patola saree undergoes an elaborate process that includes meticulous designing, tie-dyeing of both the warp and weft threads, and precision weaving. The result is a saree that reflects unmatched skill, cultural depth, and timeless appeal.

Through Patola by Prakash Bhai, we continue to celebrate the heritage of Patola weaving, honoring its legacy while bringing its elegance to the contemporary world.
