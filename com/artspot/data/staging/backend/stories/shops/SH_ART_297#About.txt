Panchmura Terracotta Panchmura is the terracotta village near Bishnupur (Bankura, West Bengal) famed for its Bankura-style terracotta horses and pots. The village is home to a community of potters producing the long-necked horses, figurines and household terracotta that are widely sold through shops and cooperatives. The terracotta of Panchmura is a recognised living tradition and a key ODOP/heritage product for the region. Panchmura is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
