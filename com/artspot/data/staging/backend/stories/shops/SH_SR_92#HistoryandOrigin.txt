The legacy of Pothys began over 90 years ago, when K.V. Pothy Moopanar, a visionary and skilled weaver, started weaving and selling cotton sarees, dhotis, and towels under the name “Pothy Moopanar” in Srivilliputhur, Tamil Nadu. What started as a modest venture in 1935 has now become a multi-city textile empire admired for its heritage and commitment to craftsmanship.

Pothys was initially rooted in royal weaving traditions, with its founders serving as artisans to the Maharajas of South India. This deep-rooted connection to Indian royalty laid the foundation for the luxury and authenticity that defines the brand even today.

Over the decades, Pothys has expanded significantly:

1986: Tirunelveli showroom

1999: Traditional Silk Sarees showroom, Chennai

2009–2024: Expansion into major cities including Madurai, Coimbatore, Thiruvananthapuram, Pondicherry, Bangalore, Salem, Trichy, Nagercoil, and Cochin

Pothys earned major accolades, including being voted Chennai's most preferred saree showroom in a SUN TV Nielsen Survey (2002) and becoming Tamil Nadu’s first ISO 9001 certified textile showroom in 2003.

Today, Pothys is more than just a retail brand — it’s a legacy of weaving excellence, royal heritage, and customer trust, carrying forward a tradition of textile artistry into the future.
