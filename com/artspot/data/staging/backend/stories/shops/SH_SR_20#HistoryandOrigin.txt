Handloom weaving in Bengal is an ancient art—steeped in tradition, storytelling, and meticulous craftsmanship. Tantuja Legacy was established with the mission of revitalizing this heritage by directly connecting the talent of Bengal’s rural artisans to modern markets.

Our origin lies in reaching the remotest weaving villages of West Bengal, providing artisans with a platform to showcase their work to a wider audience. As we evolved from a loss-making entity to a benchmark in the textile sector, we remained firmly committed to empowering weavers and preserving a zero-carbon, sustainable industry.

Today, Tantuja Legacy stands not just as a brand, but as a movement—encouraging every individual to choose handloom, support artisan livelihoods, and carry forward a legacy that dates back centuries.
