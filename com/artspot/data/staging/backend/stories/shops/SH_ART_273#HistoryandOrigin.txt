Shah Rasheed Ahmed Quadri Born in 1955 in Bidar, Shah Rasheed Ahmed Quadri belongs to a lineage of Bidri craftsmen and has been instrumental in preserving the approximately 500-year-old Bidri art form. His national recognition includes awards and honors acknowledging his contributions to craft preservation, and he continues to work with younger artisans to keep techniques alive. Shah Rasheed Ahmed Quadri is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
