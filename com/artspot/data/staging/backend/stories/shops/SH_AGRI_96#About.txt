In the mist-covered hills of Wayanad, where nature moves slowly, and the land tells its own story, Wayanad Virgin is born.
Sourced from forest-grown farms and nurtured by local communities, every bean and spice carries the richness of its origin pure, unhurried, and deeply authentic.
Working closely with tribal farmers, the brand preserves traditional practices while creating sustainable livelihoods, ensuring that every harvest supports both people and place.
