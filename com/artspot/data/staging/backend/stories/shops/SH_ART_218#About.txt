Amba The House Of Fashion is a small fashion label and manufacturer associated with handcrafted Bagh and traditional print textiles, blending classic motifs with contemporary apparel silhouettes. The brand operates via social media storefronts and regional wholesale networks, highlighting hand‑printed fabrics and artisanal trims. Customers engage with Amba for bespoke and small‑batch production runs, especially for ethnic wear and festival collections. The label supports local printers by commissioning hand block‑printed textiles and fostering collaborations between designers and craft studios.
