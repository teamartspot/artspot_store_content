Patola by Prakash Bhai is a distinguished family-run studio based in Rajkot, Gujarat, specializing in authentic handmade Patola sarees. Since 1974, each piece has been woven with pure mulberry silk by expert artisans, upholding nearly 50 years of dedication to preserving the true essence of Patola craftsmanship. Their collection highlights intricate, vibrant sarees—crafted with precision, vibrant natural dyes, and reversible double-ikat patterns—reflecting both tradition and elegance.
