Established in 1990 in the textile-rich city of Surat, Yadu Nandan Fashions began with a vision to democratize access to high-quality Indian ethnic wear. What started as a small venture focused on supplying sarees to local retailers has since transformed into a global name serving clients across five continents.

Over the years, we’ve evolved with technology, developing a robust digital platform that allows for smooth ordering, catalog browsing, and business support for resellers and boutiques alike. Our foundation is built on three principles — authenticity, accessibility, and affordability — ensuring that every garment tells a story of heritage while catering to the tastes of today’s consumer.
