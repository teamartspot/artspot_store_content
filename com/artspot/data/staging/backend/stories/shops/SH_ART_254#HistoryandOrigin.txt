The Razia Mat Industries The Razia Mat Industries emerged from Pattamadai’s long‑standing weaving tradition. Using Korai grass harvested from the Tamirabarani river region, artisans weave fine mats that have been associated with ceremonial and cultural use for generations. As the craft received GI recognition, enterprises like Razia Mat Industries have carried forward the weaving legacy, ensuring the continuity of artisanal skills and promoting wider recognition for Pattamadai products.
