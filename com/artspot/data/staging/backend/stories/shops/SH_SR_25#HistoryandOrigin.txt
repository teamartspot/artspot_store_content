The journey of Sri Venkateswara Saree Emporium began in 1980 in the culturally rich town of Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu—widely regarded as the silk capital of South India. Founded with just eight traditional handlooms, the brand was built on a mission to uphold the intricate weaving traditions passed down through generations.

In an era when power looms were on the rise, Sri Venkateswara Saree Emporium chose to remain rooted in the authenticity of handloom craftsmanship. With growing appreciation for the beauty and durability of pure Kanchipuram silk, the enterprise steadily expanded its operations to over 100 looms, becoming a trusted name among saree connoisseurs.

Each saree crafted under the Sri Venkateswara Saree Emporium banner reflects a perfect harmony of tradition, precision, and cultural pride. The brand’s commitment to quality and its artisans has not only preserved a centuries-old craft but also nurtured livelihoods and cultural identity within the weaving community.

Today, Sri Venkateswara Saree Emporium stands as a proud guardian of heritage—transforming tradition into wearable art for generations to come.
