Meghalaya’s silk weaving heritage, especially in Eri silk—also known as Ryndia or peace silk—is a powerful expression of sustainability, tradition, and women-led craftsmanship. Primarily practiced in the Ri-Bhoi district and Garo Hills, Eri silk weaving is deeply interwoven with rural life, where women play a central role in rearing silkworms, spinning yarn, and weaving fabric. More than a livelihood, it’s a cultural legacy passed down through generations. Today, this eco-friendly and ethical silk is gaining recognition globally for its softness, warmth, and sustainable roots.
