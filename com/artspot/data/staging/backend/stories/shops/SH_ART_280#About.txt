Vishwakarma - Banaras Gulabi Meenakari Artist Vishwakarma refers to master artisans in Varanasi who practice Banaras 'Gulabi' (pink) Meenakari — a locally celebrated enamel jewellery and decorative art. Practitioners produce colourful, lead-free enamel work on metal jewellery, temple icons and decorative panels, often following family lineages of the craft. Local artisan directories and craft pages list individual Vishwakarma family members and recognised masters who supply both tourists and designers. Vishwakarma - Banaras Gulabi Meenakari Artist is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
