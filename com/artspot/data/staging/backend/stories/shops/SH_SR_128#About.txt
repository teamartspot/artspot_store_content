We are a passionate team rooted in North East India, united by a shared mission to introduce the region’s rich culture to the rest of the country. While our work has taken us across major cities like Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore, and Delhi, our identity remains deeply connected to the North East — a land often overlooked and misunderstood. Through this platform, we aim to bridge the gap between urban India and the rural artisans of our homeland by showcasing authentic, hand-crafted products made by local artists and weavers.
