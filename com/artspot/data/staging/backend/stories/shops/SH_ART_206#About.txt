Praniita Embroidery is a Vijayawada-based registered company specialising in computerised embroidery, garment manufacturing and tailored apparel production. The company offers a range of services from embroidery job work to finished garments and operates a manufacturing unit in Madhura Nagar, Vijayawada. Praniita serves both B2B clients and retail customers, focusing on consistent quality, timely delivery and scalable production for larger orders. Their corporate registration and listings include official contact emails and a physical factory address used for supply chain and compliance.
