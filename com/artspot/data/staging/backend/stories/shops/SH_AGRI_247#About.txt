In the grain-rich fields of Punjab, where tradition quietly shapes every harvest, Sharma Rice Mills began its journey in 1998 under the guidance of Shri Bal Krishan Sharma and his sons. What started as a family-driven effort soon grew into a trusted name, built on dedication and a deep understanding of basmati rice. Each grain passes through careful selection and processing, preserving its natural aroma, taste, and purity. What makes this story unique is its rooted simplicity where family values and craftsmanship guide every step from field to final pack. Over the years, it has steadily expanded while holding onto the essence of quality and consistency. From local markets to wider reach, its journey reflects patience more than speed. In every serving, Sharma Rice Mills delivers not just rice, but a legacy shaped by tradition and trust
