Long before tea becomes a daily ritual, it passes through a world of quiet precision where timing, temperature, and technique decide its character.
At Hittakkal, this world has been carefully built over decades. What began under the vision of H.R. Muthaiah in 1984 has grown into a group of three advanced tea factories, each operating with a singular focus: consistency without compromise.
From the hills of Kotagiri, thousands of kilograms of tea move through a system that blends craftsmanship with engineering. With a daily capacity of CTC and orthodox teas, every batch is shaped through controlled processes, trained hands, and technology that ensures uniformity at scale.
Recognition has followed naturally. From ISO certification to repeated Golden Leaf Awards, the group has earned its place not just as a producer but as a benchmark within the Nilgiri tea landscape.
Yet, beyond numbers and accolades, what defines Hittakkal is something quieter 
the ability to make every cup feel consistent, no matter how far it travels.
