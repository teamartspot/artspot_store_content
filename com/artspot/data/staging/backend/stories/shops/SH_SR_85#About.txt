Shikha’s Fab is a Hyderabad-based textile brand known for its authentic hand-block printed sarees, dress materials, and fabrics. Located in Kondapur—one of the city's leading IT hubs—Shikha’s Fab is a harmonious blend of traditional craftsmanship and contemporary fashion. From dyeing raw fabric to block printing, embroidery, patchwork, and fusing varied textures and colors, each product is crafted with precision and creative flair.

Working with sustainable fabrics such as cotton and viscose, the brand not only manufactures in-house but also curates a handpicked range of textile artistry from artisans across India. Shikha’s Fab is more than just a textile label—it’s a platform for creativity, artisan empowerment, and the celebration of India’s textile legacy.

At its heart is a team of skilled artisans trained in centuries-old block printing techniques. With expert craftsmanship, they transform wooden blocks into living pieces of art, ensuring that every design tells a story and preserves the authenticity of Indian textiles.
