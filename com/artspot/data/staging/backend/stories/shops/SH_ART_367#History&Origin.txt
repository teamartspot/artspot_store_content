Rajouri Chikri Wood Handicrafts Industrial Cooperative Limited chikri woodcraft of Rajouri uses a fine-grained pale wood, carved into jali and decorative items. Recently recognized as a GI product, it sustains local livelihoods. Rajouri Chikri Wood Handicrafts Industrial Cooperative Limited is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
