Majuli’s satras (Vaishnavite monasteries) developed the art of mask-making as part of bhawna theatre. 
The craft uses eco-friendly materials, reflecting Assam’s folk aesthetics. 
Families like Khagen Goswami’s maintain this tradition as both cultural practice and livelihood. 
Government and cultural institutions highlight Majuli masks as Assam’s intangible heritage. 
These masks remain integral to satra festivals and cultural tourism on the island.
