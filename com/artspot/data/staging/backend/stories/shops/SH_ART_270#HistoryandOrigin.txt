Ajay Glass Emporium Ajay Glass Works traces its origins back to the early 1970s, expanding from small glass production units to a larger manufacturing setup with modern furnaces. Over decades the company scaled its production quality and product range, integrating contemporary manufacturing techniques while retaining artisanal glass-forming skills common to Firozabad's cluster. Ajay Glass Emporium is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
