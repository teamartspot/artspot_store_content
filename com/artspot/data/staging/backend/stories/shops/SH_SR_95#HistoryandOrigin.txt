Sundari Silks began with a vision nurtured in the home of its founder, Mr. K. Rajaram, who carried forward his family's generational expertise in weaving long before launching his own brand in T. Nagar in 1998 . Early success was followed by expansion: they set up a handloom facility in Thirucherai with 70 looms and later opened their flagship store in Mumbai’s Vile Parle.

Under their leadership, Sundari Silks pioneered technical innovations—such as silk-bamboo fiber blends, copper zari in Kanchipuram silks, and the 2-in-1 Uppada-Jacquard weave.

They also revitalized Kodalikaruppur sarees and launched services like the "Athirshtam" oxidized silver jewelry line and in-house tailoring (Naran & Alankara). A crowning achievement was the 90-foot custom-woven saree created for Goddess Ambal—a tribute to craftsmanship and reverence.

Their work has drawn attention from dignitaries, including Princess Mary of Denmark. From a first showroom in 1998, Sundari Silks has grown into a premier retailer that champions both heritage and innovation in every thread.
