Nestled in the tranquil hills of Nainital, Himalya Global Traders began as a vision to carry India’s natural richness and artisanal heritage to global markets with authenticity and care. Founded in 2024, the enterprise brings together wellness-focused herbal products, handcrafted décor, and thoughtfully curated goods that reflect India’s craftsmanship and sustainable spirit. Built on ethical sourcing, dependable quality, and customer trust, the company transforms traditional excellence into international offerings, creating a story where nature, culture, and commerce travel hand in hand across borders.
