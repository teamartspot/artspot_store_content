Thanjavur Veenai Works The Thanjavur veena‑making tradition dates back centuries, with artisans crafting instruments from jackfruit wood using time‑tested methods. Thanjavur became the centre of veena‑making due to royal patronage and the cultural importance of Carnatic music in the region. Thanjavur Veenai Works continues this heritage, blending traditional craftsmanship with modern refinements to keep the art form alive.
