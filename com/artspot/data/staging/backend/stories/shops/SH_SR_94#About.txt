Deepam Silks is a premier destination for exquisite handwoven silk sarees, based in Bengaluru. Celebrating over 50 years of legacy, we specialize in luxurious bridal Kanjivaram, Banarasi, Mysore silk, tussar, Ikat, and other traditional weaves.

Our curated collection features handcrafted masterpieces sourced from master weavers across India, ensuring a blend of heritage, quality, and contemporary elegance.

Our brand goes beyond retail—we’re storytellers, offering not just sarees but the rich cultural narratives they carry. With both offline showrooms (MG Road, Jayanagar) and a robust online presence, Deepam Silks ensures premium silk experiences delivered with care, supported by personalized shopping via video and exceptional service
