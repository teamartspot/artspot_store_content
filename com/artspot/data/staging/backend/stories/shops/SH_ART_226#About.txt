River of Rugs River of Rugs is a Bhadohi-based manufacturer and exporter of handmade rugs and carpets, specialising in custom designs and traditional knotting techniques. The company works with local weaving households in the Bhadohi district, producing hand-knotted and hand-tufted rugs for domestic and international buyers. Their catalog includes wool, viscose and blended-fibre rugs in contemporary and heritage designs, with options for customization and bulk orders. River of Rugs utilises Bhadohi’s long-established carpet-making infrastructure to maintain quality control across weaving, finishing and packing stages. They are present on business directories and social platforms where they accept wholesale and retail enquiries.
