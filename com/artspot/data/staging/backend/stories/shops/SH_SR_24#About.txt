Founded in 1986, Shiv Shakti Handloom & Crafts has grown into a trusted name in the textile industry as a manufacturer, wholesaler, and trader of premium home furnishing products. Our diverse product line includes printed bedsheets, quilts, mink blankets, 3D bedsheets, and flannel blankets—designed to meet the comfort and style needs of every household.

We are committed to delivering products that meet the highest standards of quality and customer satisfaction. Guided by ethical business practices and a transparent approach, we aim to build strong, lasting relationships with our clients by consistently exceeding their expectations.

Our journey of success has been made possible through the steadfast leadership of Mr. Pardeep Gupta, whose vision and guidance have propelled Shiv Shakti Handloom & Crafts to achieve remarkable growth and recognition in a competitive marketplace.
