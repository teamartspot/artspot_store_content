Shobitam was born from a shared dream between two sisters, Aparna and Ambika, who grew up immersed in Indian textiles and aesthetics. As Indian women living abroad, they noticed a gap: there were few platforms that offered high-quality, stylish ethnic wear at fair prices—with a personal, customer-first approach.

Encouraged by their mother’s suggestion to name the brand with meaning and their father’s support in managing logistics, they began their entrepreneurial journey with just a handful of sarees and a vision to change how Indian fashion is experienced overseas.

Since its inception, Shobitam has evolved into a global online brand, serving thousands of customers across continents. With a deep respect for heritage and an eye on modern trends, the brand continues to grow—celebrating tradition with every weave, and community with every sale.
