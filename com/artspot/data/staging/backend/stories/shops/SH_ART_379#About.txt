Odisha Handloom Emporium in Bhubaneswar is a state-backed emporium showcasing Odisha handloom crafts including ikat, bomkai, and appliqué. Odisha Handloom Emporium is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
