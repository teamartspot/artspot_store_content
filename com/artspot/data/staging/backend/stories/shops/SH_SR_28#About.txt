Welcome to Prashanti Sarees, where timeless craftsmanship meets thoughtful curation. As a proud part of the heritage textile legacy under the Padma eRetail Collective, we have earned the trust of saree enthusiasts for our vibrant handloom creations—especially in silk and silk-cotton.

With over 25 years of experience in the weaving industry, we take immense pride in offering exclusively handwoven sarees that carry the soul of tradition and the elegance of modern aesthetics.

We are especially known for our rich and varied range of silk-cotton sarees. From elegant hand block prints and the ever-charming Kuppadam silks with rich borders, to classic Korvai weaves, our collections are designed to bring diversity, charm, and heritage into every wardrobe.
