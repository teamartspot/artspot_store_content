Purbashree is the product sales and e-commerce division of the North Eastern Handicrafts and Handlooms Development Corporation Ltd. (NEHHDC). It serves as a vital link between skilled artisans of Northeast India and a wider market, offering a curated selection of handcrafted and handwoven products. Through its physical emporia and digital platform, Purbashree promotes the region’s diverse cultural heritage while creating sustainable livelihoods for local craftsmen and weavers.
