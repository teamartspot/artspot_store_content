Karaikkudi Antiques Market Karaikkudi, located in the Chettinad region, became a centre for antiques as old Chettiar mansions were dismantled or repurposed. Traders collected doors, pillars, tiles, utensils, and furnishings, which were then resold through the antique market. Over time, the Karaikkudi Antiques Market grew into a recognised hub where heritage items from Tamil Nadu are preserved and circulated among collectors and designers.
