In the mist-covered mountains of Wayanad, where coffee grows beneath forest canopies and carries the spirit of the land, Mountain Brewed Coffee was born from a simple yet powerful realization that a region rich in heritage deserved to be celebrated, not overlooked.
Driven by a vision to elevate Wayanad’s rare GI-tagged Robusta, the journey began by bringing farmers, land, and coffee lovers closer together, restoring value where it truly belongs. Each bean is carefully handpicked, naturally processed, and shaped by the rhythms of the mountains, capturing a bold character that is both intense and refined.
Here, coffee is not just crafted, it is curated as an experience. From forest-grown cultivation to cup, every step reflects authenticity, sustainability, and a deep respect for origin.
Mountain Brewed Coffee is more than a brew; it is the soul of Wayanad in every sip, where heritage, community, and the quiet luxury of bold, uncompromised coffee come alive
