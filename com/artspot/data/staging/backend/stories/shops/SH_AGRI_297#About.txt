Chikhale Export Private Limited is a family-owned agricultural export company based in Sangola, Maharashtra, dedicated to bringing India’s premium fresh produce to international markets. Specializing in pomegranates, bananas, and mangoes, the company sources high-quality fruits from trusted farms while maintaining strict quality and compliance standards. With APEDA and FSSAI certifications, Chikhale Export offers complete export solutions, from sourcing and packaging to documentation and global shipping. Their mission is to connect local farmers with worldwide buyers, combining agricultural expertise, sustainability, and reliable service to build lasting global partnerships.
