Mahakissan Agro Farms was founded by an agricultural family with over 25 years of farming experience, transforming deep-rooted expertise into a modern fresh produce export business in 2023. Based in Maharashtra, the company specializes in export-quality bananas, pomegranates, coconuts, grapes, and other agricultural commodities sourced through carefully managed farming practices. With a strong focus on organic cultivation, quality assurance, hygienic packaging, and a self-owned supply chain, they ensure freshness from farm to destination. Beyond exports, their expert agronomy team actively supports farmers from planting to harvest, helping strengthen sustainable farming and agricultural growth.
