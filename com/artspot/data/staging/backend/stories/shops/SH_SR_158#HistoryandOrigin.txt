KCPC Bandhani began its journey with a mission to modernize traditional Bandhej and Gota Patti designs while retaining their age-old charm. What started as a passion for hand-crafted fashion has grown into a full-fledged fashion house, leading the revival of heritage crafts in everyday wear.

Our collections are the result of deep research, innovation, and a relentless drive to make traditional Indian art forms like Bandhani accessible and appealing to today’s women — whether she's a teenager or a grandmother. With over 800+ unique designs, KCPC Bandhani has become a trusted favorite for resellers and retail customers alike.

Rooted in Indian culture yet tailored for the modern wardrobe, our designs reflect a confident, graceful woman — one who values tradition but embraces the new. As we say, “Fashion with Tradition” isn’t just our tagline — it’s our identity.
