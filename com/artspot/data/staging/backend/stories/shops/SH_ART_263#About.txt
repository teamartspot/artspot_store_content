Agra Dhurrie Bhandar Exports Agra Dhurrie Bhandar Exports is a long-established manufacturer and exporter of cotton dhurries, jacquard rugs, and printed carpets based in Agra. The company supplies a broad catalogue of floor coverings including shaggy rugs, herringbone dhurries and jute blends for home and contract markets. They provide both wholesale and retail services and cater to international buyers seeking ethically produced floor textiles. The firm highlights its production experience and capacity for large orders and bespoke designs.
