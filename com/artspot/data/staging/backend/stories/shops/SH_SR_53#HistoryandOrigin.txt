Kesaria Textile Co. was established with a vision to bridge the gap between traditional Indian craftsmanship and modern retail demands. Based in the heart of Surat, Gujarat—India's textile hub—we began as a modest supplier and steadily evolved into a trusted wholesale partner for thousands of fashion entrepreneurs.

Our foundation is built on years of industry insight and a passion for preserving India's textile legacy. From intricate Banarasi and Kanjivaram sarees to contemporary gowns and fusion wear, our journey reflects a deep respect for heritage, while continuously innovating to meet the needs of a global clientele.

With a strong online presence and commitment to ethical sourcing, Kesaria Textile Co. continues to empower small businesses and fashion-forward minds through accessible ethnic fashion.
