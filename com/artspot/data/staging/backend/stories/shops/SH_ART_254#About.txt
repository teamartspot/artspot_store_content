The Razia Mat Industries The Razia Mat Industries is a Tamil Nadu‑based enterprise engaged in the weaving and sale of Pattamadai Korai mats. It produces a variety of mats, including ceremonial mats, personalised mats, and decorative items for household and ritual use. Known for its quality weaving and finish, the enterprise has been recognised in craft directories and serves local as well as regional customers. Its products are valued for durability and authenticity in carrying forward the Pattamadai tradition.
