AAISAHEB Women Farmer Producer Company began its journey in Akluj, Solapur, initially working as a third-party organic banana exporter before evolving into an independent women-led farmer producer company. Built by a collective of dedicated women farmers, the enterprise focuses on cultivating premium organic bananas and pomegranates with a strong commitment to quality, sustainability, and ethical farming practices. Over the years, their work has expanded beyond cultivation into international exports, reaching markets in Dubai, Iran, Iraq, and other Gulf countries. With hands-on expertise in plantation management and agricultural processes, the company also promotes organic banana farming through consultancy services, aiming to empower more farmers and strengthen sustainable agriculture across Maharashtra and beyond.
