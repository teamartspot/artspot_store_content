Utkalamrita emerged from a shared dream to preserve and promote Odisha’s rich cultural artistry. Drawing inspiration from the term “Utkala”—land of excellent art—the brand embodies a mission to support local artisans, empower women-led enterprise, and introduce Odisha’s vibrant craft legacy to national and global platforms.

Since its inception in 2015, the brand has bridged traditional craftsmanship with contemporary markets. Every product is a tribute to the skilled hands of regional artisans, designed not just for adornment but as a symbol of identity, heritage, and sustainable living.

Guided by purpose and rooted in authenticity, Utkalamrita continues to evolve as a celebration of “Utkarsha Kala”—the spirit of exquisite Indian art.
