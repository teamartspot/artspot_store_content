Tarkashi Hastshilp Sahkari Samiti mainpuri Tarkashi involves brass, copper or silver wire inlaid on sheesham wood. The cooperative continues this historic craft, now recognized under UP’s ODOP program and India’s GI system. Tarkashi Hastshilp Sahkari Samiti is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
