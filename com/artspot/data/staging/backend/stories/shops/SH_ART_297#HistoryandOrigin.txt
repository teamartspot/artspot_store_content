Panchmura Terracotta Bankura–Panchmura pottery traces back generations and is woven into local temple and folk practice; families in Panchmura continue to fire and hand-decorate terracotta wares using traditional kilns and coloring methods. Regional craft pages and tourism resources document Panchmura as a concentrated centre for this terracotta tradition. Panchmura is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
