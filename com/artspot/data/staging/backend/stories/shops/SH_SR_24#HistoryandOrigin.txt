The roots of Shiv Shakti Handloom & Crafts trace back to 1986, when it began as a small-scale textile unit dedicated to delivering comfort through quality home furnishings. Originating in a region known for its rich textile traditions, the brand was established with the vision of combining affordability with durability—offering everyday essentials like bedsheets and blankets that families could trust.

What started as a modest venture steadily evolved into a large-scale manufacturing and wholesale operation under the stewardship of Mr. Pardeep Gupta. His keen business acumen and commitment to customer satisfaction helped build a company that values craftsmanship, consistency, and ethical trade.

Over the decades, Shiv Shakti Handloom & Crafts has embraced innovation while staying true to its roots. The brand has expanded its product range and distribution network, reaching homes across India and earning the trust of retailers and customers alike.

From humble beginnings to industry presence, Shiv Shakti Handloom & Crafts continues to weave tradition into every thread—serving not just as a business, but as a name built on trust, warmth, and quality.
