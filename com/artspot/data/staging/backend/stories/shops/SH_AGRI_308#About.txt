Green and Grains began with a mission to make safe, naturally grown food accessible while creating sustainable livelihoods for small and marginal farmers. Built on a farm-to-fork model, the company partners directly with thousands of farmers across India, helping them adopt better agricultural practices and bringing fresh produce straight to consumers. What started as a wholesale agricultural venture evolved into a wider food ecosystem spanning fruits, vegetables, grains, and healthy staples. Their journey reflects a strong commitment to sustainable farming, farmer empowerment, and delivering trustworthy, chemical-conscious food to modern households.
