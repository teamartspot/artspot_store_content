Utkalamrita is a Bhubaneswar-based brand focused on Odisha’s traditional food heritage and cultural products. 
It promotes authentic Odia recipes, millet-based foods and regional crafts through verified outlets. 
The company emphasizes natural ingredients and artisan-made products. 
Its offerings include packaged foods, handicrafts and lifestyle items rooted in Odisha’s culture. 
The brand has been profiled in state media for promoting Odisha’s heritage on modern platforms.
