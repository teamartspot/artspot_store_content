The origins of Pochampally Sarees trace back to the heartland of Pochampally in Telangana, a town renowned globally for its signature double Ikat weave. The tradition began in our family decades ago when our grandfather took to the loom, mastering an art that requires mathematical precision, artistic flair, and extraordinary patience.

Over time, this intricate technique—where patterns are dyed onto the yarn before weaving—became our family’s calling. What started with a few handlooms in a small workshop evolved into a generational craft preserved by passion and passed on with pride. With every new generation, we’ve added our unique touch—embracing innovation while never straying from the roots.

Today, Pochampally Sarees is a living story of revival, reinvention, and respect for an art form that continues to bind us to our past while dressing the future.
