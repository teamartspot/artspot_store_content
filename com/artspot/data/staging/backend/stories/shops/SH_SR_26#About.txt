uppada is a visionary textile design house known for blending the elegance of traditional Indian weaves with modern aesthetics. Led by the renowned textile revivalist and designer Ghanshyam Sarode, the studio is unique in its approach—empowering boutiques to carry his designs under their own label, making artisanal excellence more accessible and widespread.

With weaving clusters spanning Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, and Maharashtra, uppada commands one of the most robust design and distribution infrastructures in the Indian ethnic fashion landscape. From celebrities to connoisseurs across the globe, the brand’s handwoven sarees and fabrics are celebrated for their artistic refinement and cultural depth.

Each piece is the result of collaboration between designer and artisan, rooted in authentic craftsmanship yet designed for the contemporary wearer. From Uppada and Paithani to Jamdani, Kalamkari, Khadi, and Ikat, the studio offers a curated blend of India’s most intricate weaves in pure, natural materials.
