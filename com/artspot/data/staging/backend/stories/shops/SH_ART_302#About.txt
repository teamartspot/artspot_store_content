Shri Sharada Shilpa Kala Mandhiram (also written Sarada Shilpa Kala Mandiram) is a sculptors' institute in Allagadda, Kurnool district, Andhra Pradesh, founded to teach and produce stone sculpture and metalwork. The Mandiram trains artists in carving, casting and restoration and undertakes large-scale sculpture commissions for temples and public spaces. It also sells sculptural works and provides demonstrations that preserve traditional carving skills.
