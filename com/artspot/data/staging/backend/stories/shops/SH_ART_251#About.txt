Rathna Metal Mart Rathna Metal Mart is a metalware retailer and manufacturer located in Kumbakonam, Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu. It deals in brass ornamental lamps, bronze statues, and pooja items such as Vaasaal Maalai, Yega Deepam, and decorative mandapams. The store is well known for handmade traditional items used in temples and households. Established decades ago, it continues to supply both retail and wholesale customers, and is also listed on exporter directories as a reliable supplier of South Indian bronze crafts.
