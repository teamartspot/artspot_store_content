Kalanjali Emporium, a division of Margahita Lifestyle Private Limited, is a celebration of India’s rich artistic heritage—offering a curated collection of ethnic wear, handcrafted furniture, exquisite sarees, and timeless artefacts. Every piece we offer is a journey into centuries-old craftsmanship, carefully sourced from skilled artisans across India’s most remote and culturally vibrant regions.

With five flourishing showrooms across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, and a growing global footprint via our eCommerce platform, Kalanjali has become a trusted destination for traditional Indian luxury. From handloom Kanchipuram silks and Kalamkari-painted textiles to miniature Rajasthan paintings and heirloom stone carvings—our selections capture the soul of India’s art traditions.

At Kalanjali, we also offer bespoke handcrafted furniture and custom gifting collections tailored to both individual and corporate preferences. Our dedicated bridal line, personally curated by our leadership team, is crafted with love, detail, and elegance—making it the dream choice for brides seeking grandeur and authenticity.
