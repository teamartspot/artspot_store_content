Kumbhikagaz is a Guwahati-based brand specializing in handmade paper products. 
It collaborates with artisans and women’s groups to make eco-friendly stationery and packaging. 
The enterprise emphasizes sustainability, recycling and traditional craft processes. 
Products include notebooks, folders and decorative paper goods. 
Kumbhikagaz has been highlighted by Assam’s entrepreneurship networks as an innovative green startup.
