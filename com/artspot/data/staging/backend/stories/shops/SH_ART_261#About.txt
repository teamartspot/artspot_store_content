N.Rajendran Veenai house N. Rajendran Veenai House is a Thanjavur-based luthier workshop specialising in the carving and finishing of the Saraswati veena. The workshop crafts fully carved ekanda and carved-resonator veenas using seasoned jack and teakwood, offering instruments for concert use and instructional institutions. Their instruments are tuned, finished and often feature hand-carved decorative motifs consistent with South Indian classical instrument making. The workshop maintains direct contact lines for orders and repairs and is known locally for quality workmanship and timely service. Customers include performing musicians and music schools seeking authentic Thanjavur-style veenas.
