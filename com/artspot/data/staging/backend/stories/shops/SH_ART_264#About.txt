Mughal Carpet Company Mughal Carpet Company of Agra is a manufacturer and exporter of hand-knotted and hand-tufted carpets, offering a range of Persian-inspired and contemporary designs. The company operates a showroom near Tajganj and provides custom-order services for both retail customers and interior projects. The business emphasises skilled knotting, durable backing and careful finishing for export-grade rugs. Their product range includes wool, silk and blended fibre carpets with custom sizing available for international clients.
