Some teas are blended. Others are grown with a voice of their own.
At Darmona, that voice comes from a single estate where, since 1984, every leaf has been cultivated, picked, and crafted within the same landscape. There is no fragmentation here, only continuity from garden to cup.
High-grown in the Nilgiris, the leaves develop a quiet intensity—clean, aromatic, and layered. Handpicked with care, they are shaped into teas that range from bold dust brews to refined whole-leaf expressions, each carrying a distinct personality.
What sets Darmona apart is not just origin, but intent. The estate now runs on 100% solar energy, aligning craftsmanship with responsibility where purity extends beyond taste into the way it is made.
Even in its spiced blends where cardamom, clove, ginger, and pepper come together, the foundation remains unchanged: tea that holds its ground, no matter how it is experienced.
Every cup carries a place, uninterrupted.
