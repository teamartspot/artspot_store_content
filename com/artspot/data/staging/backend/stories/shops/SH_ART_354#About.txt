Tarkashi Hastshilp Sahkari Samiti is documented as an applicant in the Geographical Indication registry for Mainpuri Tarkashi, the metal inlay woodwork craft. It represents artisans practicing the tradition in Mainpuri, Uttar Pradesh. Tarkashi Hastshilp Sahkari Samiti is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
