Sunasa is a fashion-tech brand born from a deep reverence for India's cultural legacy. We are committed to preserving the rich heritage of handloom artistry, intricate weaving techniques, and the timeless tradition of adorning Indian ethnic wear. At Sunasa, every piece we offer is not just a garment—it is a carefully woven poem crafted by our skilled artisans with passion, precision, and pride.

Dedicated to the philosophy of being a ‘weavers-first’ company, our mission is to support and empower India’s weaver communities by providing sustainable livelihoods and a global platform for their craft. From regal silk sarees and Banarasi masterpieces to handloom linen and bridal lehengas, our collections showcase the extraordinary craftsmanship and cultural richness of India. With each product, we strive to keep alive the beauty of handcrafted traditions and offer customers an experience steeped in elegance and authenticity.
