From the mist-laden hills of Coorg (Kodagu), where spice cultivation has flourished for centuries, Mercara Flavours brings the essence of this storied land directly to your kitchen. Rooted in the region’s rich agricultural heritage, the brand works closely with local farmers to preserve the authenticity, purity, and character of true Coorg spices.
Handpicked at peak harvest and carefully processed, each spice retains its natural aroma, vibrant color, and depth of flavor. Guided by sustainable practices and a direct-from-farm approach, Mercara Flavours ensures that every pack reflects both tradition and uncompromising quality.
More than just spices, Mercara Flavours offers a sensory journey where the legacy of Coorg meets modern refinement, delivering freshness, authenticity, and quiet luxury in every pinch
