The name Aura Benaras captures the enduring charm and spiritual energy of Varanasi’s rich handloom legacy. Established on April 10th, 2019, Aura Benaras is built on three timeless pillars: culture, simplicity, and elegance. Rooted in the sacred city of Varanasi—India’s cultural and spiritual heart—our brand draws from a centuries-old tradition of intricate brocade weaving and artisanal excellence.

Our vision is to celebrate and expand the reach of handloom products not just within India, but across the world. With the new generation embracing traditional wear like sarees more than ever before, and with growing support for handloom from both the public and the government, we see a bright future for this beautiful craft. Aura Benaras aims to be an active force in reviving and honoring this heritage.

We promise our community that every product we create is crafted with care, offered with integrity, and designed to bring delight. Quality is our priority, and we are committed to delivering the finest handloom pieces at affordable prices. At Aura Benaras, we envision a world where the handloom industry regains its rightful place of pride.
