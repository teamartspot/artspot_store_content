From the historic saffron heartland of Pampore in Kashmir’s Pulwama district—where crimson threads have defined flavour and tradition for centuries—Mir Kashmir Saffron brings you the authentic essence of this rare spice. Grown and traded from the very soil that gives the world its most prized saffron, their selection is a celebration of purity, heritage, and sensory richness.
Hand-selected and offered in premium forms, from organic 10 g strands to half-cut Kesar and fine saffron powder, each batch reflects the deep red colour, potent aroma, and delicate flavour that only true Kashmiri saffron can offer. Beyond saffron, Mir Kashmir Saffron curates a distinguished range of Kashmir’s finest — from dry fruits and traditional Qawah tea to natural honey, shilajit resin, and artisanal oils — each chosen for uncompromising quality and authenticity.
Rooted in the legacy of Kashmir’s spice culture and presented with quiet elegance, Mir Kashmir Saffron is an invitation to experience the valley’s treasures in their most refined form — a true luxury for connoisseurs of flavour and heritage.
