Indrayani Rice (Krushijanya) was created with a mission to bring the authentic aroma and taste of Maharashtra’s traditional Maval-grown Indrayani rice directly to consumers. Rooted in the heritage of the Maval region, the brand focuses on supplying genuine aromatic Indrayani and Ambemohar rice, along with other traditional grains, while preserving authenticity and quality. What began as an effort to celebrate regional agricultural produce has grown into a trusted name among households seeking the original taste of Maharashtrian staples. Their journey reflects a blend of local farming heritage, purity, and a commitment to keeping traditional food culture alive.
