The legacy of Chanderiyaan is deeply rooted in the historic town of Chanderi, renowned for its exquisite weaving tradition that dates back over 1,000 years. Chanderi textiles, known for their sheer texture, lightweight fabric, and intricate motifs, have adorned royal courts and cultural festivals across generations.

Recognizing the need to protect and modernize this heritage, Chanderiyaan was established as a digital initiative in collaboration with Media Lab Asia, the Digital Empowerment Foundation (DEF), and the Ministry of Communications & IT, Government of India. The goal was to empower local weavers by introducing digital tools, education, and direct access to the global market.
