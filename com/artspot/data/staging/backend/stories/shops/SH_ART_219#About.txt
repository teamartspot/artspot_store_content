RATAN Textiles is a textile manufacturing and retail unit (Jaipur) involved in fabric production, garmenting and home textiles, supplying both domestic retailers and wholesale buyers. The business offers block‑printed fabrics, ready‑made garments and furnishing materials and has an online presence for B2B inquiries and retail customers. Their product lines often include traditional Rajasthani prints adapted for contemporary interior and fashion markets. RATAN focuses on consistent quality, timely deliveries, and trade relationships across the region.
