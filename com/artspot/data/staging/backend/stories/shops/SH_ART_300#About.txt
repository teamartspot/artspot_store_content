Haasini Handlooms Haasini Handlooms is a Warangal/Deshaipet-based handloom and carpet manufacturer dealing in dhurries, home textiles and floor coverings. The business operates a regional showroom and supplies a variety of woven floor and home products to local markets and trade customers. Haasini Handlooms is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
