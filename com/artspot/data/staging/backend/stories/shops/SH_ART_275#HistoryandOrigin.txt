Girish Rajsoni Girish Rajsoni continues a familial practice in Thewa that dates back several generations in Pratapgarh. As part of a local artisan community, he has participated in exhibitions and craft festivals that promote Thewa as a living heritage artform. Girish Rajsoni is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
