The Kantha Store curates and retails authentic Bengali Kantha embroidery—sarees, dupattas, quilts and stoles—made by skilled rural artisans across West Bengal. The organisation emphasises hand‑stitched kantha, traditional quilt‑making, and upcycled textiles where vintage cloth is repurposed into contemporary products. Their collections are marketed through an ecommerce storefront and are positioned as ethically sourced, with craft documentation and maker stories accompanying many listings. The Kantha Store also provides contextual information on kantha techniques to help buyers distinguish genuine handwork from machine‑made imitations. Their range includes both heritage patterns and modern design adaptations suited for urban markets.
