In the misty hills of Coorg, where coffee has long been a way of life, Coorg Coffee carries forward a legacy that traces its roots back to 1944, a story brewed across generations with quiet dedication and craft.
What began as a humble family pursuit evolved into a refined expression of excellence, guided by visionaries who combined deep-rooted tradition with modern precision. From the early days of learning the art of coffee to building state-of-the-art facilities, every step has been shaped by a commitment to quality and consistency.
Today, each blend reflects this journey, carefully processed, expertly crafted, and shared with coffee lovers across the world.
Coorg Coffee is more than a brand it is a legacy in every cup, where heritage, innovation, and the timeless allure of fine coffee come together in perfect harmony.
