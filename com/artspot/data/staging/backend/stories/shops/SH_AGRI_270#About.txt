Since 1982, ABC Raisins has been weaving a story of dedication from the fertile vineyards of Sangli, transforming humble grapes into premium raisins trusted across India and global markets. Built on a legacy of agricultural expertise, strong family values, and an unwavering commitment to quality, the brand has grown into a dependable name for institutions, retailers, and international buyers alike. With advanced processing, timely delivery, and a passion for excellence, ABC Raisins is more than a manufacturer it is a symbol of tradition meeting innovation, carrying the sweetness of Indian craftsmanship to the world.
