Purandar Highlands grew from a visionary idea into a farmer-led movement transforming challenges into opportunities. Inspired by global agricultural innovation and rooted in the GI-tagged richness of Purandar figs, the enterprise united smallholder farmers, modern farming practices, and sustainable innovation to create products that travel from local orchards to international markets. More than a food business, it is a story of ambition, resilience, and rural empowerment where every fig, custard apple, and handcrafted product carries the pride of Indian farmers and the promise of nature’s best.
