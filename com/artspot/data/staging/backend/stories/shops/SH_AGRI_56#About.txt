Since 1979, in the misty hills of Coorg, where spices have long been treasured as nature’s finest luxury, Coorg Greens has quietly carried forward a legacy of quality and authenticity. What began with a vision rooted in responsibility has grown into a trusted name for premium spices and dry fruits.
Inspired by a time when spices were rare and revered, Coorg Greens remains devoted to preserving their true value, carefully sourcing, curating, and delivering products that retain their natural richness, aroma, and character.
With decades of experience and thousands of satisfied customers, Coorg Greens continues to honor India’s spice heritage, bringing timeless flavor, purity, and quiet excellence to every home.
