Selakiya Sculpture Works Selakiya Sculpture (Selakiya Sculpture Works) is a Mahabalipuram (Mamallapuram) stone-sculpting workshop and showroom producing traditional South Indian stone sculptures, temple figures and garden statuary. The studio emphasises hand-carved granite and sandstone sculptures that reflect local temple iconography and tourist tastes. Selakiya Sculpture Works is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
