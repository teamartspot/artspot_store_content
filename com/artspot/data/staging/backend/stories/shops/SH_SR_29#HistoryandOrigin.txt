The legacy of Mavuri's is rooted in the inspiring life of Veeramma Garu, our great-grandmother and a pioneering figure in her time. Known for her keen eye and exquisite bridal saree selections, she would travel from village to village, offering handpicked weaves for special occasions. Her entrepreneurial spirit and reverence for the loom laid the foundation for what would later become a brand built on heritage and heart.

Drawing from her boldness and vision, Mavuri's was born with a mission to preserve the handloom heritage of India while empowering the artisans behind each masterpiece. From our first thread to today’s collections, her legacy continues to guide us in championing slow fashion and conscious craftsmanship.

With every saree, we aim to carry forward her belief—that tradition can be both rooted and relevant, and that style should always come with a soul.
