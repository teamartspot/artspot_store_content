Haasini Handlooms Haasini Handlooms was set up to capitalise on local weaving skills and small-scale manufacturing of floor coverings. Trade listings and company profiles show the firm providing product ranges for both household retail and contract orders, reflecting the region’s woven-textiles capacity. Haasini Handlooms is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
