Chikoo Parlour was born from an innovative idea to transform Dahanu-Gholvad’s famous GI-tagged chikoo into a wide range of unique value-added products. Founded by engineer and entrepreneur M. P. Churi in 2016 after years of research, the venture created sweets, milkshakes, ice creams, and other preservative-free chikoo products using farm-fresh fruit. Based near the scenic Bordi Beach, the brand blends local agricultural heritage with food innovation. Beyond business, it also aims to create employment opportunities for villagers and give greater recognition to the region’s iconic chikoo fruit, turning a local specialty into a celebrated consumer brand.
