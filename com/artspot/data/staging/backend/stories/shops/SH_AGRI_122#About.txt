High above the plains, in the cool blue mountains of the Nilgiris, tea is not hurried it is shaped.
At elevations rising beyond 6,000 to 8,000 feet, the estates of Chamraj have, for over a century, cultivated teas that carry a clarity only altitude can create, bright, fragrant, and quietly complex.
What begins as carefully plucked leaves is transformed within the estate itself, crafted into orthodox and specialty teas that retain their origin in every sip. From rare frost harvests to delicate whole-leaf selections, each expression reflects both climate and craft.
Among the early pioneers of organic tea in India and long trusted in global markets, Chamraj’s journey has never been about scale alone, but about refinement, perfecting the art of tea while staying rooted in its terrain.
Some teas refresh.
Others leave an impression long after the cup is empty.
