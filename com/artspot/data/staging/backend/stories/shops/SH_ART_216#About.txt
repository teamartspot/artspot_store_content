Madhukar Bagh Print is a Bagh (Dhar) based workshop and manufacturer producing traditional Bagh block‑printed textiles—sarees, dress materials, stoles and home fabrics—using wooden blocks and natural dyes. The workshop offers wholesale and retail supplies and is visible on local directories and social platforms where customers place orders and enquire about custom prints. The studio highlights artisan‑led printing techniques and offers a range of motifs rooted in regional Bagh vocabulary. They also supply to trade buyers and participate in craft fairs to demonstrate block‑printing processes.
