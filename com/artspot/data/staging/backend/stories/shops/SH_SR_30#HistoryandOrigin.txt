Avishya was founded by a group of textile enthusiasts who were passionate about bringing India's handloom treasures into the spotlight. Their goal was simple: to create a platform that connects connoisseurs of Indian textiles with the skilled hands that craft them.

What began as a small initiative has grown into a trusted online marketplace known for authenticity, transparency, and ethical sourcing. Inspired by the depth of India's weaving traditions and the need to support artisans directly, Avishya bridges heritage with modern convenience—offering timeless weaves to a global audience.

Each product tells a story—of heritage passed through generations, of hands that continue to create magic on the loom, and of a future where sustainability and elegance go hand in hand.
