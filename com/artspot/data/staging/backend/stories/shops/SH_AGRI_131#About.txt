For generations, Assam tea was known for strength quick brews, bold cups, and familiar routines. At Rujani, the intent was never to follow that story but to rewrite it.
Rooted in a tea family that has cultivated the same estate since 1897, the journey begins at Aideobari, where every leaf is grown, processed, and refined within one uninterrupted landscape. Four generations have shaped this continuity not by preserving the past unchanged, but by questioning it.
The shift was deliberate. Moving away from commoditised tea bags, Rujani focuses on whole-leaf teas crafted in small batches, designed to be experienced slowly, the way tea once was.
This pursuit is not instinct alone. It is studied, tested, and evolved in collaboration with institutions like Tocklai, where tea is treated as both craft and science. Innovation here is quiet, but constant refining of how flavour is drawn from the same soil, year after year.
At the same time, nothing leaves the estate without belonging to it. The teas are grown, processed, and sent directly without intermediaries, ensuring that what arrives carries the identity of its origin, unchanged.
Rujani does not simplify tea. It invites you to experience it differently.
