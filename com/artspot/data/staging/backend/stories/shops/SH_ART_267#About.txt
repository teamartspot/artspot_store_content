Patola Of Patan Pvt Ltd Patola of Patan Pvt Ltd is an organisation dedicated to producing and promoting Patan Patola double-ikat sarees and textiles from Patan, Gujarat. The company works with master weavers to protect the double ikat tradition, offering handwoven sarees renowned for precise dyeing and symbolic motifs. Their catalogue includes heritage patolas as well as contemporary adaptations for modern wardrobes. Patola Of Patan also participates in craft events and works to keep Patan's weaving lineage visible to collectors and designers.
