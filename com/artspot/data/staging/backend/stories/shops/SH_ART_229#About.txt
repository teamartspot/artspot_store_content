Shree Mahalaxmi Jari Shree Mahalaxmi Jari (Begampura, Surat) manufactures zari and metallic embroidery threads and supplies them to textile printers, weavers and embroiderers in the Surat cluster. The firm offers a selection of metallic yarns used in zari work, together with standard embroidery threads and finishing materials. Shree Mahalaxmi emphasizes reliable supply and compatibility with mechanised and hand-embroidery workflows used by local and export-oriented producers.
