The journey of Keevaa Exports began in 1990 in Surat, Gujarat, with a small team and a big vision—to make Indian ethnic wear more accessible across the globe. What started as a traditional textile business has grown into a modern, export-ready brand powered by technology and rooted in cultural heritage.

From humble beginnings supplying sarees to local retailers, we gradually expanded into bridal lehengas and ethnic jewelry, building a global network of resellers and boutique clients. The rise of digital commerce inspired our evolution into a future-ready platform that merges traditional craftsmanship with efficient, tech-based logistics.

Today, Keevaa Exports supports a new generation of fashion entrepreneurs by offering direct-from-factory sourcing, customization, and fast international delivery. Our legacy is woven through decades of dedication, innovation, and a commitment to celebrating India’s textile excellence on the world stage.
