Saurashtra Oriental Arts is a Jaipur‑based manufacturer and exporter specialising in printed fabrics, kantha quilts, durries and home furnishing textiles. The company produces block‑printed fabrics, kantha‑style stitched quilts and machine‑finished rugs for domestic and export markets. Their catalogue emphasizes vibrant designs and bulk production capacity suitable for wholesalers and retailers. Saurashtra Oriental Arts also fulfils large orders for institutional buyers and maintains trade listings across B2B portals.
