Mirzapur Art Palace Mirzapur Art Palace is a regional carpet and rug dealer linked to Mirzapur’s large handmade carpet cluster. Dealers in Mirzapur sell hand-knotted rugs, tufted carpets and home textiles to local buyers and exporters; Mirzapur Art Palace and similar stores supply both retail visitors and trade buyers sourcing traditional Mirzapur/Bhadohi products. Mirzapur Art Palace is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
