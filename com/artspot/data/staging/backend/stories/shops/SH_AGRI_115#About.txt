Some tastes don’t come from recipes; they come from the land itself.
In the hills of Nagaland, where food is honest, and flavour carries intensity, Naga Brothers was created to bring that truth forward without dilution, without adaptation.
Founded by an Angami collective, the brand works as a farmer-led co-operative, where every harvest is grown, sourced, and crafted within the community it represents. What reaches you is not just authentic, it is traceable, rooted in people who know exactly what they grow.
At its core lies the iconic Naga chilli, bold, fiery, and unmistakably expressive. Handled with care and bottled close to its origin, it retains the raw character that defines the region’s cuisine.
There is no attempt to soften it.
Because some flavours are meant to stay exactly as they are.
