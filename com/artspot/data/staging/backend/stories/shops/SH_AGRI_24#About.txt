In the fertile heart of Palakkad, where paddy fields sway beneath the Western Ghats, Keerthi Rice has been crafting the essence of Palakkadan Matta since 1995. Born as Sreekrishna Modern Rice Mill in Nenmara, the brand carries forward a deep respect for Kerala’s cherished rice heritage.
Each grain, long or short, is carefully milled, hygienically processed, and thoughtfully packed to preserve its earthy aroma, rich texture, and wholesome nourishment. Blending advanced milling technology with time-honoured care, Keerthi Rice ensures purity and consistency in every harvest.
Backed by decades of expertise, international certifications, and a steadfast commitment to quality, Keerthi Rice brings the authentic taste of Palakkad to modern kitchens, reliable, nourishing, and quietly exceptional.
