Founded by local practitioners in Srikalahasti, Bhanodaya emerged to promote the freehand pen-kalamkari tradition unique to the region. The studio is part of a broader cluster of Kalamkari workshops that have sustained the craft through family-run studios and small enterprises. Its history is tied to Srikalahasti’s resurgence as a Kalamkari centre and ongoing efforts to register and protect the craft’s GI status.
