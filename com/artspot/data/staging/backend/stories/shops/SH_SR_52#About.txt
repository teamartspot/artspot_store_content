Based in the culturally rich state of Gujarat, Bandhaniwala Trading Co. is a trusted name in the world of traditional Indian fashion. We specialize in manufacturing, exporting, and supplying an elegant and diverse range of Indian ethnic wear that celebrates heritage with style. Our offerings include intricately designed Art Silk Sarees, Cotton Bandhani Dress Materials, Gaji Silk Sarees, Bandhani Silk Sarees, Cotton Satin Dresses, and more.

Every piece from Bandhaniwala Trading Co. is crafted using premium fabrics and adorned with exquisite embellishments like brocade, stones, sequins, beads, and threadwork. Our commitment to quality is matched by our dedication to preserving and promoting timeless Indian craftsmanship through contemporary expression.
