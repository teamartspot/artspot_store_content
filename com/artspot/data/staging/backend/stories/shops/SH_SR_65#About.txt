Yeola Paithani Kalakari Weaves is a curated initiative dedicated to promoting the authentic handloom artistry of Paithani sarees. Our purpose is to bridge the gap between skilled artisans and global consumers by showcasing the timeless elegance of genuine Yeola Paithanis. Through thoughtful market integration and direct-to-customer experiences, we aim to ensure that the craftsmanship of our weavers receives the recognition and value it truly deserves.

Our platform brings together tradition, transparency, and fair trade to create a sustainable ecosystem for both artisans and admirers of Indian textile heritage.
