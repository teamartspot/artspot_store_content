Puri has long been a center for stone carving linked to the Jagannath cult. 
Artisans carve black chlorite, sandstone and soapstone into religious icons and decorative objects. 
Workshops like Sivansi Arts continue this temple-linked carving tradition. 
The craft is formally recognized under Odisha’s cultural heritage categories. 
By sustaining this trade, the workshop preserves both spiritual and artistic traditions of coastal Odisha.
