In the quiet, forested hills of Sirumalai, farming was once a solitary journey.
Small fields, scattered efforts, and stories that rarely reached beyond the valley.
Then came a simple idea to grow not alone, but together. SVFP was born from this unity, bringing farmers, tribes, and communities into one shared purpose. Here, rare treasures like the Sirumalai banana are not just cultivated, but preserved with care. Knowledge is shared, markets are opened, and dignity is restored to the land and its people. What emerges is more than agriculture; it is resilience shaped collectively. Because true richness is not grown in isolation, but in unity that endures.
