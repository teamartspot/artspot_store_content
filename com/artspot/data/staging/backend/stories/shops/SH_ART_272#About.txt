Surendra Saddlery Surendra Saddlery is a Kanpur-based saddlery and leather goods firm producing bridles, harnesses, belts and riding gear for domestic and export markets. The workshop offers custom orders for equestrian equipment and supplies retailers and sports centres. With a focus on practical durability and leather finishing, the company serves professional riders and equestrian institutions. Surendra Saddlery is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
