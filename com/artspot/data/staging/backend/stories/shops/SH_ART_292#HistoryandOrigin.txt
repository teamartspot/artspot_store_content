Shyama bandhani center shyama Bandhani Centre Bandhani centres such as Shyama evolved from local dyeing clusters where family-led workshops produced ceremonial textiles. Over time, showrooms and retail points emerged to sell finished sarees to tourists and brides; listings and social pages document Shyama Bandhani’s presence in the Jamnagar textile marketplace. Shyama bandhani center is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
