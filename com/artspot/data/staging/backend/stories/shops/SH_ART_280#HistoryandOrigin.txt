Vishwakarma - Banaras Gulabi Meenakari Artist Banaras Gulabi Meenakari has a documented history in Varanasi and is sometimes traced to Persian enamellists whose techniques blended with local metalwork. The craft developed into a distinctive pink-hued enamel tradition associated with Varanasi, transmitted through family workshops and sustained by patronage, temple commissions and modern craft fairs that enabled artisans to adapt designs for contemporary markets. Vishwakarma - Banaras Gulabi Meenakari Artist is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
