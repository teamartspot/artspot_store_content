Beadwaala Beadwaala’s growth as a bead supplier mirrors Varanasi’s long tradition of glass-bead production and trade. Over decades the firm specialised in scaling bead manufacture and satisfying export and retail demand for costume and fashion jewellery components. Beadwaala is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
