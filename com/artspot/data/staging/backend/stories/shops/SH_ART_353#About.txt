Tibetan Handicraft Emporium is a shop on Leh’s Main Bazaar reputedly run by Tibetan refugees. It sells silver and stone jewelry, homewares, and gifts, and is referenced in Ladakh travel directories. Tibetan Handicraft Emporium is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
