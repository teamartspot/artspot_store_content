Sivansi Arts is a Puri-based handicraft enterprise associated with stone sculpture and temple-inspired crafts. 
It is listed in Odisha’s artisan directories as a maker of decorative and religious sculptures. 
The firm sells both small souvenir items and larger temple-style statues. 
Its products are connected with the continuing craft traditions near the Jagannath Temple. 
Sivansi Arts caters to pilgrims, tourists and collectors interested in Puri’s stone craft heritage.
