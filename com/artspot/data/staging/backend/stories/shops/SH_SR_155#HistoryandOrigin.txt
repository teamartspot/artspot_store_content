Founding and Inspiration: In 2016, textile enthusiast Hetal Desai, then 53, reignited her lifelong love for fabrics by creating handcrafted kurtas and palazzos for her daughters. She introduced them on social media, and an order the next day sparked the formal journey of The Indian Ethnic Co.

Growth through Storytelling: What began at Hetal’s Mumbai home evolved into a small studio. By 2020, the team was selling out online launches—most notably a saree drop that sold out in a day—reaching ₹10 crore in turnover and scaling to three offices and a showroom within two years.

Leadership & Craft: The brand is led by Hetal (with a background in home science and textile design) and Lekhinee (an Odissi dancer with an MBA in marketing). United by their love for handloom and crafts rather than formal fashion training, the duo built a sustainable, artisan-focused fashion venture.
