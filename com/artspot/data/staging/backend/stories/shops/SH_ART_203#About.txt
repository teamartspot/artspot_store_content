Cilwana Studio is a Srinagar-based fashion and design label and private limited company run by Nusaiba and Adil Masood, offering contemporary Kashmiri apparel and handcrafted collections. The studio blends traditional Kashmiri embroidery with modern garments, showcasing ready-to-wear and bespoke pieces informed by local motifs. Cilwana also works on creative collaborations and occasionally participates in exhibitions and pop-ups to reach urban markets outside Kashmir. The company positions itself as a platform for young Kashmiri designers aiming to preserve craft skills while providing modern product interpretations.
