Hupari developed as a silver-ornament cluster supported by family-run workshops. 
Generations of artisans practiced casting, forming and filigree suited to daily wear. 
The town’s proximity to Kolhapur encouraged a steady trade in anklets and payals. 
Shops like SRM Jewellery showcase this living tradition in retail storefronts. 
Local designs continue to evolve while maintaining regional identity and techniques.
