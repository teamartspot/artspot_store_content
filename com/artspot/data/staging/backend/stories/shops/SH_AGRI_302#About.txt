Glosky Farm LLP was founded with the vision of delivering fresh, farm-grown produce from Maharashtra to wider domestic and international markets. Based in Solapur, the company focuses on premium agricultural products such as pomegranates, bananas, onions, and fresh vegetables, sourced with an emphasis on quality and freshness. Built on a commitment to reliable supply and hygienic handling, the business combines farming knowledge with export-oriented operations. Their journey reflects a growing agricultural enterprise dedicated to connecting Indian farm produce with global buyers through trust, consistency, and quality service.
