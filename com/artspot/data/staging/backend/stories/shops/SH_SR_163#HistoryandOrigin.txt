The tradition of Patola weaving originated in Gujarat centuries ago, flourishing as a highly refined single-ik at (Rajkot) and double-ikat (Patan) art form. Rajendra Patola continues this age-old legacy, upholding techniques passed down through generations. All our sarees are handwoven in Rajkot by skilled artisans who practice the meticulous resist-dyeing process on silk yarns—a craft that demands patience, precision, and an unwavering commitment to authenticity.
