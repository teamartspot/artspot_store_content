Basabi Terracotta Centre Retailers like Basabi operate as intermediaries between village potters and domestic/international buyers. Over the last several decades, such centres helped popularise Bankura terracotta through tourist circuits, fairs and handicraft expos, ensuring greater market access for potters. Basabi Terracotta Centre is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
