At Shobitam, our vision is simple yet powerful: to make elegant Indian ethnic wear accessible to women around the world. Founded by two fashion-forward sisters, Aparna and Ambika, in the vibrant city of Seattle, Shobitam is the result of their love for timeless drapes, creative design, and a deep connection to Indian handlooms.

Driven by a mission to empower women to look good, feel confident, and do good, our platform offers a carefully curated collection of sarees, jewelry, and ethnic ensembles—each piece crafted with care, affordability, and elegance in mind.

We work directly with artisans and weaving communities across India to co-create exclusive collections, bringing lesser-known and heritage-rich weaves into the global spotlight. At Shobitam, we believe fashion should not only be beautiful, but also purposeful—uplifting both the wearer and the hands that make it.
