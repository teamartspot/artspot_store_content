The journey of KasavuKada began over 50 years ago, rooted in the cultural and artisanal traditions of Kerala. Established by pioneers passionate about preserving the essence of Kasavu, the brand has grown into a torchbearer for traditional handloom clothing.

For the past 32 years, KasavuKada has dedicated itself to not just selling clothing, but celebrating Kerala’s heritage through every weave. Built on the foundations of family values, skilled craftsmanship, and an unwavering commitment to quality, the brand has nurtured strong relationships with artisans across the region.

What began as a humble initiative has transformed into a globally recognized name in handloom fashion, with a future-focused approach that includes creating job-oriented weaving courses, launching skill development institutes, and ensuring that traditional crafts continue to thrive for generations to come.
