Sambalpuri Bastralaya Co-op, based in Bargarh, Odisha, is a pioneering handloom cooperative that has been empowering weavers since 1954. Functioning as an apex production and marketing body under the state’s Handlooms, Textiles & Handicrafts Department, the society supports a vast network of artisans with a turnover of approximately ₹50 crore annually.

We operate as a production-cum-marketing unit that provides weavers with quality raw materials, ensures fair compensation for their craft, and facilitates the sale of their finished goods. Our products are available through 40+ production centers, 43 exclusive outlets, and sales representatives across and beyond Odisha. We also showcase our collections at national expos and exhibitions to expand reach and impact.

From tie-dye sarees and dhotis to bed linens and dress materials in cotton, silk, mercerized, and tussar yarns, every piece is AZO-free, quality-checked, and made with care. Through ethical practices, innovation, and tradition, Sambalpuri Bastralaya Co-op is committed to sustaining livelihoods and preserving Odisha’s vibrant textile legacy.
