Originating from the culturally rich land of Utkal (now Odisha), known as the "Land of Exquisite Art," Mehers Handlooms carries forward a centuries-old legacy of weaving. Odisha’s handloom sarees were once traded with distant lands like Java and Sumatra. In modern times, this legacy was revived by Chaturbhuj Meher, who began weaving as a child and rose to become a master artisan. His journey, marked by resilience and innovation, transformed the traditional craft into globally admired art. Together with his son, Sankar Kumar Meher, they have preserved rare weaving techniques and nurtured a new generation of weavers, safeguarding the future of Odisha’s iconic handlooms.
