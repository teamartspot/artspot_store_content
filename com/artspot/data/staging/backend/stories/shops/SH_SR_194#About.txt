At iTokri, every order carries a personal touch — a handwritten note on recycled paper and a handmade trinket, wrapped lovingly in eco-friendly packaging. Based in Gwalior, iTokri has become one of India’s most beloved e-commerce platforms for handicrafts and handlooms, cherished by customers across the globe who value authenticity, sustainability, and craftsmanship.

As India’s only craft and loom retailer with a 100% handmade and artisanal inventory, iTokri showcases a vibrant range of products — from Punjab’s intricate Phulkari dupattas and Gujarat’s colorful Bandhani sarees to Andhra’s elegant Ikkat fabrics and Odisha’s detailed Pattachitra paintings. Beyond apparel, it offers a vast collection of jewelry, home décor, accessories, and artworks, making handmade heritage accessible to all.
