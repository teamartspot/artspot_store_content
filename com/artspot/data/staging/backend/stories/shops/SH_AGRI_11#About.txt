In the heart of the Konkan coast, where sea breeze meets mineral-rich soil, lies Pawas, Ratnagiri—home to orchards that have nurtured the Alphonso mango for over a century. Desai Products carries forward this legacy, preserving a tradition shaped by land, patience, and generations of devoted cultivation.


Spread across vast orchards and guided by Good Horticultural Practices, every Alphonso is handpicked, naturally ripened, and thoughtfully processed to capture its divine sweetness and signature aroma. From being India’s only successful exporter of Alphonso mangoes to Japan to earning top honors at national mango shows, Desai Products stands as a symbol of authenticity and excellence.


Under the trusted AMAR brand, our mango pulps and signature Alphonso Tidbits are crafted to recreate the experience of biting into a sun-ripened fruit—pure, indulgent, and timeless. This is not just mango processing; it is Konkan’s finest heritage, refined for the world.
