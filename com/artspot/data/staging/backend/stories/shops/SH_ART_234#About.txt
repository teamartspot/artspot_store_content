Lakshmi Jewellery (Payyanur) Lakshmi Jewellery, established in Payyanur, Kannur, is a long-standing jeweller known for crafting and retailing the Payyannur Pavithra rings and traditional Kerala jewellery. The shop offers gold and silver jewellery, including ritual rings, and serves local ceremonial markets while also facilitating orders from visitors and devotees. They maintain contact details and operate through storefront and local outreach.
