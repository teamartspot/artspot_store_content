Kalra’s Cottage Industry kalra's Cottage Industry Kalra's Cottage Industry is an Agra-based rug and carpet manufacturer and showroom known for hand-knotted wool and silk rugs. The company operates as a production-export unit with a long history of servicing domestic and international clients seeking traditional carpets with quality knotting and finish. Their showroom displays regionally inspired designs and custom-made rugs tailored for interior designers and collectors. Kalra's positions itself as a heritage rug house combining handcraft techniques with modern quality control.
