Mahavir Bandhani Mahavir Bandhani’s roots go back to the early 20th century (the brand cites a 1904 heritage) and it has grown into a recognised Bandhani retailer and exporter in Jamnagar. Over the decades the label has helped sustain Jamnagar’s Bandhani production and served as a visible heritage wholesaler for the craft. Mahavir Bandhani is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
