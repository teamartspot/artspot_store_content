Bhadohi Carpets House Bhadohi Carpets House is a well-known Bhadohi manufacturer and retailer of handwoven and tufted rugs, showcasing traditional Indian motifs and contemporary patterns. They offer a wide range of products—hand-knotted, hand-tufted and machine-made rugs—serving interior designers, retailers and end consumers. The brand highlights craft provenance from Bhadohi and provides care instructions, customization and bulk-order services for institutional buyers. Bhadohi Carpets House maintains an online storefront and social presence to reach national and international customers.
