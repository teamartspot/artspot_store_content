Rooted in the historic city of Bhagalpur, Bihar, our brand draws from a centuries-old tradition of silk weaving. Bhagalpur has long been renowned for its unique tussar and Andi silk textiles, known for their natural sheen, texture, and earthy charm. This ancient weaving craft has been passed down through generations of skilled artisans, who have preserved its authenticity while adapting to evolving styles.

Bhagalpur Silks was founded with a mission to support these local weavers and share their remarkable craft with the world. By sourcing directly from the looms and ensuring fair practices, we aim to uphold the legacy of Bhagalpuri silk while offering our customers unmatched quality and heritage in every weave.
