Gallery Gold is a long-established contemporary art gallery in Kolkata (Kalighat) founded and run by Shaankar Sen, showcasing fine art, paintings and sculptures by established and mid‑career Indian artists. The gallery hosts exhibitions, art events and supports emerging artists through curated shows, providing collectors access to original works and limited editions. Gallery Gold also participates in art fairs and collaborates with institutions for public programming, contributing to Kolkata’s vibrant art scene. The gallery maintains a small team for curation, sales and client servicing and runs an active contact line for enquiries.
