In the historic city of Amritsar, where tradition meets global ambition, Supple Tek began its journey in 1977 with a simple foundation built on honesty and trust. What started as a domestic sourcing effort gradually transformed into a global powerhouse, now recognized as one of the world’s largest exporters of Indian basmati rice. What makes its story unique is its steady evolution from local trade to reaching over 90 countries, while maintaining a deep commitment to quality in every grain. With state-of-the-art milling facilities and a strong “paddy-to-plate” approach, each step is designed to preserve purity and authenticity. Behind its growth lies a vision not just to export rice, but to carry the aroma of Indian basmati across the world. Guided by values of integrity, innovation, and responsibility, it continues to build a legacy that balances scale with sincerity. In every grain it delivers, Supple Tek tells a story of ambition rooted firmly in trust.
