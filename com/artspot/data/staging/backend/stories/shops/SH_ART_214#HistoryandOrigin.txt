Bastar Handicraft grew from local artisan initiatives in Jagdalpur to a formal retail and supplier unit serving regional markets. The business has long-standing ties to Bastar’s tribal communities—where metal casting and terracotta are traditional crafts—and acts as a conduit between village artisans and urban buyers. Over years the proprietor formalized contacts with craft cooperatives and increasingly used trade portals to reach national customers. The enterprise helps preserve Bastar’s craft vocabulary by sustaining demand for heritage object forms and decorative items.
