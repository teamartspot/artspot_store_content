At Utkalika (OSCHC Ltd.), our mission is to preserve and promote the exquisite artistry of Odisha’s traditional handicrafts and handlooms. Established with a vision to support local artisans, we serve as a bridge between heritage craft communities and modern marketplaces. We empower artisans through marketing support, design innovation, and skill development—while offering customers beautifully handcrafted products rich in culture.

To ensure broader reach and impact, we showcase our creations online and collaborate with national e-commerce platforms such as Flipkart and Gocoop. Whether through vibrant exhibitions, curated collections, or digital outreach, Utkalika (OSCHC Ltd.) brings Odisha’s timeless craftsmanship to global audiences.
