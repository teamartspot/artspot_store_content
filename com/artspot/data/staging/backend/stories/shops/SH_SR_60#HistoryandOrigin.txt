The saree traces its lineage back to the Indus Valley Civilization, where early traces of cotton cultivation and natural dyes birthed one of the world’s oldest unstitched garments. Over thousands of years, this drape evolved — embracing regional styles, cultural shifts, and climatic needs — to become the timeless attire we know today.

With over 70 regional draping styles and fabric variations tailored for every season and lifestyle, the saree has become universal — worn with pride by rural women, urban professionals, royalty, and artisans alike.

Very Much Indian was born out of a desire to honour this timeless legacy. We work directly with weaving communities across India to bring you sarees steeped in tradition and woven with care. With each saree, you’re not just wearing fabric — you're draping generations of craftsmanship, stories, and culture.
