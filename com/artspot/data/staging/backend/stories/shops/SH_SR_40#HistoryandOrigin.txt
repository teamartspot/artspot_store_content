Taneira was born with a singular purpose—to preserve India’s weaving traditions while creating meaningful opportunities for the artisans behind them. Backed by years of design research and community collaboration, the brand quickly grew into a trusted platform representing the finest regional weaves from states like Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Chhattisgarh, and Uttar Pradesh.

To deepen this impact, Vivira launched its pioneering “Weaver Hubs”—dedicated spaces to empower craft communities with advanced looms, health facilities, skill training, and fair wages. These hubs in cities like Varanasi, Champa, Coimbatore, Phulia, and Baruipur are shaping a more sustainable and dignified future for Indian handloom artisans.

Every saree from Vivira carries a story—from warp to weft—and stands as a tribute to cultural legacy, ethical fashion, and the hands that weave dreams into fabric.
