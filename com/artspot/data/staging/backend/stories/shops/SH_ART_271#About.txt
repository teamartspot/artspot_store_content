Kanpur Saddle House Kanpur Saddle House is a manufacturer and exporter specialising in equestrian saddlery, harnesses, leather goods and bags produced in Kanpur's leather belt. The company supplies saddles, bridles and horse-riding equipment to domestic and international customers, combining traditional leather-craft skills with industrial production capabilities. Their online presence showcases product categories and export readiness, addressing both sport and leisure equestrian markets. Kanpur Saddle House is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
