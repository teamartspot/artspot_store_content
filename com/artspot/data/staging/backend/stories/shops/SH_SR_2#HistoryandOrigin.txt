The legacy of Veer Sons. began in 1987 with a deep-rooted passion for preserving the traditional art of Chikankari embroidery. What started as a humble venture by Mr. S.K. Jain has grown into a celebrated name in handcrafted fashion, known for combining age-old techniques with modern aesthetics.

Drawing inspiration from the cultural richness of Lucknow, Veer Sons. has nurtured a community of skilled artisans who have mastered the fine art of Chikankari. Over the years, the brand has remained committed to sustaining this heritage while evolving with contemporary trends.

Today, with over three decades of craftsmanship and creativity, Veer Sons. continues to honor tradition while redefining elegance for the modern world.
