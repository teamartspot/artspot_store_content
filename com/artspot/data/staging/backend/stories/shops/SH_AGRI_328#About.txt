Satbahani Agro Pvt. Ltd. (SAPL) was founded with the vision of delivering high-quality rice that reflects the rich agricultural heritage of Bihar. Starting from humble beginnings and backed by more than 25 years of experience in rice milling and paddy procurement, the company grew into one of the region’s leading rice processors. Based in Aurangabad, Bihar, SAPL combines modern milling technology with strong partnerships with local farmers to ensure purity, consistency, and superior grain quality. Through its flagship brand, Republic Rice, the company brings premium Katarni, Sonam, and other rice varieties to customers across India. Their journey reflects a commitment to preserving Bihar’s farming legacy while embracing innovation, sustainability, and farmer-first growth.
