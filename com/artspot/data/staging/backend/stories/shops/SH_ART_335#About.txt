The Manamadurai Pottery Workers Co-operative is a documented cooperative of potters in Manamadurai supporting over 100 artisan families.
Academic papers and government cooperative lists reference the co-operative as a local institution for pottery production.
The society provides shared kiln facilities, training and market linkages to local potter families and cottage producers.
The co-operative model is part of Tamil Nadu’s efforts to sustain local cottage industries and ensure fair wages and distribution.
Public documents and research papers corroborate the cooperative's longstanding role in Manamadurai's pottery sector.
