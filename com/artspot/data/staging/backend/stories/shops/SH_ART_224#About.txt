Ghamani Bai is an independent craftsperson (Karnataka) known locally for her practice in traditional craft skills—often in terracotta, weaving or regional embroidery—working with community groups and local craft centres for sales and commissions. As an individual artisan she participates in local fairs and contributes to community craft showcases, keeping regional material and decorative traditions alive through practice and teaching. Her work typically reflects local motifs and functional art suitable for household use or ornamentation.
