Bundelkhand Bell Metal Handicraft Bundelkhand Bell Metal Handicraft refers to the craft cluster in Tikamgarh and Datia districts of Madhya Pradesh. Artisans here produce bell metal utensils, pooja articles, and decorative objects using traditional casting methods. The craft is distinguished by its durability and rustic aesthetic, and continues to be practised by artisan families across the region. Cooperative groups and independent artisans market these products locally and nationally.
