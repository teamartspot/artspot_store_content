Madhukar Bagh Print developed as part of Bagh’s revival of wood‑block printing traditions, leveraging the village’s historic hand‑printing legacy. The workshop consolidated local block cutters and printers, formalised production workflows and began catering to both domestic retail and export channels. Over time it established a presence on social platforms, enabling customers to access traditional Bagh designs and natural‑dye printed clothes from a reliable source in the Bagh region.
