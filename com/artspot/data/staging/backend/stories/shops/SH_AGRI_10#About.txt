Founded in 2016, Kubalwadi began with a simple devotion—to preserve the true taste of India’s finest mangoes. Rooted in the sun-washed orchards of Ratnagiri and Devgad, their journey started with authentic Alphonso mangoes, grown with care and respect for nature. Guided by the trust of their patrons, they later embraced the golden richness of Kesar mangoes from Gujarat’s Junagadh region.


When seasons faded but the love for mangoes endured, Kubalwadi evolved. By thoughtfully preserving Alphonso and Kesar mangoes at peak harvest, they extended their essence beyond time and distance—pure, unadulterated, and natural. Today, this philosophy flows into a curated selection of organic foods, united by one belief: true luxury lies in authenticity, sustainability, and harmony with nature.
