Thirukkanur Papier-Mâché Artisans Thirukkanur papier‑mâché artisans (Puducherry) produce painted papier‑mâché items such as decorative boxes, masks and giftware following traditional lacquering and varnishing techniques. The craft utilises papier‑mâché pulp formed on molds, followed by intricate hand‑painting with natural and synthetic pigments. Products range from miniature tableaux and religious motifs to colourful home décor pieces popular with tourists. Artisans sell through local craft emporia and workshops, often training visitors in basic papier‑mâché techniques.
