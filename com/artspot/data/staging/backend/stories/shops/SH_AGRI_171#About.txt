In the quiet valleys of Marayoor, where sugarcane has been nurtured for generations, a sweeter story began, one rooted in purity and care. At Messa Marayoor Jaggery, tradition is not left untouched, but refined with a promise of hygiene and precision. Founded in 2018, the journey set out to transform how jaggery is made, retaining its authenticity while elevating its safety and quality. Each batch is crafted from 100% natural sugarcane juice, free from chemicals or artificial colors, preserving its true richness. Handmade using time-honoured techniques, yet guided by modern certifications like FSSAI and ISO, every piece reflects both heritage and trust. As a GI-tagged Marayoor jaggery producer, the origin itself becomes a mark of authenticity and distinction. From local fields to homes across the country and beyond, the journey carries consistency, care, and credibility. Because true sweetness is not created in haste, it is patiently crafted, where tradition meets thoughtful innovation.
