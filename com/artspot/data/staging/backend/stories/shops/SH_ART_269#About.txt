Jain Glass & Handicraft Jain Glass & Handicraft is a Firozabad-based glassware and handicraft dealer known for mouth-blown and molded glass articles, decorative glassware and functional household items. The shop sources products from Firozabad's wider glass-manufacturing cluster and serves local and regional customers with a diverse range of glass goods. Their offerings include decorative bowls, vases, and festival glassware often sold to retailers and wholesalers.
