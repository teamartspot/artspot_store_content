Mahavir Bandhani Mahavir Bandhani is an established Bandhani textile house based in Jamnagar (Gujarat), active on social media and trade listings. The firm specialises in traditional tie-and-dye (bandhani) sarees, dupattas and garments, serving retail customers and exporting traditional Bandhani textiles and wedding attire. Mahavir Bandhani is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
