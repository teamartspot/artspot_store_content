Harish Kumar Soni Harish Kumar Soni is a Madhya Pradesh‑based artisan known for his work in metal crafts and traditional decorative art. He represents the artisan community engaged in Bundelkhand’s bell metal craft, producing ritual items, utensils, and decorative figures. His work has been showcased at regional fairs and exhibitions that highlight Madhya Pradesh’s craft heritage. His shop provides authentic handmade bell metal items to buyers seeking heritage crafts.
