Rathna Metal Mart Incorporated as a partnership in 1977, Rathna Metal Mart has been serving the devotional and decorative metal crafts sector for more than 40 years. Located in Kumbakonam, a historic centre of South Indian metal artistry, the firm represents continuity of brass and bronze lamp‑making traditions. Over time, the mart expanded into wholesale and export channels while preserving its artisanal roots in temple and household crafts.
