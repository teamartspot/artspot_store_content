Established in 1980, Sri Venkateswara Saree Emporium has stood as a symbol of timeless craftsmanship in the Indian textile industry. What began as a humble initiative with just eight handlooms has flourished into a renowned manufacturer of pure Kanchipuram silk sarees, now supported by a network of over 100 looms.

Dedicated to preserving the elegance of traditional handloom weaving, Sri Venkateswara Saree Emporium continues to uphold the legacy of Kanchipuram artistry—one exquisite saree at a time.
