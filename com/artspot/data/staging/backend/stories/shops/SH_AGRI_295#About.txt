Born from an ambition to create impact across both infrastructure and agriculture, Adhij Infra & Commerce has shaped a distinctive journey where development and farming progress side by side. Based in Solapur’s fertile landscape, the enterprise brings together thousands of farmers, modern agricultural practices, and a commitment to delivering premium bananas, pomegranates, sugar, and allied produce to global markets, while also contributing to land development and road construction. More than a conventional business, Adhij’s story is one of building connections between rural producers and international buyers, between infrastructure and opportunity, and between local potential and global growth.
