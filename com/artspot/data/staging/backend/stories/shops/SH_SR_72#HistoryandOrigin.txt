The Phulkari was born from a moment of realization—a journey across Punjab led by our founder, Prabhav Chopra, in search of the soul of a fading tradition. Once lovingly hand-stitched by women as heirlooms for generations, Phulkari embroidery had begun to disappear, its intricate patterns and radiant spirit becoming scarce in the modern world.

Moved by the loss of this rich legacy, Prabhav founded The Phulkari with a heartfelt mission: to preserve and elevate the timeless craft. Working directly with skilled artisans across Punjab, we marry traditional handwork with modern design sensibilities to create authentic, high-quality pieces that honour the past while embracing the present.

Each product from The Phulkari is not just a garment—it’s a living artifact of Punjabi culture, emotion, and artistry. Our journey is one of revival, empowerment, and storytelling, dedicated to bringing the spirit of Phulkari back into wardrobes and homes across the world.
