Founded in 1924 by the visionary Sri Rm.K. Visvanatha Pillai, RmKV began its journey with a commitment to redefine silk weaving traditions through quality and innovation. Over the decades, the brand has flourished under the leadership of successive generations — most notably Sri V. Kumaraswamy and Sri K. Viswanathan — and is today guided by Sri Rm.K. Sivakumar, who continues the family’s proud legacy.

RmKV’s contribution to handloom silk weaving has been nationally recognized, with the brand earning two National Awards for its innovative techniques and design excellence. What started as a modest endeavor has evolved into a powerhouse of textile innovation — credited with bringing thematic storytelling into silk sarees and transforming customer engagement through personalized design options.

As RmKV celebrates its centenary, it stands not just as a retail brand but as a custodian of India's handloom heritage, continuously innovating while staying deeply rooted in tradition.
