Launched just a year ago, Kunbi Boutique began as a humble venture with a passion for India’s rich handloom legacy. The overwhelming support and love from our patrons propelled us forward at a remarkable pace.

What started with a thoughtfully selected saree collection soon evolved into a broader platform for ethnic fashion, sourcing directly from artisans and weavers across multiple states. At its core, Kunbi Boutique stands for inclusivity, empowerment, and the beauty of tradition carried forward into the modern era—crafted for women of all ages, styles, and stories.
