Abdul Rehman Naqash and Sons Abdul Rehman Naqash and Sons is a Srinagar-based Naqash (wood‑carving and papier‑mâché) workshop creating ornamental wood panels, kufis, and decorative artifacts reflecting traditional Kashmiri motifs. The studio combines carving, lacquering and inlay work with painted papier‑mâché to produce temple panels, prayer stands and bespoke furnishings. They supply both retail customers and institutional restorations, often collaborating with local restoration specialists for heritage projects.
