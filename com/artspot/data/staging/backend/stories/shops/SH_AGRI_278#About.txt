From the sun-soaked fields of Sangli, Umrani Farms tells a refreshing story where agriculture is nurtured not just by nature, but by the empowering hands of women farmers committed to sustainable cultivation. Built on the belief that real food begins with healthy soil and mindful farming, the enterprise transforms naturally grown harvests from wholesome grains to signature sun-dried raisins into honest nourishment for modern homes. More than a farm, it is a movement where tradition, environmental care, and women-led resilience come together, creating food that carries the warmth of the land and the promise of a healthier tomorrow.
