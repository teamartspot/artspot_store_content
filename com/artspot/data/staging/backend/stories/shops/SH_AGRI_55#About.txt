In the spice-laden hills of Wayanad, Kerala, where the air carries the fragrance of pepper, cardamom, and turmeric, Mulakupetti was born from a shared passion for preserving Kerala’s spice heritage. Founded by three friends with deep roots in these fertile lands, the brand began with a simple vision to bring the authentic taste of Kerala’s finest spices directly from farmers to kitchens around the world.
Working closely with local growers, Mulakupetti sources single-origin, farm-fresh spices, ensuring fair partnerships and sustainable practices that honor generations of farming wisdom. Each spice is carefully selected and prepared to preserve its natural aroma, color, and bold character.
More than a spice brand, Mulakupetti is a celebration of origin, a bridge between the legendary spice gardens of Kerala and homes that value purity, authenticity, and the timeless art of flavor.
