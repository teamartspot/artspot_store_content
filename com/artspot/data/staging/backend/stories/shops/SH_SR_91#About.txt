Kerala State Handloom Development Corporation Ltd, widely known as Hanveev, is a government-backed organization devoted to preserving Kerala's rich handloom heritage. With a commitment to empowering traditional weavers, Hanveev promotes hand-woven craftsmanship through sustainable employment and authentic products.

Headquartered in Kannur, a region globally celebrated for its loom legacy, Hanveev has become a trusted name in both domestic and international markets. The brand represents authenticity, quality, and heritage — woven into every fabric. With over 1.5 million meters of fabric produced annually by nearly 2,000 skilled artisans across 32 centers in Kerala, Hanveev plays a vital role in keeping this timeless tradition alive.

Backed by qualified professionals, state-of-the-art operations, and strong government support, Hanveev continues to stand as a guardian of Kerala’s handloom sector.
