SRM Jewellery operates from Hupari, a silver-jewellery hub near Kolhapur. 
The shop specializes in anklets, toe rings and traditional Maharashtrian silver ornaments. 
Retail and wholesale buyers use Hupari for quick access to craft-focused designs. 
SRM Jewellery presents classic patterns alongside contemporary updates for urban customers. 
Its catalog reflects everyday wear as well as festive and bridal selections.
