Sangola Agro Pvt Ltd was established in 2005 with the vision of taking India’s premium fruits to international markets, led by Prabhakar Chandane, a pioneer in commercial pomegranate cultivation. Backed by decades of agricultural and marketing expertise, the company built a strong network of thousands of farmers to create a reliable export ecosystem for fresh produce. Starting with pomegranate exports to Europe and global markets, the business later expanded into bananas through contract farming to ensure consistent quality and supply. Over time, Sangola Agro also diversified into additional fruits, vegetables, and imported produce, reflecting its ambition to grow as a trusted name in both domestic and international agricultural trade
