Langdaaam was created with the vision of bringing the authentic taste of Bihar’s famous Langra mangoes directly from orchard owners to consumers. Founded by brothers Deepak and Vikash Kumar, the venture began as an effort to support local mango growers while preserving the heritage of the renowned Bhagalpur Langra variety. By working closely with farmers and eliminating middlemen, the brand ensures naturally ripened, farm-fresh mangoes reach customers across India. Their journey reflects a commitment to quality, fair returns for growers, and showcasing Bihar’s rich mango-growing tradition on a national scale.
