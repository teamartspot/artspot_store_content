Shree Ganesh Jari Covering Pvt.Ltd. shree Ganesh Jari Covering Shree Ganesh Jari Covering (Ganesh Group) is a Surat-based industrial manufacturer of flora zari, polyester jari, nylon jari and various metallic embroidery threads serving national and international customers. The group produces a wide variety of metallic threads and yarns used in zardozi, kasab and machine embroidery. The company supplies larger manufacturers, embroidery units and exporters with consistent-grade zari used in bridal wear, ceremonial textiles and decorative products.
