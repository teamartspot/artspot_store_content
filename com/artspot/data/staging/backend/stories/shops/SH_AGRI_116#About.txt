A Memory That Refused to Fade
It began with a taste that couldn’t be recreated.
Far from home, a simple broth once effortless in a Naga kitchen felt incomplete, missing something impossible to name. The answer wasn’t in technique, but in the land itself, the wild biodiversity of the Indo-Burma region, where every ingredient carries a depth shaped by nature, not intervention.
Hill Wild was born from that realisation. Not as a brand, but as a return to flavours rooted in Indigenous knowledge, to recipes carried by grandmothers, and to a food system that has quietly sustained communities for generations.
Each ingredient is sourced directly from villages across Manipur and Nagaland, where farming is still guided by seasons, intuition, and respect for the ecosystem. What emerges is not just flavour, but a living expression of place, wild, complex, and deeply original.
At the same time, the journey extends beyond the plate. By working closely with Indigenous farmers, restoring biodiversity, and creating fair value systems, Hill Wild reshapes how food travels from source to story.
Some flavours are remembered. Others lead you back to where they belong.
