Koftgiri Art Workshop with Shyamlata Gahlot is a Udaipur workshop noted for koftgiri (gold/silver inlay on steel and arms). It is run by craftsperson Shyamlata Gahlot. Koftgiri Art Workshop with Shyamlata Gahlot is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
