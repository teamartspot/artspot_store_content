Imran Ahmed Sofi Imran Ahmed Sofi is a Srinagar-based artisan known for traditional papier‑mâché and wood carving, producing decorative lacquered boxes, trays and traditional Kashmiri souvenirs. His work reflects careful hand‑painting, floral motifs and the characteristic Kashmiri palette applied to papier‑mâché surfaces. Imran often participates in local craft fairs and supplies galleries and emporia that promote Kashmir handicrafts to tourists and collectors.
