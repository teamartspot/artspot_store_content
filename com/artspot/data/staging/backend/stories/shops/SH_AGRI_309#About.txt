Kiyansh Exporters and Suppliers was founded with the vision of taking Maharashtra’s premium banana produce from the farms of Jalgaon, Raver, and Solapur to domestic and international markets. Rooted in India’s banana-growing heartland, the company specializes in export-quality G9 and Cavendish bananas sourced directly from farmers with a strong focus on freshness and quality. Built on values of trust, sustainability, and ethical sourcing, the business emphasizes strict quality control, customized packaging, and reliable logistics. Their journey reflects a commitment to empowering local farmers while showcasing India’s agricultural strength on the global stage through consistent and high-standard produce exports.
