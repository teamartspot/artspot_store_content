Gulam Hussan Wood Carvers Gulam Hussan Wood Carvers (Jammu & Kashmir) are a traditional family of artisans producing carved walnut furniture, decorative panels and small artefacts in Kashmir. Their output includes deeply carved doors, handrails, trays and ornamental boxes featuring floral and geometric motifs typical of Kashmiri design. They serve both local clients and tourists, offering commission work and restoration of antique pieces.
