Meghloom is a producer-owned enterprise established by Khasi Eri silk rearers, hand spinners, dyers, and weavers. In partnership with the Meghalaya State Rural Livelihood Mission (MSRLM) and FDRVC, Meghloom brings hand-spun, naturally dyed, and handwoven Eri silk products from the Ribhoi district of Meghalaya. Rooted in the rural economy, every product is crafted with care using traditional techniques and natural dyes sourced from local flora. Meghloom’s mission is to empower rural women by transforming generational skills into sustainable livelihoods.
