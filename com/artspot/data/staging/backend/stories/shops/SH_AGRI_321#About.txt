Kolkata Rice, established under Sandhya Food Care in 2010, is an online rice retailer focused on bringing premium-quality rice directly from trusted farmers to consumers. The brand emphasizes naturally grown, hygienically packed rice free from harmful chemicals and pesticides. Rooted in Kolkata’s culinary heritage, its name reflects the city’s deep cultural connection with rice as a daily staple. Their motto, “From Fields to Families,” highlights their commitment to delivering authentic, farm-fresh rice with care and sustainability. Through their in-house brand Gopal, they blend traditional values with modern quality standards to serve families with trusted rice products.
