Pokaran Pottery Pokaran’s pottery techniques reflect regional clay choices and kiln practices that evolved under desert conditions; local potters preserved utilitarian forms that later found a market among collectors and tourists. Tourism and craft pages describe Pokaran pottery as part of Rajasthan’s broad ceramic heritage. Pokaran Pottery is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
