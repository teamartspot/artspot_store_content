What began in 1995 as a modest raisin trading venture built on entrepreneurial grit gradually blossomed into Malu Agrotech, a family-led enterprise carrying the sweetness of Indian vineyards to markets across the world. Rooted in Sangli’s rich agricultural heritage, the company grew through strong farmer relationships, modern processing innovation, and an unwavering promise of quality. From domestic trade to global exports, its journey reflects a story of resilience, vision, and generational commitment where every raisin is not just a product, but a testament to tradition, progress, and customer trust.
