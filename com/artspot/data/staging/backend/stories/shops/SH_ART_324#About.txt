Tamta Mohalla (Almora) refers to a traditional artisan quarter in Almora associated with metalworking—Tamta meaning copper/tin—and houses small workshops producing copperware, utensils and decorative metal items. The neighbourhood includes artisans practising repoussé, beating and finishing techniques used for household and temple utensils. Visitors find Tamta Mohalla as a cluster where local metal craft traditions are still practised and sold.
