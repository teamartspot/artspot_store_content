Hastshilp Sewa Samiti is a Delhi-based non-profit that organises craft promotion, exhibitions, and artisan welfare programs.
Their public website lists contact details and explains work in artisan training, marketing support and exhibitions.
The organisation connects rural artisans to urban markets via craft fairs and Dilli Haat-style events.
Hastshilp Sewa Samiti also facilitates design workshops and supports livelihoods for handicraft artisans.
Directory and official pages confirm the organisation's address in North Delhi and its programmatic focus.
