Taneira, an ethnic wear brand from the house of TATA, offers a wide range of sarees, dress materials, and ready-to-wear kurtas made from pure, natural fabrics. Celebrating the essence of Indian culture, Taneira curates over 100 textile clusters from across the country, blending traditional craftsmanship with contemporary style. Designed for the modern Indian woman, the brand offers elegant collections for every occasion—from everyday wear to grand celebrations.
