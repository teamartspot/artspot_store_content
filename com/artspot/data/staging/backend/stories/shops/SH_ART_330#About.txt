NLSK Nadaswaram Maker is listed in local business directories as a maker of traditional nadaswaram (classical South Indian wind instrument).
Directory entries show an address near the railway station in Narasinganpettai (a village near Kumbakonam reputed for nadaswaram making).
The listing confirms they manufacture, tune and repair nadaswarams and supply musicians and temples.
Narasinganpettai is a recognised centre for nadaswaram making; listings confirm multiple small workshops there.
Contact and local directory entries provide operational details but no formal founding-year on public listings.
