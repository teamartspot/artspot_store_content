Mughal Carpet Company Founded in the late 1990s, Mughal Carpet Company built on Agra's existing craft and tourism infrastructure to serve both visitors and trade buyers. The firm professionalised production methods, integrating design development, dyeing and finishing to produce export-standard carpets. Over time the company established a visible presence in Agra's Tajganj area, catering to both tourists seeking souvenirs and interior designers sourcing handcrafted rugs.
