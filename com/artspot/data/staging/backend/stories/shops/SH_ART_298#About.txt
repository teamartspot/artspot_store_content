Basabi Terracotta Centre Basabi Terracotta Centre (Bishnupur/Bankura) is a wholesaler and retail outlet dealing with Bankura terracotta and Panchmura items — pots, horses and decorative figurines. Local business listings and craft directories list Basabi as a marketplace presence for tourist and trade purchases. Basabi Terracotta Centre is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
