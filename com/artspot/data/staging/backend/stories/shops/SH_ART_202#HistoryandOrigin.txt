Founded by local entrepreneur Aiman Jehan, Zari Poshak grew from small atelier-style beginnings into a recognised local boutique in Safakadal, Srinagar. The brand developed through family and local artisan networks that specialise in metal-thread embroidery (tilla) and aari techniques. By offering customization and direct retail, Zari Poshak helps sustain the livelihoods of embroiderers practicing age-old methods, while adapting designs for contemporary tastes.
