In the quiet valleys of Palampur, where the mountains cradle endless green, a story of tea has been passed down through generations. For Surya, tea was never just a beverage it was a way of life, woven into childhood memories of misty estates, factory visits, and conversations steeped in tradition.
As a fourth-generation planter, he returns not just to inherit, but to refine. At Wah Tea Estate, every leaf is nurtured under his watchful eye, crafted with an intimacy that only comes from living amidst the gardens themselves. Here, tea is not sourced; it is lived, grown, and perfected at its origin.
With a vision of true garden-to-cup purity, the journey of each harvest remains untouched by distance or dilution, delivered just as it was meant to be, authentic and unhurried.
Wah is more than tea; it is a personal legacy, gently brewed into every cup, where heritage meets quiet luxury, and every sip tells a story.
