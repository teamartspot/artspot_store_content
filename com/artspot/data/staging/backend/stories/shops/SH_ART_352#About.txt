Ladakh Art Palace is a retail brand with a storefront in Leh Main Market and an active e-commerce presence. It highlights jewelry, decor and Tibetan/Ladakhi crafts, and appears in local shopping directories. Ladakh Art Palace is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
