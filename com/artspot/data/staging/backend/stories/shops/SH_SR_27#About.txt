Welcome to Pochampally Sarees, where timeless artistry meets generational devotion. We are a proud family-run establishment rooted deeply in the soul of Pochampally’s iconic Ikat weaving tradition. For over three generations, our family has been dedicated to crafting sarees that honor the cultural richness and precision of this celebrated textile art.

Our story began with our grandfather—a skilled master weaver—whose hands brought to life the intricate patterns that define Pochampally Ikat. His passion was inherited by our father, who safeguarded these traditional weaving methods while embracing subtle innovations that enriched both form and finesse. Today, we continue to uphold this legacy, blending age-old craftsmanship with refined modern aesthetics.

Weaving at Pochampally Sarees is not simply a livelihood—it’s a sacred tradition. Each saree is a reflection of skills passed from generation to generation, where every thread carries stories of patience, precision, and pride. Our commitment lies in preserving the essence of Ikat while offering sarees that feel timeless yet contemporary.
