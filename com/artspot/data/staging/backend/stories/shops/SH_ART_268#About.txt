Patans Patola Art Patans Patola Art is a family-run weaving workshop and retail outlet in Patan, Gujarat, managed by traditional patola families (Patolawalas) producing double-ikat sarees. The studio offers bespoke patola sarees woven on pit looms using hand-dyed yarns, and provides in-house demonstrations for interested buyers. Their pieces are noted for intricate symmetry and vibrant color palettes, highly prized by collectors and cultural connoisseurs.
