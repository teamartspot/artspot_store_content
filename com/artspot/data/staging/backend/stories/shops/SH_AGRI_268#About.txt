What began in 1972 in the bustling agricultural heart of Lasalgaon, Maharashtra, grew from a family’s entrepreneurial vision into Jain Export a trusted bridge between India’s fertile farms and international markets. Strengthened by decades of experience and enduring relationships with dedicated farmers, the company built its reputation on delivering fresh, nutrient-rich agro products with uncompromising quality and integrity. From onions and garlic to a broader range of produce, Jain Export’s journey reflects a legacy where honest trade, timely delivery, and global trust come together, carrying the essence of Indian agriculture across borders.
