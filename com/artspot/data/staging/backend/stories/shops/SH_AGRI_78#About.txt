In the tranquil embrace of the Dhauladhar mountains, where the air carries whispers of a 160-year-old tea legacy, Manjhee Valley Estate stands as a story of devotion, lineage, and quiet refinement.
Drawn to Kangra by a deep-rooted connection to tea, the Singh family found not just an estate, but a calling nurturing bushes first planted in the 1880s. Here, rare Chinese varietals flourish, giving rise to teas of delicate, floral character, subtle, elegant, and unmistakably unique to this valley.
Guided by generations of mastery from the legendary “light and bright” craftsmanship to modern expressions of pure, flavored teas, each harvest is shaped with precision, patience, and an intimate understanding of the leaf.
Manjhee Valley is more than an estate; it is a living heritage, where every cup reveals a story of mountains, memory, and mastery… crafted for those who seek tea in its most refined form.
