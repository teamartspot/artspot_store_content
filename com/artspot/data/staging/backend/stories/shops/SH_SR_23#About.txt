Utkalamrita, the flagship brand of Sabat Exports Private Limited based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, was co-founded by two visionary women entrepreneurs—Dr. Anita Sabat and Amrita Sabat. Incorporated on May 11, 2015, the brand officially began operations on July 17, 2015, during the sacred Naba Jaubana Darshana day of the Nabakalebara Rath Yatra, a deeply auspicious occasion marked by devotion to Lord Jagannath.

At Utkalamrita, we specialize in a curated range of Odisha’s finest heritage products, including handwoven textiles, silver filigree jewellery, brass artifacts, tribal ornaments, terracotta crafts, and folk paintings such as Pattachitra and Saura. Our collection also features eco-conscious items crafted from paper-mâché, marble dust, pipili appliqué, and more—each echoing Odisha’s timeless elegance.
