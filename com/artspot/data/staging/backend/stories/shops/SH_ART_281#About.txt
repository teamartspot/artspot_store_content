Sudha Handicrafts Sudha Handicrafts is a Varanasi-based handicraft retailer operating from prominent local marketplaces and arcades; the business sells Banaras textiles, woodcraft, toys and tourist-focused souvenirs. Local business listings show the shop in AG Palace/Golghar area, and they serve both local footfall and occasional wholesale orders. The store’s inventory typically reflects the region’s diverse craft idioms, from textiles to wooden lacquerware. Sudha Handicrafts is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
