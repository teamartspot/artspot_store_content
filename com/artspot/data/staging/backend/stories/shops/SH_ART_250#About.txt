Santoshkumar Anjaneya Chitragar Santoshkumar Anjaneya Chitragar (Karnataka) is a traditional artist and chitragar (painter) known for religious and folk tableaux, mural work and framed paintings used in temple and festival contexts. His practice includes mythological scenes, ritual paintings and community mural projects that draw on regional iconography and temple art styles. The artist undertakes commissions for local temples and cultural organisations and teaches traditional painting techniques to apprentices.
