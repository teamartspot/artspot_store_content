Since 1994, Kumud Group has carried forward a quiet legacy of purity, precision, and trust. Rooted in the fertile lands of Uttar Pradesh and strengthened in the heartland of Basmati in Karnal, the journey began with a simple promise to deliver grains that honor both tradition and taste.
Over three decades, that promise has matured into excellence. With advanced milling technology and meticulous quality refinement, every grain of rice and every pulse is crafted to preserve its natural aroma, texture, and nourishment. From fragrant, long-grain Basmati to wholesome dals, Kumud blends heritage wisdom with modern innovation, serving homes and global markets alike.
Guided by sustainability, ethical sourcing, and an unwavering pursuit of perfection, Kumud Group stands for more than quality food. It represents a tradition refined over time, authentic, dependable, and quietly distinguished.
