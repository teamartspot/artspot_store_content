Villianur Terracotta Works Villianur terracotta has a documented lineage spanning many generations and was GI‑registered in 2011 to protect its identity. Master craftsmen like V.K. Munusamy have raised the craft’s profile nationally and internationally, including workshops at NIFT and UNESCO recognition for artisans. The craft’s institutional oversight includes quality committees and ties to Pondicherry’s craft organisations to preserve traditional methods and training.
