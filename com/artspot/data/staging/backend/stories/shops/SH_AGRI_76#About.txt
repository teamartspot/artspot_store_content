Born from India’s storied tea landscapes, Tea1888 is a quiet tribute to heritage, craftsmanship, and the art of fine tea. Drawing from the pristine foothills of Kangra and other celebrated gardens, each leaf is carefully handpicked and brought directly from estate to cup, untouched, authentic, and alive with character.
At Tea1888, tradition is not preserved; it is refined. Age-old cultivation meets thoughtful innovation, where teas are delicately processed, sealed at origin, and curated into blends that honor both purity and modern taste.
From classic greens and bold blacks to elegant floral infusions, every cup unfolds like a story, subtle, layered, and deeply rooted in its origin.
Tea1888 is more than tea; it is a gentle indulgence, where heritage lingers, and every sip carries the quiet luxury of India’s finest leaves.
