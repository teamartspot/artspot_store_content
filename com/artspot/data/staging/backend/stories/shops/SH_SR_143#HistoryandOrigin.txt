Boutique Dheu was born from a shared passion for Indian heritage and design. The idea took shape in 2016, when Mrs. Gargi Paul, a talented designer with a keen eye for Indian textiles, teamed up with Mr. Biman Paul to create a boutique that celebrates handloom and handcrafted fashion. What began as a small initiative has now grown into Dheu Retail Private Limited, a recognized and respected name in the ethnic fashion landscape.

Over the years, Dheu has not only expanded its product range but also its footprint—establishing itself both offline and online. Recognized under the Government of India’s Start-Up India program and certified with ISO 9001:2015, the brand is also MSME registered and holds a Trademark Registration, adding further credibility to its growing success.

Dheu continues to evolve while staying rooted in its mission—to promote the richness of Indian craft through fashion that is thoughtful, inclusive, and timeless.
