Established in 1977 under the Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (DoNER), NEHHDC was created to nurture and promote the traditional crafts of Northeast India. Representing the eight Northeastern states—Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, and Tripura—NEHHDC sources and retails authentic handicrafts and handlooms through its network of Purbashree Emporia in cities like Shillong, Guwahati, Kolkata, New Delhi, and Bangalore. It also participates in national and international exhibitions, expanding market access for local artisans. Over the decades, NEHHDC has also played a pivotal role in artisan development through skill training, design innovation, and technical support.
