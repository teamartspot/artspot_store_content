Chikri Wood Crafts is a generic label for Rajouri district’s artisans making carved items from the soft Chikri wood. Public profiles highlight its uniqueness and recent GI recognition. Chikri Wood Crafts is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
