The journey of Shikha’s Fab began in 2003, when Shikha Kaushal, a university gold medalist in Textile Design from Dayalbagh University, Agra, envisioned a brand rooted in tradition but tailored for the modern consumer. Her love for handloom textiles and passion for preserving India’s cultural heritage led her to establish a unit in Hyderabad dedicated to the art of block printing.

In 2015, Shubham Agrawal, a dynamic entrepreneur from a traditional Marwari family in Varanasi, joined the venture as a business partner. Bringing with him sharp business acumen and energy, he became instrumental in scaling operations and expanding the brand’s reach.

A major turning point came in February 2023 with the opening of Shikha’s Fab's first retail showroom in Kondapur. The showroom offers a curated selection of sarees, fabrics, and dress materials, along with customized stitching services. Today, Shikha’s Fab continues to thrive as a hub of heritage craft, sustainable fashion, and community engagement, weaving the essence of Indian art into every thread.
