Naqash Carpets Kashmir’s Naqash traditions—chainstitch and crewel embroidery—have long roots in Srinagar. Firms such as Naqash evolved to provide both fabric panels and home textiles, building an export and retail presence that keeps Kashmiri surface-embroidery techniques visible to modern interiors markets. Naqash Carpets is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
