Rooted in the textile-rich culture of Gujarat, Mina Bandhej began its journey with the mission to revive and promote Bandhani — a traditional tie-and-dye technique known for its intricate patterns and vibrant colors. Over the years, the brand has worked closely with skilled artisans and weavers, ensuring that traditional knowledge is preserved and celebrated in each creation. With decades of experience, Mina Bandhej continues to reinvent ethnic fashion, offering garments that bridge the gap between the past and the present, while staying deeply connected to Indian roots.
