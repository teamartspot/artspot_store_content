Thirukkanur Papier-Mâché Artisans Papier‑mâché in Thirukkanur traces its origins to cultural exchanges in the region and developed as a cottage industry catering to colonial and later tourist markets. Artisans refined painting styles and lacquer finishes over decades, combining local iconography with applied decorative traditions. Today the craft is sustained through local cooperatives and periodic craft fairs that showcase Thirukkanur work.
