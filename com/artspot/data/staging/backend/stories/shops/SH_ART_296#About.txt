Venee Art Sculptures Venee Art Sculptures is a Mamallapuram (Mahabalipuram) sculptors’ workshop and showroom that makes stone and sand-sculpted figures for temple, garden and decorative markets. The studio supplies local tourists and large-scale architectural commissions and is listed on local trade directories with showroom addresses. Venee Art Sculptures is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
