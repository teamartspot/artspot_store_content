The Cottons Daily began with a simple yet powerful vision: to create clothing that embodies everyday comfort while honouring India’s artisanal legacy. Founded in 2017 by a woman entrepreneur passionate about sustainable fashion, the brand was born from a love of natural fabrics and a desire to bring ethical, breathable cotton wear to the forefront of contemporary fashion.

From its early days, the brand committed itself to handpicked collections featuring traditional techniques like hand block printing, artisanal weaving, and regional dyeing processes. This commitment quickly gained attention, earning accolades such as Ecommerce Company of the Year (2021) by Business Goa and Women Entrepreneur of the Year by Incredible Goa — recognitions that fuel our ongoing mission.

Today, The Cottons Daily is more than a clothing label — it’s a movement to celebrate conscious fashion, empower women, and uplift artisan communities. Every stitch is a tribute to tradition, every piece a step towards a more mindful wardrobe.
