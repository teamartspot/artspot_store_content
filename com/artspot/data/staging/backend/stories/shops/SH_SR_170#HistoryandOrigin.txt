Foundational Legacy (1975): Established on January 13, 1975, Ananda Textiles began as a private limited textile manufacturing company in Karnataka, registered under MCA as Ananda Textiles Pvt Ltd.

Expansion into Retail: Evolving under the S.A. Aanandan Mill Group, the brand extended its reach into brick-and-mortar markets in Chittoor (Church Street), offering an array of textiles and garments through its two multi-story retail outlets—Ananda Textiles & Readymades and Ananda Lifestyle—spanning over 11,000 sq. ft.

Ongoing Commitment: With nearly five decades of operational continuity, Ananda Textiles has upheld its legacy in textile manufacturing and retail, blending its deep-rooted industry experience with contemporary customer service and convenience.
