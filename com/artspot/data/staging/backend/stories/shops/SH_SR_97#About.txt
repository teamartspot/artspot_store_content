Founded in 1986, Gadwal Weavers Society is a cooperative deeply committed to preserving the traditional Gadwal handloom saree craft—an elegant fusion of lightweight cotton and silk renowned for its fine zari work. 

Operating from Hyderabad, the society brings together skilled weavers, offering authentic Gadwal weaves—including pure silk, silk-cotton blends, and cotton variants—at competitive prices below market rates . Each piece is woven with passion and precision, preserving the legacy of one of Telangana's most elegant saree traditions. 

The society champions sustainability in handlooms under the motto: "Wear Handloom | Save Weavers."
