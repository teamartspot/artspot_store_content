Bihar Mangoes began in 2014 with a small delivery of just 20 kilograms of mangoes from the fertile orchards of Bhagalpur, Bihar. Founded with the goal of bringing authentic Bihar-grown fruits directly to consumers, the venture gradually grew into a trusted brand serving more than 10,000 customers across India. The company is known for naturally ripened, carbide-free mangoes and celebrated regional varieties such as Dudhiya Malda, Langra, Bambai, Himsagar, and GI-tagged Jardalu. Alongside mangoes, it also promotes traditional Bihar products, supporting local farmers and preserving the state's rich agricultural heritage. Their journey reflects a commitment to authenticity, sustainability, and sharing the true taste of Bihar with a wider audience.
