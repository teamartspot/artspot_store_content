Tapas Jana is a celebrated Madur (matt) weaver from Sabang, West Bengal, known for exceptionally fine Mataranch and madur weaving. He and his cooperative produce handwoven mats using vegetable dyes and fine-thread techniques, and their products are used domestically and exported to niche markets. Tapas is an award-winning craftsman whose work supports local women’s self‑help groups and sustainable dyeing practices.
