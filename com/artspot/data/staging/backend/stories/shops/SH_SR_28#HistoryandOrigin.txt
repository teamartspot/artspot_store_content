The origins of Prashanti Sarees lie in a passionate effort to revive and promote authentic South Indian handloom traditions. Starting as a humble textile initiative under the larger umbrella of the Padma eRetail Collective, the brand has grown steadily under the leadership of Ms. Brindha Padmanaabhan, who envisioned a space where traditional weaves could be both cherished and accessible.

Backed by a legacy of over two decades in silk and silk-cotton sarees, the brand has established direct relationships with skilled weavers and artisans. Every PadmaVastra saree is a testament to time-honored techniques passed through generations—from looms in Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh to homes across the globe.

Today, Prashanti Sarees stands as a symbol of authenticity, elegance, and ethical craftsmanship, committed to preserving heritage weaves for the modern woman—one saree at a time.
