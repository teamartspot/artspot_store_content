Murshidabad Saree Kuthir was born in the historic textile region of Murshidabad, West Bengal, a land known for its heritage silk weaving and artistic finesse. Founded in 2017 under the visionary leadership of Mr. Animesh Bardhan, the brand set out to revive and promote regional weaving traditions through a contemporary lens.

Combining artisanal techniques with modern production capabilities, Murshidabad Saree Kuthir quickly carved a niche in both domestic and international markets. The brand champions the legacy of Murshidabad textiles by empowering local weavers, ensuring fair trade practices, and delivering impeccable quality.

From humble beginnings to becoming a respected name in handcrafted fashion, Murshidabad Saree Kuthir continues its mission to make traditional Indian sarees globally cherished, stitch by stitch.
