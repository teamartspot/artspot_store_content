The journey of Aura Benaras began in the heart of Varanasi, where tradition, devotion, and craftsmanship come together in harmony. Founded on April 10th, 2019, Aura Benaras was born out of a desire to revive and celebrate the handloom heritage that defines this ancient city.

Varanasi, known as the spiritual soul of India, has long been a center for artistic expression—especially in the realm of brocade and silk weaving. Drawing from this deep-rooted legacy, Aura Benaras set out to bring the elegance of handcrafted textiles into modern wardrobes, blending cultural richness with contemporary taste.

What started as a humble initiative quickly evolved into a movement—one driven by passion, creativity, and an unwavering respect for the artisans behind each thread. Today, Aura Benaras stands as a tribute to Varanasi’s weaving legacy, aiming to carry its essence across generations and around the globe.
