Inspired by the festive warmth of Bhogali Bihu, Bhogali Jalpan has carried forward the rich culinary soul of Assam since 1997. What began as a humble cottage effort in Guwahati has grown into a trusted name preserving the flavors, traditions, and spirit of Assamese kitchens for over two decades.
Rooted in local sourcing and time-honoured recipes, every product from traditional snacks to everyday staples is crafted with care, purity, and a deep respect for heritage. Blending authentic methods with modern processes, Bhogali Jalpan ensures that each offering remains natural, wholesome, and true to its origin.
More than a food brand, Bhogali Jalpan is a celebration of Assam’s living traditions, bringing the taste of home, the joy of festivals, and the comfort of honest food to families across India and beyond.
