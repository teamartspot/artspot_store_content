Rameshwar Singh Wooden Toys The Varanasi wooden toy tradition grew from household workshops and temple-town demand; families such as the Singhs passed carving skills across generations. Craftspeople like Rameshwar Singh feature in local reports and short documentaries about wooden toy makers, recording multi-decade practice and the challenges of market access and materials sourcing. Rameshwar Singh Wooden Toys is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
