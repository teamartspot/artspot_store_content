The roots of KashmKari trace back nearly 150 years, when our great-grandfather, Peerzada Saif-Ud-Din, along with his fellow master artisans, wove the first piece of Kashmiri textile art. Since then, each generation of our family has carried forward this legacy with passion and pride.

Today, brothers Hamees and Noumaan represent the fourth generation, continuing to craft, preserve, and celebrate this ancient art form. What began in the valleys of Kashmir now reaches homes across the world — each piece a testament to tradition, skill, and soulful expression.
