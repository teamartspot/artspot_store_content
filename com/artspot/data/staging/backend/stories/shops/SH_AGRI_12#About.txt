Since 1932, Desai Bandhu Ambewale has been a name synonymous with trust, tradition, and the true taste of Maharashtra’s finest mangoes. Rooted in family values and nurtured across generations, our journey spans over nine decades of careful cultivation along the Konkan coast.


Across our 400-acre GI-certified orchards in Ratnagiri, Devgad, and Pawas, mangoes are grown with patience and respect for nature—using sustainable, chemical-free practices and natural ripening methods. Handpicked at peak perfection and delivered farm-to-table, every fruit reflects authenticity, provenance, and quiet indulgence.


Carried forward today by Vasantrao Desai, the legacy continues with the same promise—to offer mangoes as they were always meant to be: pure, seasonal, and unmistakably special.
