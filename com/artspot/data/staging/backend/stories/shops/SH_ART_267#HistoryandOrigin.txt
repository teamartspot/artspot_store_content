Patola Of Patan Pvt Ltd Patan Patola weaving is an ancient double-ikat technique associated with Gujarat's Patan region. Patola Of Patan builds on a centuries-old tradition where warp and weft are resist-dyed in specific patterns before weaving to create the iconic geometric and floral motifs. The craft has been preserved by families such as the Salvis and Patolawalas, and modern organisations help market and certify authentic Patola weaves to keep the technique alive.
