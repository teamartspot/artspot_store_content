Gajam Govardhana is a Padma Shri–awarded master weaver from Puttapaka known for Telia Rumal (double ikat) textiles; his gallery in Hyderabad exhibits Telia Rumal and related ikat handloom works. The gallery presents the family’s weaving legacy, samples of double‑ikat motifs and educational material on the Telia Rumal tradition. It functions as both a retail space and a knowledge centre that promotes Puttapaka weaving to designers, scholars and collectors. The gallery supports the weavers’ network by showcasing and selling authenticated handloom pieces.
