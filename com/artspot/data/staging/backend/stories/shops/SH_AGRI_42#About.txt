What began as a childhood ritual in the vibrant streets of Delhi soon transformed into a lifelong passion. Watching the neighborhood paan artisan carefully blend choona and katha, fold a betel leaf, and craft a perfect cone of flavors felt less like a transaction and more like witnessing an art form. That early fascination stayed with Ankit long after he moved across the world.
Years later, in New York, longing for the authentic taste he grew up with, he began recreating that very craft, sourcing the finest ingredients from India and refining recipes in his own kitchen. What started as a personal pursuit slowly evolved into a shared passion, supported by family and shaped by a vision to present paan not just as a tradition, but as a refined cultural experience.
Founded in 2015, The Paanwaala brings together heritage, craftsmanship, and elegant presentation, transforming the humble betel leaf into a sophisticated indulgence. Each paan is a tribute to tradition, carefully crafted and beautifully presented, inviting you into a world where flavor, culture, and artistry come together.
