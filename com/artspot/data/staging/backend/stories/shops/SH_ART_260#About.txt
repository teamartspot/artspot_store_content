Thanjavur Veenai Works Thanjavur Veenai Works is a specialised shop in Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, producing the Saraswati veena, the classical string instrument of South India. The workshop employs skilled luthiers who hand‑carve veenas from jackwood, ensuring tonal quality and aesthetic excellence. Its instruments are sought after by classical musicians, music schools, and cultural organisations across India. The shop is regarded as one of the authentic sources for Thanjavur veenas.
