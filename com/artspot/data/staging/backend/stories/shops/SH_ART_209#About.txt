Bhanodaya Kalamkari is a Srikalahasti-based Kalamkari producer specialising in hand-painted pen-kalamkari and block-printed designs on sarees and fabric panels. The studio takes custom commissions and wholesale orders, and is active on local business directories and social platforms for visibility. Bhanodaya concentrates on traditional motifs from Ramayana and Mahabharata narratives, producing detailed storytelling panels and wearable art. The studio supports local artisan groups, often working with self-help groups and cooperative networks for production.
