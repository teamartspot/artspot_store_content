For nearly five decades, Heerson has been a cherished name in Mumbai’s world of sweets and dry fruits, where timeless recipes meet modern indulgence. Rooted in tradition, the brand carries forward the art of crafting authentic Indian mithai using methods passed down through generations, preserving the flavors that have delighted families for years.
Alongside these classic sweets, Heerson curates an exquisite selection of premium dry fruits, savory namkeens, mukhwas, and khakhras, each chosen and prepared with exceptional care and quality. While honoring its heritage, the brand continues to reimagine traditional treats with contemporary flair, creating experiences that feel both nostalgic and new.
At Heerson, every offering is a celebration of craftsmanship, flavor, and refinement, bringing together the richness of Indian tradition with the elegance of modern taste.
