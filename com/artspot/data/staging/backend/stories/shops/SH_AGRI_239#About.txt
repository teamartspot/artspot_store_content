In the fertile plains of Punjab, where the fragrance of basmati defines tradition, DRRK Foods began its journey in 1967 as a small venture driven by dedication and vision. Founded by Mr. Mahinder Pal, it slowly transformed from modest beginnings into one of India’s leading rice brands, built on patience and relentless effort. What makes its story unique is its evolution embracing modern technology, global expansion, and advanced processing while preserving the authentic taste and aroma of basmati rice. From local fields to kitchens in over 30 countries, each grain carries a legacy shaped by generations of trust and craftsmanship. Every step, from cultivation to packaging, reflects a commitment to quality and consistency that defines its identity. It is not just about rice, but about a heritage that continues to grow with time. In every serving, DRRK Foods offers more than flavor it offers a story of tradition refined through progress.
