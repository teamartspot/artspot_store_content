Gagan Leather House Founded as a family-owned leather business in Indore, Gagan Leather House developed through local retail trade and gradually added manufacturing and customized orders. Public business listings and trade profiles show the firm operating in the Khajrana/New Palasia area, reflecting Mirzapur/Indore leather-cluster connections and decades of leathercraft knowledge passed between makers. Gagan Leather House is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
