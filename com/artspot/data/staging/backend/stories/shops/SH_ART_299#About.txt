Pokaran Pottery Pokaran Pottery refers to the pottery tradition around Pokaran (Rajasthan), known for resilient, often pink-hued clay wares and local functional pottery. Contemporary potters and small shops in Pokaran sell a variety of storage and decorative items that draw from Rajasthan’s rustic aesthetics. Pokaran Pottery is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
