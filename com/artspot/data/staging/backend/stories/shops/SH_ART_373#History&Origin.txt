Jot Bandhej House bandhej or bandhani is a textile resist-dye craft with deep roots in Rajasthan and Gujarat, traditionally worn in festive attire. Jot Bandhej House is recognized for its role in regional craft traditions, sustaining artisan skills and heritage practices.
