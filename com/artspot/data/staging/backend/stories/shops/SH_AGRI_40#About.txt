Rooted in India’s timeless paan tradition and reimagined for the modern world, The Betel Leaf Co. brings a fresh perspective to one of the country’s most cherished after-meal indulgences. Founded by Prem and Payal Raheja, the brand blends heritage with innovation, transforming the classic paan into a refined, contemporary dessert experience.
Crafted with signature recipes and premium ingredients, every paan is prepared contact-free and sealed to preserve its freshness, aroma, and flavor. This thoughtful approach ensures that each bite captures the authentic taste of tradition while meeting the standards of today’s lifestyle.
From festive tables to everyday moments, The Betel Leaf Co. invites you to rediscover paan, elegant, hygienic, and delightfully modern.
