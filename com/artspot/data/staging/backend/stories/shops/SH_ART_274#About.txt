Jagdish Lal Raj Soni Jagdish Lal Raj Soni is a master craftsman from Pratapgarh, Rajasthan, renowned for the Thewa jewellery technique—enamel painting on gold set over intricately carved frames. His oeuvre includes pendants, framed panels and decorative jewellery that combine miniature painting with filigree metalwork. He is recognized among Thewa practitioners and has showcased at national craft platforms. Jagdish Lal Raj Soni is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
