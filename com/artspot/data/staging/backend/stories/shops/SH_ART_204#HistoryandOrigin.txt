New Kashmir Zari evolved from small family-run embroidery workshops in Maharajganj and expanded into a retail presence catering to Srinagar’s local market. Its operations reflect the classic Kashmiri artisan-retailer model—direct collaboration with local needleworkers and dyers. Over the years, New Kashmir Zari has sustained traditional metal-thread embroidery techniques by ensuring a steady stream of bespoke and retail orders.
