In the fertile plains of Assam, where agriculture shapes both livelihood and identity, Trifac Agro Private Limited began its journey as a young yet determined venture in 2023. Built with a vision to connect local produce to wider markets, it focuses on sourcing and supplying staples like turmeric, rice, and cardamom with care and consistency. What makes its story unique is its early ambition starting small but aiming to create a reliable chain between farmers and buyers. Rooted in regional strength, it reflects the richness of Assam’s agricultural produce while adapting to modern trade needs. Each product carries the effort of local cultivation, shaped by soil, climate, and dedication. With a growing foundation and a clear purpose, the company continues to build its path step by step. In every supply, Trifac Agro tells a story of new beginnings grounded in tradition and trust.
