Manjal, M.Rm.Rm. Cultural Foundation Manjal, operated under the M.Rm.Rm. Cultural Foundation, is a cultural shop and initiative in Tamil Nadu that promotes Chettinad heritage crafts and traditions. It offers traditional products such as Athangudi tiles, hand‑woven sarees, Chettinad jewellery, and household artefacts. The shop not only serves as a retail outlet but also as an awareness centre highlighting the region’s cultural heritage and craftsmanship. It collaborates with artisans to ensure fair trade and sustainability.
