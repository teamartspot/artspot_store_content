Spoil Me Silly began as a dream in the heart of a young girl growing up in 1970s India—a time when creating your own fashion was an adventure. Trips to Delhi’s bustling fabric markets, selecting trims with care, working with local tailors and embroiderers—these were treasured experiences for our founder, Sonali, that shaped her creative spirit.

Rooted in these memories, Spoil Me Silly was born. It’s a tribute to the hand-involved process of creation—to the joy of dreaming up a design, sourcing the right fabric, watching it come to life, and cherishing the final result. It’s a continuation of those days of stitched stories and personalised pieces, reimagined for today’s discerning, mindful customer.

While we began small, our vision was always expansive: to preserve traditional Indian crafts and pair them with elegant, accessible design—blending nostalgia with fresh perspective. Today, Spoil Me Silly stands as a bridge between past and present, between craftsmanship and individual expression.
