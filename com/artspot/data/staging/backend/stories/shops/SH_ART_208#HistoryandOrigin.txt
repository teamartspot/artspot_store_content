Usha Kalamkari grew from a family-run artisanal practice in Srikalahasti, a historic centre for pen-kalamkari. Over decades the studio adapted practices to modern markets, maintained natural-dye protocols and trained successive generations in the craft. As Srikalahasti’s profile increased (including GI recognition), studios like Usha Kalamkari helped formalise sales channels and preserve pen-kalamkari techniques for wider audiences.
