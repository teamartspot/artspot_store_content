In the cool, fertile slopes of Mahabaleshwar, where strawberries first found their Indian identity, a journey began over five decades ago not just to grow, but to transform how berries are cultivated. At SB Agritech, experience is not measured in years alone, but in the evolution of every harvest shaped through changing climates and continuous innovation.
From strawberries to blueberries, raspberries, and rare global varieties, each crop is introduced, nurtured, and adapted with precision to Indian soil. But the story does not remain within the farm; it extends to farmers across regions, who are guided, trained, and supported to grow these high-value crops successfully. With approved growing facilities and deep technical expertise, every plant reflects both science and stewardship. What begins as cultivation becomes opportunity, creating prosperity not just for one farm, but for an entire community. From supplying fresh berries to leading food industries to introducing new crops for future markets, the journey is both rooted and forward-looking. Here, farming is not routine — it is reinvention guided by knowledge and trust. Because true growth is not just in what you harvest, but in how many lives you help grow along the way.
