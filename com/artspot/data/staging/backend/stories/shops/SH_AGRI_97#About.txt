Born in Bangalore, Genetics Coffee began as a quiet curiosity, an exploration into what a coffee bean can truly become.
Sourced from traceable, sustainably grown estates, each coffee carries the story of its origin, its soil, its process, and its character carefully understood and respected.
Through slow, small-batch roasting, every profile is meticulously developed to reveal balance, depth, and complexity, never rushed, never compromised.
This is not just coffee.
It is a study in flavour, crafted for those who seek to experience it.
