Hastkala is an ethical apparel and textile label that collaborates with handloom weavers and traditional painters to produce sarees, stoles and apparel inspired by Indian art forms. The brand sources silk and woven fabrics—often from Bhagalpur and nearby weaving clusters—and applies hand-painted and block-printed motifs to create contemporary yet craft-rooted garments. Hastkala works with artisan groups to maintain fair trade practices and often lists its collections online for pan‑India customers. Their product range highlights regional painting styles (Madhubani, Jhaar art) adapted to wearable formats. Hastkala also provides design inputs that help artisans translate traditional motifs to contemporary apparel markets.
