Mirchi Seth Spices Bhiwapur was established with the aim of bringing the authentic taste of Bhiwapur’s renowned red chilies to consumers through pure and traditional spice products. Rooted in a region celebrated for its chilli heritage, the business focuses on producing original Saoji red chilli powder along with turmeric and coriander powders without artificial colors or preservatives. With FSSAI licensing and recognition through Khadi registration, the company emphasizes purity, authenticity, and trusted quality. Its journey reflects a commitment to preserving regional spice traditions while delivering genuine flavors from Maharashtra’s chilli heartland to a wider market.
