Founded five years ago, Surati Fabric started with a simple yet powerful idea — combining the rich textile legacy of Surat, India’s textile capital, with modern e-commerce. The name Texora is inspired by a fusion of "Textile" and "Aura", symbolizing the vibrant energy and craftsmanship rooted in our offerings.

Having played key roles in the textile trade for years, we knew the pulse of the market — and saw the opportunity to bring Surat’s finest fabrics directly to people’s homes, without the middlemen. We envisioned a name that not only reflects our origin but also our purpose: to become one of India’s most trusted and memorable fashion platforms for quality textiles.
