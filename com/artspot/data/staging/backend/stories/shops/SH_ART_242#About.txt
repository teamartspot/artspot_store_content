Villianur Terracotta Works Villianur Terracotta Works—protected under a Geographical Indication—are created by artisans in Villianur and surrounding areas using a locally sourced fine clay. The product range includes utilitarian wares, devotional icons and large sculptural pieces often finished with natural slips and low‑temperature firing. Producers today balance functional wares with decorative pieces for tourists and export markets, and many studios accept custom commissions for public installations. Community artisans continue to teach younger generations in village workshops, sustaining the craft’s continuity.
