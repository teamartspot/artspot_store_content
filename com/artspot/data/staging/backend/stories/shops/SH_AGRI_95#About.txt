In a quiet corner of Bengaluru, where craft meets curiosity, GB Roasters was born not in a factory but in a garage, where passion slowly transformed into precision. At its heart is a relentless pursuit of excellence, led by a Q-Grader whose journey spans continents, cultures, and the science of coffee itself.
Here, coffee is not produced; it is studied, refined, and reimagined. Each bean is sourced with intention, roasted in small, thoughtful batches, and shaped through meticulous cupping and profiling to reveal its truest character.
From global origins to local cups, GB Roasters bridges distance with transparency, sharing the story behind every bean, every process, and every flavour note. It is a space where craftsmanship meets education, and every cup becomes an experience of discovery.
GB Roasters is more than coffee it is a quiet mastery of the bean, where knowledge, precision, and passion come together to create a truly elevated cup.
