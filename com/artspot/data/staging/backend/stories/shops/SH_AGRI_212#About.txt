In the bustling lanes of Kolhapur, where tradition and enterprise quietly coexist, Prestine Naturals began its journey as a small yet determined venture with a big vision. Established in 2025 under the guidance of Mr. Dattatray Patil, it set out to create natural, health-focused products that blend everyday nutrition with purity. From jaggery cubes and herbal teas to moringa powder and chyawanprash, each offering reflects a commitment to simple, chemical-free living. What makes this story unique is its humble scale built by a close-knit team, yet reaching customers across the world with care and consistency. Every product carries the intention of making wellness accessible, without losing the authenticity of traditional ingredients. It is a journey shaped not by speed, but by steady growth and trust. With each step forward, Prestine Naturals proves that even the smallest beginnings can create a meaningful impact.
