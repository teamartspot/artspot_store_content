In the mist-covered plantations of Idukki, where the world’s finest cardamom finds its home, Idukki Spices & Co. traces its roots back over a century.
What began in the early 1900s as a family’s devotion to the land has evolved into a legacy of craftsmanship, carried forward through generations.
At its heart lies a singular pursuit: perfecting cardamom. Each pod is cultivated with care and graded to exceptional standards featuring bold, 8mm+ capsules that embody richness, aroma, and rarity.
Untouched by additives and guided by time-honoured methods, every harvest preserves the pure essence of Idukki.
This is more than spice, it is a lineage of flavour, refined across generations.
