Saklain's Coterie Shops like Saklain’s Coterie have grown from local merchant traditions in Srinagar’s Polo View market, diversifying from local spice and dry-fruit trading into curated organic cosmetics and gift goods. Travel reviews and local listings highlight the store’s approachable scale and its role in championing Kashmiri natural products. Saklain's Coterie is recognized as part of regional artisan heritage and contributes to sustaining traditional skills.
