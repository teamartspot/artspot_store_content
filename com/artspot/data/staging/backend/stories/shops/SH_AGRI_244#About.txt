In the fertile fields of Haryana, where basmati rice carries both aroma and legacy, Sulson Overseas began as a journey shaped by courage and conviction. Founded by Rajesh Kumar, who left behind a rising corporate career to follow his roots, the company was built with a dual purpose to deliver world-class rice and to ensure farmers receive the value they deserve. What makes its story unique is this balance of heart and enterprise, where every grain reflects both quality and fairness. Blending traditional processing methods with modern techniques, each batch is crafted to preserve its natural fragrance and taste. From local fields to more than 50 countries, its journey carries the essence of Indian basmati across borders. It is not just about exporting rice, but about honoring the hands that grow it. In every serving, Sulson tells a story of passion, purpose, and a deep connection to the land.
