Founded in the Eastern hills of the Himalayas, Meghloom’s story is deeply tied to the cultural and ecological heritage of the Khasi community. Eri silk—locally reared and woven—has been a part of village life in Meghalaya for generations. Women in these communities have long nurtured this tradition, preserving natural dyeing and hand spinning techniques. Meghloom continues this legacy, blending age-old knowledge with modern design, ensuring the survival of this timeless craft while creating economic opportunities for its artisans.
