In the historic chilli markets of Byadagi, Karnataka, where generations of farmers cultivate India’s most vibrant red chillies, Dilip & Company has been a trusted name since 1995. Founded by Mr. Rehmatulla I. Mattikotti, fondly known as Dilip, the company grew from a vision rooted in integrity, expertise, and a deep respect for the spice trade.
Positioned at the heart of one of India’s most renowned chilli-growing regions, Dilip & Company works closely with local farmers to source chillies celebrated for their rich colour, balanced heat, and exceptional quality. Every batch reflects the heritage of Byadagi’s fields and the careful selection that defines the brand.
For decades, Dilip & Company has carried forward a tradition of reliability and craftsmanship delivering the authentic character of India’s red chillies to markets with trust, consistency, and quiet distinction.
