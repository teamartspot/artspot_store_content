Founded in 2022 in Pune, Ratnagiri Hapus Store was born from a simple promise—to bring the true taste of the Konkan coast to discerning mango lovers. Sourced from the sun-soaked orchards of Ratnagiri and the celebrated Devgad region of Sindhudurg, their Alphonso mangoes carry a legacy shaped by land, climate, and generations of devoted farmers.


Hand-selected from growers with decades of mango-growing heritage, each Ratnagiri Hapus is naturally sweet, richly pulped, and unmistakably authentic. Seasonal, refined, and carefully delivered, this is not just a mango—it is Konkan’s finest tradition, presented with quiet elegance.
