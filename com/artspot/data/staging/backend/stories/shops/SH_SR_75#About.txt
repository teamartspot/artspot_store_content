At Sohum Sutras, we believe in celebrating the grace and grandeur of Indian handwoven sarees. Our brand is a homage to tradition, craftsmanship, and the enduring charm of pure, handcrafted textiles. We collaborate directly with skilled weavers across India to curate a thoughtfully selected collection that showcases the richness of natural fibres and the beauty of regional artistry.

Created for the modern Indian woman who values authenticity and elegance, our sarees reflect a unique blend of tradition and contemporary sensibility. Each piece is a testament to diverse weaving legacies — curated with love, designed to inspire, and crafted to stand the test of time.
