The foundation traces its roots to the centuries-old Sujani embroidery tradition of Bihar, where women stitched symbolic motifs onto old fabric as a form of storytelling and expression. Revived in the late 20th century as a means of women’s empowerment, this art form became central to community-based organizations like Sujanimahila Jeevan Foundation. By carrying forward this legacy, the foundation ensures the continuity of Bihar’s rich textile heritage while supporting local artisans.
