Bhadohi Carpets House Operating since the late 20th century, Bhadohi Carpets House grew alongside the Bhadohi district’s emergence as India’s major carpet hub. The firm institutionalised traditional weaving practices into scalable production suitable for export markets, investing in quality control and design development. As Bhadohi gained recognition (including GI status for carpets), firms such as Bhadohi Carpets House helped channel artisan production into branded offerings.
