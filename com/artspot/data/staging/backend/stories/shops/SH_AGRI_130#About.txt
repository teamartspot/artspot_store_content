Some enterprises are built over years. Others are shaped across landscapes.
For over nine decades, Woodbriar Group has grown not just tea but an entire ecosystem stretching across the highlands of South India, where plantations unfold over thousands of acres and generations of expertise take root.
Within these estates, tea is not sourced; it is lived. From cultivation to processing, every stage unfolds within the same terrain, allowing each leaf to retain the clarity of its origin. This continuity has defined the group’s identity as a custodian of single-origin teas, crafted with depth, consistency, and quiet precision.
Beyond tea, the land yields more coffee, cardamom, and pepper, each cultivated within the same disciplined framework that values scale without losing character.
With a workforce that spans thousands and an annual production that reaches millions of kilograms, the operation moves with both magnitude and method, earning recognition such as the Golden Leaf Awards along the way.
What defines Woodbriar is not just its reach, but its continuity, a legacy where the land, the leaf, and the people have grown together over time.
