Anokhi Anokhi is a Jaipur‑based heritage brand founded by John and Faith Singh in 1970, renowned for reviving Rajasthani hand‑block printing and vegetable dyes. The brand curates a wide range of handcrafted textiles, home furnishings and lifestyle products produced in collaboration with regional artisans. Anokhi operates retail stores, workshops and a training centre, and invests in artisan welfare and craft documentation projects. The company blends traditional motifs with contemporary design, positioning itself as a leader in ethical craft retail and design‑led conservation.
