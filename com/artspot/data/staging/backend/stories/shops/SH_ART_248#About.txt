Khatamband Ceiling Kashmir Khatamband ceiling artisans in Kashmir create ornate wooden ceilings (khatamband) using intricate interlocking timber patterns without nails, often seen in traditional Kashmiri architecture. These ceiling panels are handcrafted from seasoned walnut or deodar wood and feature geometrical motifs that complement mosque and palace interiors. Specialist workshops produce panels, domes and decorative cornices on commission for restoration and new construction projects. The craft is valued for precision joinery and ornamental geometry, combining aesthetic and structural functions in interior design.
