For over 15 years, Palam Silks has been redefining how women drape tradition with grace and style. Specializing in Kanchipuram silk sarees, Palam blends the richness of heritage with contemporary design. Whether you're embracing a festive moment or making a subtle fashion statement, every saree from Palam captures elegance in its purest form.

With three exclusive showrooms across Chennai—Pondy Bazaar, Luz Church Road, and Anna Nagar—Palam offers a curated experience steeped in tradition. Personally curated by Mrs. Jeyasree Ravi, daughter of the legendary silk merchant Dr. Nalli Kuppuswamy Chetty, every collection is a tribute to quality, craftsmanship, and culture.

The showrooms are designed to allow customers to browse at leisure, offering personal attention and a tranquil atmosphere to choose from Palam’s vast repertoire of traditional and fashionable silks.
