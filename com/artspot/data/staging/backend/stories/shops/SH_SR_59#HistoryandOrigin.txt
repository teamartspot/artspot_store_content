Founded in the cultural heartland of Yeola, the traditional hub for Paithani weaving, Om Paithani has grown to become the region’s largest manufacturer and retailer of handcrafted Paithani sarees. With deep roots in Maharashtra’s weaving legacy and decades of experience, our brand emerged from a passion to preserve and promote the rich artistry of silk.

From a humble start to becoming a sought-after name across Nashik and beyond, our journey has been built on dedication, perseverance, and a constant pulse on evolving fashion trends. Using advanced weaving techniques while honoring traditional methods, we ensure every saree we produce speaks of authenticity, purity, and pride.
