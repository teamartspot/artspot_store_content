When the world thinks of Basmati, it thinks of India and of India Gate.
Rooted in a legacy of excellence and backed by the strength of KRBL, India Gate has become a symbol of purity, provenance, and perfection in every grain. Carefully nurtured through one of the world’s largest farmer networks and aged for two full years, each harvest is crafted to deliver signature aroma, graceful length, and a delicate, fluffy texture that elevates every dish.
Blending agricultural heritage with state-of-the-art milling and global standards, India Gate brings together tradition and innovation in seamless harmony. From classic Basmati to thoughtfully curated health grains, every offering reflects a commitment to quality, sustainability, and the communities that make it possible.
India Gate is not just rice. It is the quiet luxury of authenticity grown with care, aged with patience, and served with pride across tables around the world.
