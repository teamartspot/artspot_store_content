Studio Sui Dhaga, founded in 2018 by Anuja Jain, was born out of a deep love for handcrafted fabrics and the desire to fill the gap in smart, elegant cotton clothing for those who prefer to stand apart with subtle sophistication. What began as a personal passion has evolved into a thoughtful, full-time pursuit of preserving and promoting India’s rich textile heritage. Based in Bhopal, Studio Sui Dhaga offers a curated collection of hand block-printed suits, sarees, blouses, shrugs, jackets, skirts, tops, and fabrics — each piece blending traditional craftsmanship with a contemporary aesthetic. Every product reflects the spirit of slow fashion, celebrating the magic that skilled hands and ancient techniques can create — a uniqueness machines can never replicate.
