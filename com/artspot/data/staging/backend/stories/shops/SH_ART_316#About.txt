Asmangiri is a Karuppur‑based Karuppur Kalamkari practitioner and studio known for hand‑painted Kalamkari using natural dyes and traditional brushes; the venture highlights state awardees and master craftspeople who continue hand‑drawn Kalamkari in the region. Asmangiri sells hand‑painted textiles, conducts workshops and participates in craft fairs, promoting sustainable dyeing and traditional motifs to a wider audience.
