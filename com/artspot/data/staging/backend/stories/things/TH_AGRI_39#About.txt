
Mango Malihabadi Dussehri stands out among fruit varieties, particularly known in regions like Varanasi (Malihari), India. It belongs to the 'Daus'-season category of mangoes, typically ripening from late winter through spring.

This variety presents itself with a deep golden-yellow flesh and possesses stone-fruit-like tenderness, meaning it remains soft yet succulent even days after ripening fully. Its large size is typical for its name, promising generous fruit pieces. The flavor profile offers significant sweetness, but unlike many tropical varieties, it retains subtle spicier notes when truly ripe.

Often described simply as *Aam* (mango), Malihabadi distinguishes itself with its characteristic pinkish hue, setting it apart from the common bright yellow or green mangoes typically sold during other seasons. This richer color suggests a deeper flavor and greater maturity compared to early-season cultivars like Langra or Etibari.

In culinary use, Malihabadi Dussehri offers incredible scope once ripe – perfect for fresh eating where its large, flavorful pieces are ideal for shakes or lassis (Malai Lassi), requiring less waste. It's naturally sweet enough for desserts sans sugar syrup but still finding its way into some recipes when unripe as well.

As a quintessential Indian summer mango variety, Mango Malihabadi Dussehri is celebrated for its substantial size, firm yet yielding texture at peak ripeness, intense sweetness balanced with a hint of spice or redness (perhaps magenta), and supreme taste especially noticeable during the 'Daus-Heri' season.
