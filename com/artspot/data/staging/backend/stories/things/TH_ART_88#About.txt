
Sankhede Furniture stands as a distinguished name in India's vibrant handicraft and wooden furniture industry, proudly originating from the skilled artisan communities of Sankhed village near Surat city. The brand leverages its deep roots in this region, known for centuries as a hub of exceptional craftsmanship.

The hallmark of Sankhede Furniture is meticulous handiwork. Skilled master craftsmen meticulously shape solid woods like rosewood, teak, and mango wood – often sourced locally from Gujarat forests or imported carefully from renowned suppliers across India and abroad – ensuring every piece tells a story of traditional artistry combined with contemporary design sensibilities.

Each unique product boasts superior quality, durability, elegance, and intricate details. The artisans employ classical techniques passed down through generations to sculpt beautiful forms like intricately carved sofa frames, elegant dining tables, sophisticated centerpieces, bespoke beds, custom chairs, and ornate sideboards. Upholstery is typically done locally using carefully selected fabrics.

The brand emphasizes honest value – offering meticulously crafted, high-quality pieces produced sustainably and ethically at its own workshop units in and around Sankhed village by master craftspeople known as 'Kumkars'. The preference for teak furniture sets often highlights good quality, comfortable seating.
