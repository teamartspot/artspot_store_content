
Originating primarily from India, specifically regions like Devanahalli known for excellent pomelo production near Bangalore, Devanahalli Pomello refers to a particular strain or variety of the hybrid citrus fruit commonly called Chinese grapefruit.

This specific type is celebrated for its unique characteristics derived directly from this lineage. Often noted are large fruit sizes and juicy flesh which contrasts pleasantly with its typically very acidic pulp. While generally following the standard pomelo profile – large, segmented, sweet aroma – fruits identified as originating here or bearing the 'Devanahalli Pomello' name often express these traits particularly well.

The Devanahalli Pomello thrives in tropical climates characteristic of parts of Karnataka and other suitable Indian regions where citrus cultivation flourishes. It is valued locally not just for its potential to yield high-quality table fruit, but also historically as a source material or stock used for grafting onto rootstocks that produce smoother-textured sweet oranges (not the same Pomello). This grafting practice helps manage tree vigor and disease.

Growing these pomelos requires specific care. They typically need well-drained soil prepared before planting, patience during their maturation period, regular watering in warmer months, moderate fertilization primarily with nitrogen during growth phases, careful pruning, and pest control to yield a rewarding harvest characterised by its robust nature and distinct flavour profile compared to other hybrids like the Shatavari.
