The narrative begins in the 1950s and 60s, as Tur dal cultivation gained prominence south of Nashik's direct irrigation belt, particularly across the fertile stretches between Nanded and Parbhani. Over generations of selective harvesting under these varied yet geographically close conditions (within what's colloquially 'Navapur region'), certain seeds retained longer dormancy and produced better yield even after secondary processing compared to nearby variants like Kalonchi or Turki.

This inherent resilience and consistent quality gave rise to the name 'Hata Tare' or simply distinguished it as belonging to this area.
