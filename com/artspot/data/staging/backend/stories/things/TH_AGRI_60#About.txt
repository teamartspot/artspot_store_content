
Nagpur offers more than just sunshine; it gifts us its legendary oranges. Also known as Ruby Blizzards or Nagphalindri, these citrus fruits have earned their fame through exceptional quality and unique characteristics.

Their origins trace back to local varieties around the 1920s-30s, where selective breeding within Maharashtra eventually led to this superior cultivar from Nagpur, formally recognized by the Indian Agricultural Research Institute. The most striking feature is often the fruit's appearance itself – sun-kissed oranges frequently develop a beautiful blush on the skin.

While prized for their flavour, mature Nagpur oranges typically display thin, easy-to-peel skin that may even be slightly leathery with fine oil glands (puncts), though the 'Nagpur Super' varieties aim to be juicier and less tough overall. The fruit itself usually doesn't need peeling under ideal circumstances; however, consuming them requires it.

Inside awaits juicy segments bursting forth – sweet flesh that retains a delicate hint of tartness, giving an invigorating burst in each bite. Despite their common name's suggestion otherwise, the typical Indian orange experience involves enjoying the peeled fruit whole. This refreshing citrus burst possesses tremendous natural appeal and is cultivated far beyond Nagpur, reaching parts like Ratnagiri as well.

So whether you fancy a fresh juice squeeze or simply want to experience an unparalleled sweet-tart balance in your fruit bite, the humble Nagpur orange delivers more than just flavour; it gives happiness!
