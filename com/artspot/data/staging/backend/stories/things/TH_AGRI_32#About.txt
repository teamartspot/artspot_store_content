
Malda Khirsapati is an exceptional cultivar of the mango fruit native to India. Originating from the Malda district across West Bengal and Odisha, often referred to by its local name Himsagar (meaning 'Golden' or 'Sweet as Gold'), it has since gained prominence throughout the Indian subcontinent.

This variant is celebrated for its large, oblong fruits with slightly curved sides. Its skin exhibits a distinctive rich golden hue, promising an intense flavour within. Upon maturity, fingers of dark green emerge from beneath dense red blotches and protruding fuzzy markings known as 'khirsapati'. The flesh of Malda Khirsapati turns bright orange/yellow when ripe, offering substantial yields at harvest time.

Comparisons are often made to the popular Dussehri variety due to similarities in taste. It possesses firm flesh that remains juicy even after ripening and detaching from the stone, while retaining a soft texture. Importantly, this mango is notably free from major defects like gummosis or tip burn – significant advantages for farmers in tropical climates.

The flavour profile of Himsagar/Khirsapati is typically rich and buttery, sweet yet refreshing, with a characteristic distinctive taste that sets it apart. It has excellent keeping quality once detached from the tree. Malda's Mango season marks an eagerly awaited period for many.
