
Okay, here's an introduction to Dahanu Ropeway station as the 'Gholvad Chikoo' entry point:

The bustling Mumbai local train network includes stations with interesting names reflecting their location or history. One such notable name is 'Dahanu'. However, arriving at this western suburban stop requires boarding at Gholvad and Argollé in Pune via a unique aerial cable car system – the Dahanu Ropeway.

For many commuters, this ropeway connects two parts of their daily lives. On one hand, it's an essential transport link between Gholvad/Gavan station (the Argollé terminus is near Jatawadi and Sainagar Hills) and the main Dadar West Station. The journey itself offers views over the Sanjay Gandhi Park.

But in the context of everyday local travel colloquialism, 'Gholvad' is often brought up when people buy chikoo from nearby shops on platform level *before* embarking at Dadar. This sweet treats association likely gives it that shorthand name alongside its functional one. The station provides vital access to the Dadar complex for passengers coming overland from Pune and serves as a distinct gateway into this busy transport hub before transferring onto the busy platforms below.
