
Agra Durrie refers specifically to a **vegetable called Dudhi**, known in some contexts as *Asoka gandhak*. This term finds its origin within certain Ayurvedic traditions, particularly associated with the specific variety of burdock plant (*Polygonum persicolor* or similar species) sourced from the region historically known as Agra. The name highlights both the substance's function – providing acidity (gandhak meaning sour power) – and its Sanskrit designation.

Dudhi is valued for its pharmacological effects in Ayurvedic medicine, notably exhibiting anthelmintic properties that can help expel parasitic worms. Beyond this, it possesses anti-inflammatory characteristics beneficial for relieving various inflammatory conditions like arthritis or skin inflammation (vata). This natural substance contributes significantly to digestive health and overall well-being within these traditional systems of herbalism and holistic healthcare.
