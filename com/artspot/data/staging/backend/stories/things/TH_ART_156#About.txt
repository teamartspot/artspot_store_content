
Telia Rumal is not a mobile network service launched by the telecommunications provider Telia Company. Instead, **Rumal** refers to an extremely popular song number in South Indian cinema, particularly Tamil films.

The original "Rumal" song became famous from its appearance in Stanley's film Mahanagar (1975). Its immense popularity was further cemented when it appeared as a background score intro piece in the super hit comedy series Meera Paalan Madhurai Mani Kuttiyilakkaga, replacing the actor introduction that typically accompanied film titles. This catchy tune quickly ingrained itself into Indian music consciousness.

While sometimes people might mistakenly refer to this song by incorrectly combining its name with 'Telia', it's actually a distinct musical piece from cinema history. Its usage as an intro number in TV shows like Meera Paalan made it widely recognizable across India, transcending regional boundaries and cementing its place not merely as background film music but within the broader Indian popular culture soundtrack during that era and beyond through references and covers.
