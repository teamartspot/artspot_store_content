
Ganjam kewda (Candeia odorata) is a renowned edible flower endemic to India, particularly prized for its distinct and potent fragrance. Often called 'Kewra' or the "Queen of Flowers" in certain regions like Odisha's Ganjam district – known as its primary source region, though also found elsewhere – it thrives beneath the canopy of specific trees.

This unique flower offers a significant vanilla-like flavour profile far stronger than jasmine or rose. Its petals dry exceptionally well and are commonly used to flavour sweets, custards, and traditional beverages like Chai, imparting a rich aroma. The strong scent makes Ganjam kewda invaluable for perfumes and aromatic potpourris. Beyond its sensory appeal, it also carries significant medicinal value in Ayurveda.
