
Sikkim Large Cardamom originates from Sikkim's distinctive mountainous region in North-East India, specifically flourished here due to its unique climate and terrain. Also known as 'Melankotta' or Indian Cardamom (distinct from the Zingiberaceae family of regular cardamom), it was integral to local life long before commercial agriculture took hold.

Historically cultivated on misty slopes under dense forest shade, especially during summer months following monsoon rains, this ancient practice connected villagers with forests. The spice wasn't just flavourful but held deep significance in traditional rituals and ceremonies across Sikkim’s communities. Its robust aroma contributed significantly to village cuisine.

Globally recognized for nearly two centuries starting from its collection around the mid-1800s, it gained prominence post-WWII when Indian farmers transitioned cultivation upwards as forests depleted lower regions. This shift created the niche market we know today.

The 'Sikkim Large Cardamom' name itself underscores both its unique regional origin and its larger seed size compared to regular cardamom (though visually similar). Its history is a blend of traditional use for flavouring, medicine in Ayurveda, cultural ceremony, and the modern journey into commercial farming, securing its place as an iconic product deeply tied to Sikkim's past and present.
