
World-famous **Tezpur litchis**, cultivated primarily in the Sonitpur district of Odisha, India, stand apart due to their unique terroir and meticulous farming practices. Harvested seasonally between March and June depending on the cultivar, these litchis are celebrated for exceptional quality – plump fruits wrapped in thick-skinned, vibrant green clusters bursting with juice right at harvest time even when shipped internationally. The most sought-after variety is **Puttaparthi** (also known as Seedless), renowned for its intense sweetness and honey-like taste that melts in your mouth.

The cool hills of Upper Odisha provide ideal conditions – consistent temperatures keep sugars high while maintaining firm flesh, distinguishing Tezpur litchis from common ones often associated with varieties like Alphonse or Black Butte. Their extended shelf life allows fresh produce to reach global markets, including Europe and the USA during April/May/June for this specific harvest.

Tezpur's commitment lies in quality. Picking occurs early for optimal sweetness, aiming to preserve firmness until reaching consumers' plates. This dedication ensures that each litchi delivered promises a sensory experience unique even internationally – juicy sweetness amplified by slightly lower temperatures upon arrival compared to tropical counterparts. The resulting taste profile is incredibly balanced: extra sweet, very less acidic, and perfectly perculated for eating fresh or blending into smoothies.
