
Bhagalpuri Zardalu, as its name suggests (from Bihar's Bhagalpuri region), is a notable apple variety primarily grown locally. Developed through crosses with wild local strains, it boasts distinct characteristics.

Its skin is typically bright red or flushed-red, sometimes extending beyond the bottom halves with an attractive greenish-blue hue underneath. The flesh is yellow to pinkish-yellow and remarkably crisp. The most striking feature is its flavour: exceptionally sweet with minimal background taste, offering a refreshing burst without needing artificial enhancement.

This apple provides good juice yield. Its crisp texture makes it enjoyable both raw and in salads or cooked dishes.
