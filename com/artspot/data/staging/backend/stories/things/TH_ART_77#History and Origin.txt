
Nirmal Toys & Craft's inception traces back to 1974 in Delhi, India. Founded by Dr. Sunita Bhatia, it was one of the pioneering Indian enterprises focusing specifically on children's toy design. Growing up surrounded by colours and crafts herself as an artist, she felt a void – toys reflecting her rich cultural heritage were scarce.

Thus, *Nirmal* (meaning 'pure' or 'clean') was born from this conviction for creating beautiful, authentic playthings relevant to Indian sensibilities. Initially emerging from a small home workshop, the company's philosophy centred on safety and environmental responsibility right from the start. This commitment led them early towards hand-painted fabrics dyed with natural colours like indigo and turmeric, replacing plastic filler often found in cheaper alternatives.

The core of their identity lies not just in manufacturing toys, but in championing traditional Indian art forms. They utilize intricate block printing on cotton fabric throughout much of their range – from dolls fashioned into soft play shapes to puppet kits, activity toys, games like 'Shikaar' (a board game), and even innovative educational tools called Brain Toys. Often designed by women artisans themselves using sketches provided by artists, the patterns are then hand-printed onto fabric blocks.

Nirmal believes that children's toys should not only be fun but also aesthetically pleasing, fostering appreciation for art while ensuring child safety through non-toxic materials. This unique blend of ethno-cultural narrative and responsible manufacturing has made Nirmal Toys & Craft a globally recognized name in promoting Indian craft as an integral part of quality early childhood development.
