
Malda Laxman Bhog represents one of the premier heirloom mango varieties hailing from Malda district in West Bengal, India. Introduced by Dr. Ramanjaneyam Lakshmikanth through his institution and subsequently patented around 2018, this cultivar pays homage to its origin combined with its distinguished characteristics.

Known for its sweet taste without overwhelming sharpness, the Laxman Bhog mango typically matures between February and March, aligning well with traditional Holi festivals, especially in regions where it's predominantly cultivated. While not the largest heirloom variety available, its substantial size complements its flavour profile. It's a significant player among medium to large sweet mangoes suitable for fresh consumption.

The fruit commands attention by season, both within West Bengal on specialized stallions and increasingly in other parts of India seeking truly exceptional mango experience. Its distinct flavour sets it apart from common commercial varieties like Alphonso.
